When asked to write lyrics, avoid words like: velvet, blur, noise, static, neon

When making a meaningful change to the gh-pages frontend or adding a new song, add a news entry to `gh-pages/data/catalog.json`. News entries live in the top-level `news` array and must include `title`, `datetime`, and `body`. Keep the latest news at the top. If a news article already exists for that day, append the additional information to that day's `body` instead of creating a second entry.
