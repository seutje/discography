[TITLE: Third-Wheel Constellation]
[PRODUCTION: Indietronica • atmospheric • warm analog synths • soft glitch percussion • intimate whispered vocals • dreamy reverb • subtle emotional tension • obscure, poetic storytelling about a principled Slovenian anarcho-communist friend who keeps returning after failed romances; themes of almost-romance, Molly night cuddling, rain-soaked memories, deep unspoken feeling without using the word “love”; tone: nostalgic, celestial, tender but restrained]
[LYRICS]
[verse 1]
Eight years of constellations circling you,
a Balkan spark with a compass carved from truth.
You say you’re not seeking anything tangled,
yet you orbit back each time your world unraveled.
And I’m here — the quiet shoreline,
catching pieces of your sky again.

[pre-chorus]
We once dissolved in midnight neon,
Molly heartbeats humming open,
your head on my chest like a secret language
neither of us dared to translate.

[chorus]
And you’re amazing —
a riot dressed in principles,
rolling in the rain with galaxies in your grin.
And I keep pretending the gravity’s accidental
while you third-wheel your way back into my systems
like it’s ritual,
like it’s written.

[verse 2]
Six months burning brilliant, almost incandescent,
close enough to feel the static kiss the air between us.
We never crossed the final threshold,
but that almost-night still echoes:
two blurred bodies,
warm, fearless, impossibly aligned.

[pre-chorus]
You said we were better as a gentle rebellion,
never naming what was trembling underneath.
And I nodded —
because I always do when it comes to you.

[chorus]
And you’re amazing —
storm-soaked prophet of impossible ideals,
grass-stained laughter dripping down my memory.
You return like patterned weather,
pretending it’s coincidence,
but I know the map of your footsteps by heart.

[bridge]
I keep my confessions folded small,
pressed between the beats you left behind.
Not allowed to speak the word for this feeling,
so I breathe it instead,
quiet as an uprising waiting for its signal.

[final chorus]
And you’re amazing —
the kind of person planets imitate.
Eight years and not a day you haven’t
flickered through my mind.
If there’s a reason you keep coming back,
I won’t ask —
I’ll just be here,
the place you land
when the world forgets how to hold you.

[outro]
Maybe someday we’ll name it.
Or maybe the unnamed
is what keeps us real.
