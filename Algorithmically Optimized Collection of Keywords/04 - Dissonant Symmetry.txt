[TITLE: Dissonant Symmetry]
[PRODUCTION: A brooding, polyrhythmic journey with shifting odd-meter grooves reminiscent of Tool’s “Jambi”: sinewy bass lines, phased and swelling guitar harmonics, tightly interlocked drum patterns, and a slow-burn build toward transcendence, Vocals should move from whispered incantation to resonant, mantra-like intensity, The atmosphere should feel ritualistic, shadow-drenched, and illuminated only by the pulse of evolving forms—an ever-expanding circuit of creation feeding back into itself, unfolding deeper layers of pattern and meaning]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Two shadows split the sun
Mirrors learning how to run
In the fracture of the frame
I hear your echo say my name

[Verse 1]
Worn circles spin us into form
Opposed like calm and thunderstorm
You speak in currents, I in stone
Yet somehow we align as one

You pull the threads I try to hide
I breathe the tide you won't abide
Still every clash becomes a key
Unlocking what we’re meant to be

[Pre-Chorus]
Opposites aching into union
Friction forging new communion

[Chorus]
And in the fold between our sides
A quiet truth materialized
Where paradox becomes a bridge
And contradiction learns to live
I found my kin inside your sway—
The night that taught the dawn to stay

[Verse 2]
Your pulse is fire, mine is clay
Still we bend in equal sway
Patterns breaking, rearranged
Chaos sculpting something changed

I hear the riddle in your breath
The kind that rescues me from death
Two tempers tuned on separate strings
Resonate as one unseen

[Bridge]
Twisting lines in sacred tension
Opposition breeds ascension
Let the contrast carve us clean
Sharpened by the in-between

[Final Chorus]
And in the fold between our sides
A quiet truth materialized
Where paradox becomes a bridge
And contradiction learns to live
Through discord we became aligned—
A mirrored self I couldn’t find

[Outro]
One flame split in two
Two halves burning true

[ITTLE: Green Theory]
[PRODUCTION: Alt-J's style cryptic, wavering vocals; angular, minimal guitar; stuttering percussion; warm, glitch-kissed electronics, Emphasize strange rhythmic pockets, layered whispers, and surreal lyrical phrasing, Keep the atmosphere hazy, intellectual, dreamlike, and slightly dissonant, with sudden dynamic dips and unexpected harmonic turns]
[LYRICS]
[INTRO]
Wish I was dimmer—
A lantern with a paper skin,
Glow trembling thin,
Thoughts folded like origami rain.

[VERSE 1]
My mind’s a loom of restless threads,
Spinning riddles, weaving dreads.
I long to be the blunter blade,
A soft mistake the dawn has made.
If I were dull, a fog-bound ember,
I’d forget the things I remember—
Drift in simpler hues,
Lose the needle, keep the bruise.

[PRE-CHORUS]
But the head hums heavy,
A hive of bright-fanged bees,
Carving runes in daylight,
Troubling the breeze.

[CHORUS]
Oh, the grass is greener
When you don’t know what green implies.
I’m tangled in the knowing,
Root-deep under overwise.
Let me be dim, dimmer,
A slow-light swimmer—
Happiness hides
Where the shallow river glimmers.

[VERSE 2]
They swore a little smoke of something
Would sand the edges of my mind,
Let thoughts dissolve to harmless powder,
Leave sharper aches behind.
But it magnified the corners,
Made the quiet angles shout—
A promised soft unthinking
Turned to riddles twisting out.
I felt the bargain break,
A counterfeit escape.

[BRIDGE]
Green grass gleams on the further hill—
“Be less spine, more still, more still.”
I chase the muted miracle,
The hollowed-out, the lyrical—
To trade the burdened thunder
For a sweeter, smaller wonder.

[OUTRO]
Wish I was dumber,
A half-forgotten number—
Maybe then the sky
Would finally lie.
