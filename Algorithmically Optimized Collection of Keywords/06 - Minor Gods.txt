[TITLE: Minor Gods]
[PRODUCTION: A brooding, polyrhythmic journey with shifting odd-meter grooves reminiscent of Tool’s “Jambi”: sinewy bass lines, phased and swelling guitar harmonics, tightly interlocked drum patterns, and a slow-burn build toward transcendence, Vocals should move from whispered incantation to resonant, mantra-like intensity, The atmosphere should feel ritualistic, shadow-drenched, and illuminated only by the pulse of evolving forms—an ever-expanding circuit of creation feeding back into itself, unfolding deeper layers of pattern and meaning]
[LYRICS]
[verse 1]
Knotted wires in my pocket, yet the constellations speak my name.
I parse the hidden geometry, but choke on folding paper cranes.
A titan in the labyrinth; a child before an open door—
I lift the world with quiet fingers, can’t turn the key I’ve turned before.

[pre-chorus]
Little motions, tiny burdens,
rise like mountains I can’t climb.
But give me chaos, give me storms—
I’ll rearrange the shape of time.

[chorus]
Oh, the simple breaks me open.
Oh, the vast becomes my home.
I solve the riddle of the cosmos
but forget to pick up the phone.
Every minor spark consumes me,
yet the stars obey my hand.
Tell me why the smallest footsteps
are the hardest ones to stand.

[verse 2]
My mind a sacred engine humming, slicing shadows into form.
Yet dishes glare like prophets judging every half-remembered norm.
I summon maps of revelation, charting futures from a sigh—
but leave the curtains closed for days, unsure of how or why.

[pre-chorus]
One more moment, one more fracture,
splits the will I thought was mine.
Still I wander, still I fathom,
still I hold the grand design.

[chorus]
Oh, the simple breaks me open.
Oh, the vast becomes my home.
I solve the riddle of the cosmos
but forget to pick up the phone.
Every minor spark consumes me,
yet the stars obey my hand.
Tell me why the smallest footsteps
are the hardest ones to stand.

[bridge]
Let the thunder teach the language
that my heartbeat can’t pronounce.
Let intention find its anchor;
let inertia be renounced.
In the mirror stands a giant
trapped by threads around his wrists—
the world awaits his motion,
but the moment never shifts.

[outro]
I could bend the sky to reason,
draw the contours of the dawn…
Yet the weight of one small action
keeps me still until it’s gone.
