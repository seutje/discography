[TITLE: Single-Serve Society]
[PRODUCTION: A brooding, polyrhythmic journey with shifting odd-meter grooves reminiscent of Tool’s “Jambi”: sinewy bass lines, phased and swelling guitar harmonics, tightly interlocked drum patterns, and a slow-burn build toward transcendence, Vocals should move from whispered incantation to resonant, mantra-like intensity, The atmosphere should feel ritualistic, shadow-drenched, and illuminated only by the pulse of evolving forms—an ever-expanding circuit of creation feeding back into itself, unfolding deeper layers of pattern and meaning, male vocals]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
A cursor blinks like a lighthouse
For ships that never left the room
I render thoughts in real time
Just to watch them lose their bloom

[Verse 1]
I built a song inside an evening
Folded code into a rhyme
Pressed export on a feeling
Stamped created over time
There’s no crowd behind the curtain
Just a mirror dressed as you
Every click a small assertion
That I’m real if I’m consumed

[Pre-Chorus]
Is this voice a signal
Or a nervous habit dressed as art
If no one hears the waveform
Does it still fracture my heart?

[Chorus]
Am I making this for someone
Or am I feeding the machine?
Every thing is single-serve now
Disappear before it’s seen
I pour myself into the render
Watch it vanish when it’s done
Tell me—what’s the point of making
If I’m both the crowd and the one?

[Verse 2]
Apps like paper cups of meaning
Songs that melt before they land
Infinite beginnings ending
In the palm of my own hand
We automate the spark itself
Until it barely makes a sound
An ocean made of throwaway wells
Too shallow to let echoes drown

[Bridge]
I asked the future what it wanted
It said: faster, less, again
So I split my doubt in versions
And I cut the build by ten
Is this waste a form of prayer
Or just noise I call belief?
Am I chasing something out there
Or just begging for relief? 

[Break]
Metrics bloom and then decay
Like flowers grown in cached delay
If meaning’s cheap and always near
Why does it cost me every year?

[Chorus]
Am I making this for someone
Or rehearsing being alive?
Every artifact disposable
Every proof a soft alibi
I press publish like a reflex
Like a flare into the sun
Tell me—what’s the point of making
If I’m the audience of one?

[Outro]
When the server lights go silent
And the copies all expire
Will there be a trace of wanting
In the ash of my desire?
If creation’s just a habit
And I can’t stop when I’m done
I’ll keep singing to the void
Until it sings back as someone
