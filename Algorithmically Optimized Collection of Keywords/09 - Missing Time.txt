[TITLE: Missing Time]
[PRODUCTION: A brooding, polyrhythmic Indietronica journey with shifting odd-meter grooves reminiscent of Tool’s “Jambi”: sinewy bass lines, phased and swelling guitar harmonics, tightly interlocked drum patterns, and a slow-burn build toward transcendence, Vocals should move from whispered incantation to resonant, mantra-like intensity, The atmosphere should feel ritualistic, shadow-drenched, and illuminated only by the pulse of evolving forms—an ever-expanding circuit of creation feeding back into itself, unfolding deeper layers of pattern and meaning]
