[TITLE: Borrowed Light]
[PRODUCTION: A brooding, polyrhythmic Indietronica journey with shifting odd-meter grooves reminiscent of Tool’s “Jambi”: sinewy bass lines, phased and swelling guitar harmonics, tightly interlocked drum patterns, and a slow-burn build toward transcendence, Vocals should move from whispered incantation to resonant, mantra-like intensity, The atmosphere should feel ritualistic, shadow-drenched, and illuminated only by the pulse of evolving forms—an ever-expanding circuit of creation feeding back into itself, unfolding deeper layers of pattern and meaning]
[LYRICS]
[verse]
I carve a lantern out of quiet,
hold it up to show the land—
but every flame I name goes silent
when it’s carried by my hand.
Then someone lifts the same dim shimmer,
calls it new, calls it divine,
and the crowd bows like believers
as if I never drew the line.

[pre-chorus]
Maybe my voice is made of vapor,
rising first but seen the last.
Maybe truth wears different faces
depending who unveils the glass.

[chorus]
So let them cheer the echo
and forget the origin.
Let them claim my constellations
as if they were born in their skin.
I’ll stand inside the hush between
the thought and the acclaim—
where everything I offer up
returns without my name.

[verse]
I speak a seed into the open,
they salt the ground beneath my feet.
But when another plants its twin there,
suddenly the soil 's all sweet.
And I am told to join the praising,
to smile wide and play along—
as though the bloom was not my shaping,
as though I never wrote this song.

[bridge]
Is it envy, is it shadow,
or the desire to be seen?
To spark the fire that warms the world
but never feel its heat on me.
Still I whisper to the void,
where unclaimed brilliance sleeps—
for sometimes truth grows brightest
in the dark where no one peeps.

[chorus]
So let them cheer the echo
and forget the origin.
Let them crown the imitation
as the place where light begins.
I’ll stay within the quiet space
that lives behind the fame—
where everything I’ve ever made
shines best without my name.

[outro]
One day the echo might remember
the shape of its first sound.
Until then, I walk the border
of the lost and the profound.
