[TITLE: Velvet Cake]
[PRODUCTION: Indietronica with warm analog synths, soft pulsating arpeggios, and intimate vocals, A contemplative, cinematic mood—blending glitchy percussion, airy pads, and a slowly building emotional arc, The sound should feel profound, reverent, and quietly rebellious, Subtle distortion and shimmering reverb to evoke nostalgia and revelation, Atmosphere first, groove second, Comparable to artists like Sylvan Esso, Son Lux, and James Blake, but more tender, Serious, poetic tone with coded metaphors about forbidden sweetness and self-permission]
[LYRICS]
[verse 1]

They said velvet was a softness meant for someone else,
A crimson cloud reserved for quieter rooms.
They handed me apple—all rind and grit,
Told me “This is what a real one consumes.”
So I carved my days in orchard lines,
Hiding questions in the pulp I chewed,
Never knowing there was velvet at the edge of the sky,
Waiting for the hour I’d break their rules.

[pre-chorus]

But whispers grow when hunger stays,
And silence never feeds a soul.
A single taste can split a life—
Make a fractured self feel whole.

[chorus]

Velvet cake—how long you waited in the wings,
A truth disguised as sweetness under lock and key.
They said your softness wasn’t meant for me,
But I bit through the night and felt the room reshape.
Velvet cake—what else have they hidden away?

[verse 2]

I learned to smile through the orchard’s ache,
Pretending tartness was my home.
But velvet called in crimson waves,
A secret language in its gentle tone.
And when I finally let it touch my tongue,
The world rewrote itself in red—
Every gate they built around desire
Crumbling like sugar beneath my breath.

[pre-chorus]

Who decides what sweetness means,
Or which doors we’re meant to close?
I found a universe in softness,
Where the truth in me finally rose.

[chorus]

Velvet cake—how long you waited in the wings,
A truth disguised as sweetness under lock and key.
They said your softness wasn’t meant for me,
But I bit through the night and felt the room reshape.
Velvet cake—what else have they hidden away?

[bridge]

And now I stand with red-stained hands,
Learning pleasure isn’t shame.
A quiet rebellion in every crumb—
A vow to taste without their blame.
If velvet is a language,
Then let my body finally speak.
If sweetness is a freedom,
Let it find me when I’m weak.

[final chorus]

Velvet cake—no longer waiting in the wings,
You taught me that forbidden things
Are often where the spirit wakes.
I was told that apples made a man—
But velvet made me real.
Velvet cake—
The secret they could never take.
