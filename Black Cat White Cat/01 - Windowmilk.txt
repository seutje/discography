[TITLE: Windowmilk]
[GENRE: Warm Indietronica / Nocturnal Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Watchful, soft-edged, wary, quietly amused, intimate, airy]
[TEMPO: 90 BPM]
[KEY: F major with D minor verse color]
[VOCALS: Close low-register verses, gentle melodic lift in choruses, white cat voice clear and dry, thin doubles on repeated window images, final refrain warmer but still guarded]
[PRODUCTION: Soft electronic pulse like paws crossing floorboards, rounded sub bass like a purr behind the wall, sparse piano notes catching daylight, tiny bell and claw-click percussion kept low, room tone and radiator hum, reverb opening only around chorus tails]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Milk in the glass... sun on the sill...
Small bright room holding still
Clean white paws... dust in the beam...
I let them look, but not too deep

[Verse 1]
Morning sets a plate of light
Across the chair I claimed last night
The window makes a gentle frame
And teaches strangers half my name

They see the fur, the careful face
The tidy arch, the practiced grace
They do not see the little claw
Folded under every law

I warm my ribs against the pane
Watch every yard rehearse its lane
The hedge, the gate, the neighbor's shoes
The outside moving as it chooses

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't trap me like a chain
It only keeps the weather plain
A promise clear enough to touch
That still refuses me too much

[Chorus]
Windowmilk, pour it slow
Make me bright enough to know
Who is watching, who believes
Softness means I never leave

Windowmilk, pale and kind
Leave one shadow tucked behind
If they love the shape they see
Let them miss a part of me

[Verse 2]
There is a bowl beneath the clock
A cushion with my old paw mark
A hand that calls me good and sweet
As if I never dream of streets

I rub my cheek along the door
Not asking much, then asking more
The latch clicks once inside its throat
And all my courage changes coat

Across the fence a black tail slips
Like ink that learned to keep its hips
Low to the earth, away from praise
Free in ways that do not blaze

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't hurt to be adored
It only polishes the door
Til even hunger, even pride
Learns how to sit and stay inside

[Chorus]
Windowmilk, pour it slow
Make me bright enough to know
Who is watching, who believes
Softness means I never leave

Windowmilk, pale and kind
Leave one shadow tucked behind
If they love the shape they see
Let them miss a part of me

[Bridge]
I am not clean because I shine
I am not tame because I dine
I am the pawprint on the sheet
And dust beneath the polished seat

If I look holy in the glass
Let that mistaken mercy pass
I have my weather, I have my teeth
Folded where the warm light sleeps

[Break]
(whispered)
See me...
Not all of me...
Enough...
Not all...

[Outro]
Milk in the glass... sun on the sill...
Small bright room holding still
Clean white paws... dust in the beam...
I let them look, and keep the seam
