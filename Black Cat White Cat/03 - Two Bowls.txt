[TITLE: Two Bowls]
[GENRE: Warm Indietronica / Wry Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Guarded, funny-sad, domestic, tense but not bleak, quietly tender]
[TEMPO: 92 BPM]
[KEY: A minor shifting toward C major]
[VOCALS: Alternating white cat and black cat phrases, close dry verses, small harmony replies in choruses, restrained duet in final chorus]
[PRODUCTION: Soft beat built from spoon taps and floorboard clicks, rounded bass purr, small piano motif repeating with variations, bell collar accents on offbeats, narrow room reverb widening as trust appears]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Two bowls... one room...
Clock on the wall counting spoon
White paw... black tail...
Peace held thin as a fingernail

[Verse 1]
She takes the left side by the chair
As if the chair appointed her
He takes the right and looks away
As if not looking proves he'll stay

The kitchen keeps a yellow square
The kind of light that makes a dare
One bowl sings with ceramic pride
One bowl waits on the darker side

Nobody growls, and nobody bends
The room pretends that this is sense
But every whisker keeps a list
Of every move the other risks

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't sound like need at first
It sounds like water, sounds like thirst
It sounds like bowls placed far apart
To spare the furniture a heart

[Chorus]
Both: Two bowls, one room
Both: Enough, but not assumed
We eat like treaties, sip like spies
With guarded mouths and open eyes

Both: Two bowls, one room
Both: No song, but not a wound
If hunger learns to share a floor
What else can enter through the door?

[Verse 2]
She leaves three bites to prove control
He leaves none and hates the bowl
She washes paws with courtly care
He checks the hall for hidden snares

The hand that feeds them says their names
Like names are blankets, names are claims
The white cat lifts her chin and glows
The black cat hears the window close

Still, when the thunder climbs the stair
They find the rug and both sit there
Not touching yet, but near enough
To let the floor remember trust

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't solve the old design
It only moves the warning line
Til what was mine and what was yours
Can stand unarmed between two doors

[Chorus]
Both: Two bowls, one room
Both: Enough, but not assumed
We eat like treaties, sip like spies
With guarded mouths and open eyes

Both: Two bowls, one room
Both: No song, but not a wound

[Bridge]
I do not want your half of food
I want the right to not be viewed
As thief, as saint, as guest, as test
As proof that kindness did its best

I do not want your sunny place
I want a corner with my face
Turned toward the room, away from flight
And no one calling that polite

[Break]
Left bowl...
Right bowl...
Same rain...
Same room...

[Outro]
Two bowls... one room...
Clock on the wall counting spoon
White paw... black tail...
