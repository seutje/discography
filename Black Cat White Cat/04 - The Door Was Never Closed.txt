[TITLE: The Door Was Never Closed]
[GENRE: Melancholic-Warm Indietronica / Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Ambiguous, alert, wind-touched, unsettled but curious]
[TEMPO: 88 BPM]
[KEY: E minor with G major lifts]
[VOCALS: Close verses split between inner and outer voices, chorus more melodic, breath audible before door images, final refrain open but unresolved]
[PRODUCTION: Low pulse with hinge creaks as percussion, warm bass drone, sparse guitar harmonics, piano echoes, outside air widening the stereo field, arrangement thins whenever the doorway appears]
[LYRICS]

Something open without a name

[Verse 1]
The door was never closed all night
It leaned there drinking porch light
A narrow mouth, a wooden doubt
Letting weather think about

The white cat saw the cushion fade
The exact warm dent her body made
The black cat saw the alley bend
Like a road pretending friend

Neither crossed and neither slept
Both kept count of what they kept
One had rooms that knew her knees
One had roofs and elder trees

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't tell you leave or stay
It only takes the lock away
And every choice you thought was clear
Starts growing whiskers in your ear

[Chorus]
The door was never closed
Not freedom, not a home
Just a line the evening drew
Between the self and what it knew

The door was never closed
No answer in the cold
If I step out or step back in
Which part of me begins again?

[Verse 2]
The black cat placed one paw inside
And hated how the floor replied
Too warm, too known, too near a hand
Too much like being asked to land

The white cat placed one paw on stone
And felt the garden call her own
Too wide, too damp, too full of wings
Too honest with the hidden things

A moth kept tapping at the bulb
A small bright wish, a foolish skull
The bell around the white throat stirred
The black cat froze at that small word
