[TITLE: Bell Collar Theology]
[GENRE: Nocturnal Indietronica / Chamber Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Humiliated, elegant, irritated, vulnerable, inwardly funny]
[TEMPO: 86 BPM]
[KEY: B minor]
[VOCALS: White cat lead with black cat shadow doubles, low intimate verses, chorus with tight harmonies on bell phrases, spoken bridge nearly dry]
[PRODUCTION: Bell collar sample used as a measured motif, muted kick, bowed bass pad, sparse piano, tiny metal percussion, reverb pulled short in verses and widened during chorus answers]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Ring by ring... throat by throat...
Every step sends up a note
Pretty chain... little chime...
Someone else's sense of time

[Verse 1]
They put a planet at my neck
A silver rule, a bright respect
Said it made me sweet to hear
As if I asked to enter clear

Now every chair knows when I pass
Every cupboard, every glass
The hallway names my appetite
Before I earn the shape of night

The black cat watches from below
With paws that speak in almost no
He moves through rooms like held-in rain
I arrive with a little chain

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't hurt, the sound itself
It only moves me from myself
A gentle law with polished face
That makes a room announce my place

[Chorus]
Bell around my throat
Tiny sermon, borrowed note
If I am loved, why must I ring?
Why must care become a string?

Bell around my throat
Cute enough to keep me known
I was quiet once, I swear
Before they made me public air

[Verse 2]
The hand that fastened it was kind
No villain hiding in the bind
Just fear dressed up as tender sense
Just love installing evidence

The birds outside might bless the sound
The mice might live another round
The house might sleep with fewer claws
Because my body keeps its laws

Still something in me lowers flat
When metal speaks before the cat
I want one step that does not say
Forgive me, I am on my way

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't make the hunter clean
It only tells on what I mean
Til even mercy, even grace
Can feel like bells around the face

[Bridge]
The black cat says, I know the cost
Of going silent, going lost
The white cat says, I know the cost
Of being found before you're crossed

So let the bell be not a joke
Nor holy chain, nor owner's hope
Let it be what both can hear
A warning made of care and fear
