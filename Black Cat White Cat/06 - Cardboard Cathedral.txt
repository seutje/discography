[TITLE: Cardboard Cathedral]
[GENRE: Warm Indietronica / Ambient Folk Electronic]
[MOOD: Tender, absurd, makeshift, sheltered, lightly comic, quietly sacred]
[TEMPO: 91 BPM]
[KEY: C major with A minor turns]
[VOCALS: Warm close lead, small group doubles in choruses, spoken side phrases tucked low, final chorus communal and gentle]
[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked guitar blended with soft electronic pulse, brushed percussion from box taps, upright bass, small piano, low organ pad, cardboard scrape textures, room reverb like a small safe square]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Box on the floor... tape at the seam...
Kingdom folded from a shipping dream
Four low walls... roof half-torn...
Sanctuary cheaply born

[Verse 1]
The package came with someone else's name
Brown walls, a dent, a barcode frame
The hand removed the useful thing
And left the hollow offering

The white cat entered like a queen
The black cat checked behind the screen
Both found corners, both found proof
That shelter does not need a roof

The room outside kept being large
With clocks and chairs and human charge
But in the box the world reduced
To whisker width and truce

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't have to last to hold
It doesn't have to shine like gold
Some holy places crease and bend
And still know where the ribs begin

[Chorus]
Cardboard cathedral
Small square miracle
We make a chapel of the cheap
And call it ours enough to sleep

Cardboard cathedral
Ridiculous and beautiful
If all we get is borrowed walls
Let borrowed walls receive us all

[Verse 2]
The bell lay quiet in the dust
The bowls forgot their border trust
The door stayed open, rain stayed out
The black cat almost stretched his mouth

Not quite a smile, but near enough
To change the temperature of stuff
The white cat tucked her tail beside
The part of him that used to hide

No sermon came, no choir learned
Only the heater clicked and turned
Only two bodies, box and floor
Making enough from what was poor

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't fix the alley years
It doesn't make the bright room clear
But even pride can curl down small
When safety has a cardboard wall

[Chorus]
Cardboard cathedral
Small square miracle
We make a chapel of the cheap
And call it ours enough to sleep

Cardboard cathedral
Ridiculous and beautiful
If all we get is borrowed walls
Let borrowed walls receive us all

[Bridge]
When it caves in, and it will cave
When tape lets go of what it gave
We will not call the shelter false
Because it could not stop all loss

Some rooms are made for crossing through
Some names are only briefly true
Still, in the crease where dust collects
We found a place the day respects

[Break]
(soft group voices)
Fold by fold...
Breath by breath...
Room enough for what is left...

[Outro]
Box on the floor... tape at the seam...
Kingdom folded from a shipping dream
Four low walls... morning near...
Temporary can be sincere
