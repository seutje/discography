[TITLE: Snake Can Letters]
[GENRE: Wry Indietronica / Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Affectionate, spring-loaded, embarrassed, playful but sincere, nocturnal]
[TEMPO: 96 BPM]
[KEY: G minor lifting to Bb major]
[VOCALS: Conversational verses, call-and-response duet, chorus catchy but restrained, whispered message fragments in the break, final chorus wider and warmer]
[PRODUCTION: Coiled synth bass, soft clap and can-click percussion, toy-spring accents used musically, muted guitar plucks, small piano motif returning in altered form, dry vocal close-ups with wider chorus replies]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Lid in the hand... smile held back...
Something coiled in a paper stack
Pop goes the heart... out come three...
Harmless things behaving dramatically

[Verse 1]
You sent a sentence, then a room
Then twelve more rooms inside it bloomed
A hallway, staircase, attic door
Then one small joke and seven more

I read it under kitchen light
While both cats argued with the night
The black one judged the folded page
The white one sat upon the stage

Every thought came springing out
Painted tongue and open mouth
Not venom, no, not even close
Just too much care in Sunday clothes

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't bite because it leaps
It only startles what it keeps
Some messages were never planned
For fitting neatly in the can

[Chorus]
Snake can letters, flying wide
Paper hearts with spring inside
Nothing harmful, nothing grand
Just too much heart for the size of the can

Snake can letters, laugh and duck
Love arrives with awkward luck
If it spills across the floor
Maybe that is what floors are for

[Verse 2]
The black cat sniffed the ribbon twice
As if affection had a price
The white cat batted at the coil
And blessed the mess with royal toil

We tried to make the short reply
The sensible one, clean and dry
But every little thought grew legs
And knocked the cup beside the eggs

So here it comes, the overfull
The earnest little carnival
The lid that cannot keep the spring
From making weather out of string

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't mean the words are weak
Because they tumble when they speak
Some truths are clumsy when they land
And still know exactly where to stand

[Chorus]
Snake can letters, flying wide
Paper hearts with spring inside
Nothing harmful, nothing grand
Just too much heart for the size of the can

Snake can letters, laugh and duck
Love arrives with awkward luck
If it spills across the floor
Maybe that is what floors are for

[Bridge]
The white cat says, be elegant
The black cat says, be less intent
The box says, fold it, make it fit
The spring says, absolutely not

So let the message lose its shape
Let every coil make its escape
If care looks foolish when it flies
At least it does not wear disguise

[Break]
(whispered)
Too much...
Too fast...
Still kind...
Still here...

[Final Chorus]
Snake can letters, flying wide
Paper hearts with spring inside
Nothing harmful, nothing planned
Just too much heart for the size of the can

Snake can letters, laugh and duck
Love arrives with awkward luck
If it spills across the floor
We can make a room of more

[Outro]
Lid in the hand... smile held back...
Something coiled in a paper stack
Pop goes the heart... out come three...
