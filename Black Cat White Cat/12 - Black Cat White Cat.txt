[TITLE: Black Cat White Cat]
[GENRE: Warm Melancholic Indietronica / Harmonic Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Mythic, intimate, dual-voiced, resolved without certainty, gently luminous]
[TEMPO: 90 BPM]
[KEY: F minor evolving toward Ab major]
[VOCALS: True duet between white cat and black cat voices, close alternating verses, braided harmonies in choruses, call-and-response bridge, final chorus layered but not triumphant]
[PRODUCTION: White cat palette of piano, glassy keys, and air merged with black cat palette of warm bass, muffled drums, and alley reverb, bell motif slowed into harmony, purr-like sub drone, transformed returns of window, bowl, door, box, moth, and snake-can motifs]
