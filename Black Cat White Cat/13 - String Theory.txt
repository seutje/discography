[TITLE: String Theory]
[GENRE: Cosmic Theatrical Indietronica / Dramatic Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Celestial, playful, theatrical, intimate, orbiting, wonder-lit without becoming cheerful]
[TEMPO: 96 BPM]
[KEY: E minor shifting toward G major]
[VOCALS: Single intimate lead voice, theatrical but grounded verses, lifted chorus with doubled phrases and soft harmony answers, spoken low phrases in the bridge, final chorus wide and cosmic but still close-mic]
[PRODUCTION: Pulsing synth bass like gravity under floorboards, plucked string motif doubling as toy string and cosmic vibration, piano glints like moonlight on tile, low choral pads, tiny bell accents, floorboard percussion, arcing delay throws like comet trails, final third opens into a larger starfield without losing the apartment-room scale]
