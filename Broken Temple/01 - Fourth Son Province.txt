[TITLE: Fourth Son Province]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Inherited smallness -> pressure-mapped belonging -> unresolved self-recognition]

[TEMPO: 82 BPM]

[KEY: D minor with a late F major lift that never fully resolves]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead with a low, careful delivery; breath-heavy phrasing in verses; small harmonies orbit the hook unevenly; whispered family-map fragments tucked low in the mix; final refrain opens but remains fragile]

[PRODUCTION: Deep sub bass moving like a border river under the floor, sparse kick pulses, off-grid rim clicks like kitchen chairs, low organ pads, filtered guitar harmonics, dub delay tightening around "fourth" and "province", stereo field narrow in verses and wider when the body-country is named, final section centered and warm but suspended]

I am the fourth son province
