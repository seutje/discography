[TITLE: Fourth Son Province]

[GENRE: Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Inherited smallness -> pressure-mapped belonging -> unresolved self-recognition]

[TEMPO: 82 BPM]

[KEY: D minor with a late F major lift that never fully resolves]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead; breathy delivery; motif: 'rib by rib' as rhythmic anchor; whispered family-map fragments in low mix; final refrain is fragile but open.]

[PRODUCTION: Deep sub bass (border river movement); sparse kick; off-grid rim clicks. Texture shift: Verse 1 (dry/narrow) -> Hook (wide/sub bloom) -> Bridge (deconstructed/glitchy) -> Final Hook (centered/warm). Dub delay on 'fourth' and 'province'. High-pass filter sweep during bridge.]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - low room tone | distant chair creak | sub pulse]

Four cups out
one set low
smallest chair
beside the stove

Rib by rib
a border starts

I was born
outside the chart

[Verse 1 - minimal pulse | dry close vocal]

Youngest of four
in a crowded state
I learned the laws
by standing late

Three older systems
crossed the room
weather above me
claiming noon

The hallway held
its parliament
doors made votes
I could not prevent

Every joke became
a checkpoint name
every shout redrew
the same terrain

I kept a province
under breath
useful, quiet
half-addressed

[Pre-Hook - delays pull inward]

No crown
no claim

just pulse
under frame

No flag
no throne

just a fourth cup
left alone

[Hook - sub blooms | harmonies enter late]

I am the fourth son province
small in the field
a country made of borrowed ribs
and borders I could feel

Mark me late
mark me less
last signal in the line
still the smallest body-country
learns its shape by spine

[Verse 2 - wider pads | slow dub echoes]

Mother poured
the evening dark
father folded
hands like charts

Smoke held court
above the plates
while every silence
changed the weight

I watched the room
become a climate
watched the climate
choose a side

What they called
a normal shelter
felt like living
near the tide

If I laughed
I came too fast
if I cried
I came too last

So I made
a private anthem
from the places
I could not pass

[Pre-Hook - low octave shadow]

Stay near
stay small

be counted
not called

Hold still
hold breath

inherit
what is left

[Hook - fuller low end | unstable warmth]

I am the fourth son province
small in the field
a country made of borrowed ribs
and borders I could feel

Mark me late
mark me less
last signal in the line
still the smallest body-country
keeps a capital inside

[Bridge - beat thins | texture shift: glitchy/deconstructed]

If the body
is a country

mine began
divided

rib by rib
line by line

kitchen smoke
in the north
fear along
the siding

Even a narrow district
keeps a road beneath the rain

Even the late note
can return
and change
the phrase

[Final Hook - centered mix | fragile lift]

I am the fourth son province
not erased, not restored
a small republic breathing
with his hand against the door

Mark me late
let me answer
after others ring
there is room inside the measure
for the last-born note to sing

[Outro - low-pass fade | single guitar harmonic]

Four cups out
one held back

smallest country
still alive

rib by rib
line by line

(whispered: fourth... province...)

[End]
