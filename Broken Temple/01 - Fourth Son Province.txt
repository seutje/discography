[TITLE: Fourth Son Province]

[GENRE: Slowcore Indie / Ambient Folk Electronic / Deep Dub Bass]

[MOOD: Small, inherited, watchful -> tender pressure -> unresolved belonging]

[TEMPO: 82 BPM]

[KEY: D minor, final refrain turns toward F major without fully landing]

[VOCALS: Close male vocal, low and careful in verses, fragile lift in choruses, small group harmony only on the final refrain, breath left audible before family images]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked guitar with one late fourth note, slow sub bass moving like an old border river, brushed snare, low organ pad, faint kitchen chair creaks, dry verses widening only when the chorus names the body-country]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Four cups out... one set low...
Smallest chair beside the stove
Rib by rib... border drawn...
I was born where maps went wrong

[Verse 1]
Youngest of four in a crowded republic
I learned the law by listening down
Three older weather systems over me
heavy boots in a narrow town

The hallway had its own parliament
doors held votes I never won
Every shout became a border post
Every joke became a loaded gun

My body kept a little province
under blankets, under breath
I paid my tax in staying useful
I kept my questions under thread

[Pre-Chorus]
No crown, no flag
just a pulse beneath the floor
No throne, no claim
just the fourth cup near the door

[Chorus]
I am the fourth son province
small on the family chart
A country made of borrowed ribs
and a capital under the heart

Mark me late, mark me lesser
mark me last in the line
Still the smallest part of a country
learns the border by spine

[Verse 2]
Mother poured the evening darker
Father folded up his hands
Smoke held court above the table
while the plates made quiet plans

I watched the room become a climate
watched the climate choose a side
What they called a normal childhood
felt like shelter built at tide

If I laughed, I laughed too quickly
If I cried, I took too long
So I made a private anthem
from the places I was wrong

[Pre-Chorus 2]
No clean decree
just a rule beneath the skin
Stay close enough
to be counted in

[Bridge]
If the body is a country
then mine began divided
Kitchen smoke in the north
and fear along the siding

But even a narrow district
keeps a road beneath the rain
Even the late note in the figure
can return and change the phrase

[Final Chorus]
I am the fourth son province
not erased, not restored
A small republic breathing
with its hand against the door

Mark me late, let me answer
after all the others ring
There is room inside the measure
for the last-born note to sing

[Outro]
Four cups out... one held back...
Still I learned the table track
Rib by rib... line by line...
Smallest country, still alive
