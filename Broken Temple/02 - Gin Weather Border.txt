[TITLE: Gin Weather Border]
[GENRE: Dark Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Chamber Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Volatile vigilance -> adult naming -> bruised compassion without absolution]

[TEMPO: 84 BPM]

[KEY: E minor with a suspended G major lift in the final hook]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead, almost spoken in verses; doubled older-self answers in the hook; low octave shadow appears on "pressure" and "border"; no theatrical anger, only controlled breath and careful distance]

[PRODUCTION: Sub-heavy dub bass in long weather-front swells, felt piano fragments, bottle-tap percussion used sparsely, bowed pads rising like pressure changes, delay tails pulling inward after mother images, narrow verses widening only when the floodplain is named]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - filtered rain taps | low sub pressure]

storm in there

[Verse 1 - tight beat | close vocal]

Mother was a country
of sudden rain
gin in the lowlands
thunder at the sink

I learned the clouds

by the way her hand
forgot to think

to an empty room

reassigned their side

I kept my passport
under my tongue

inside

ordinary land

[Hook - sub blooms | answered harmony]

Gin weather on the border
rain inside the bone

one dry province of my own

Mother, I can name it now
and naming is not blame
some countries flood their villages
and still keep someone's name

the weather change
