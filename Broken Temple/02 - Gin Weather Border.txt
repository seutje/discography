[TITLE: Gin Weather Border]

[GENRE: Dark Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Chamber Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Volatile vigilance -> adult naming -> bruised compassion without absolution]

[TEMPO: 84 BPM]

[KEY: E minor (G major lift in final hook)]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead; whispered verses; doubled 'older-self' harmonies on hooks; low octave shadow on 'pressure' and 'border'; controlled breath.]

[PRODUCTION: Sub-heavy dub bass swells, felt piano fragments, sparse bottle-tap percussion. Dynamic spatial shifts: Verse (narrow/dry) -> Pre-Hook (widening delay) -> Hook (expansive sub-bloom). High-end polished, low-end grounded.]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - filtered rain taps | low sub pressure]

Glass on wood
weather coming

[Verse 1 - tight dry mix | intimate vocal]

Mother was a country
of sudden rain
gin in the lowlands
thunder at the sink

I learned the clouds
by the cupboard hinge
by the way her hand
forgot to think

Some nights she sang
to an empty room
some nights the walls
reassigned their side

I kept my passport
under my tongue
and crossed the border
inside

The ashtray filled
like winter law
two cigarettes
in shared command

I breathed the empire
second-hand
and called it
ordinary land

[Pre-Hook - delay widens | spatial expansion]

Children read
the barometer

before
the date

They feel pressure
entering

and make themselves
small weight

[Hook - sub bloom | doubled harmony]

Gin weather on the border
rain inside the bone
I was trying to keep
one dry province of my own

Mother, I can name it now
and naming is not blame
some countries flood their villages
and still keep someone's name

[Verse 2 - dub echoes | spacious texture]

Morning came
in careful parts
cereal, schoolbag
practiced face

Nothing broken
in the report
nothing marked
in the right place

But the body keeps
its ledger
not in words
but in the jaw

Every flinch
becomes a treaty
signed before
you read the law

I loved her
from the doorway
where loving had
a safer range

A child can miss
the person there
and still fear
the weather change

[Pre-Hook - low-end swell | heavy texture]

No verdict
no shrine

just floodplain
and higher line

No pardon
no crown

just the map
of what went down

[Hook - fuller, polished mix]

Gin weather on the border
rain inside the bone
I was trying to keep
one dry province of my own

Mother, I can name it now
and naming is not blame
some countries flood their villages
and still keep someone's name

[Bridge - percussion drops | layered textures]

If I smoke
is it inheritance
or a match
I chose to strike

If I drink
is it geography
or a road
I walk at night

I carry her
as weather

not sentence
not crown

just the lowland
in my body

where the first rain
settled down

[Final Hook - warm pads | G major lift]

Gin weather on the border
still the river knows my name
I can build above the water
without denying where it came

Mother, I can name it now
the name does not absolve
but the child beneath the stairwell
is a country that evolved

[Outro - distant glass tap | fading sub]

Glass on wood
morning after

clouds undone
but never gone

Small dry place
hand on border

I am here
I carried on
