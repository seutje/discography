[TITLE: Gin Weather Border]

[GENRE: Melancholic Indie / Dub Electronica / Chamber Slowcore]

[MOOD: Volatile, childlike vigilance -> adult naming -> bruised compassion]

[TEMPO: 84 BPM]

[KEY: E minor with a suspended G major lift in the final chorus]

[VOCALS: Intimate male lead, restrained and almost spoken; chorus opens with a second voice answering like an older self; no theatrical anger, just pressure held carefully]

[PRODUCTION: Felt piano, brushed drums, dub bass in long notes, bowed string swells, bottle-tap percussion used sparingly, reverb pulls inward after every mother reference]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Glass on wood... weather coming...
Little feet beneath the stair
Breath held tight... table turning...
No one calls it storm in there

[Verse 1]
Mother was a country of sudden rain
gin in the lowlands, thunder at the sink
I learned the clouds by the cupboard hinge
by the way her hand forgot to think

Some nights she sang to nobody
some nights the walls took sides
I kept my passport under my tongue
and crossed myself inside

The ashtray filled like winter
two cigarettes in a shared command
I breathed the empire second-hand
and called it ordinary land

[Pre-Chorus]
Children know the barometer
before they know the date
They feel the pressure entering
and make themselves small weight

[Chorus]
Gin weather on the border
rain inside the bone
I was only trying to keep
one dry province of my own

Mother, I have named it now
but naming is not blame
Some countries drown their villages
and still keep someone's name

[Verse 2]
Morning came in careful pieces
cereal, schoolbag, practiced face
Nothing broken in the report
nothing marked in the right place

But the body keeps its ledger
not in words, but in the jaw
Every flinch becomes a treaty
signed before you read the law

I loved her from the doorway
where loving had a safer range
A child can miss the person
and still fear the weather change

[Pre-Chorus 2]
I do not need a verdict
I do not need a shrine
Just a map that shows the floodplain
and the higher ground as mine

[Chorus]
Gin weather on the border
rain inside the bone
I was only trying to keep
one dry province of my own

Mother, I have named it now
but naming is not blame
Some countries drown their villages
and still keep someone's name

[Bridge]
If I smoke, is it inheritance
or a match I chose to strike?
If I drink, is it geography
or a road I walk at night?

I carry her in weather,
not as sentence, not as crown
Just the lowland in my body
where the first rain settled down

[Final Chorus]
Gin weather on the border
still the river knows my name
I can build above the water
without denying where it came

Mother, I have named it now
and the name does not absolve
But the child beneath the stairwell
is a country that evolved

[Outro]
Glass on wood... morning after...
Clouds undone but never gone
Small dry place... hand on border...
I am here. I carried on
