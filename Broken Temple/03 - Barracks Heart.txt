[TITLE: Barracks Heart]

[GENRE: Folk-Indie / Warm Dub Bass / Chamber Pop]

[MOOD: Stern tenderness, distant safety -> grateful ache -> complicated inheritance]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: G minor lifting toward B-flat major in the final hook]

[VOCALS: Close male vocal, warmer than tracks one and two; restrained verses, plainspoken chorus, group hum entering only after the bridge]

[PRODUCTION: Double-tracked acoustic guitar, brushed snare in distant marching rhythm, upright bass, muted trumpet-like synth pad, small lighter click low in the mix, final chorus opens with soft harmonies]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Fold the coat... shine the heel...
Kind hands under iron drill
Smoke in sleeve... mercy kept...
Father standing where I slept

[Verse 1]
My father wore the service like weathered brass
not for glory, not for show
A barracks heart beneath his ribs
that beat softer than he'd know

He could square a room by entering
could make a doorway stand up straight
Then give his last good coin away
and call it nothing, call it late

He smoked beside the kitchen window
one eye tired, one eye kind
The world had taught his shoulders orders
but not how to leave love behind

[Pre-Chorus]
He was not gentle like a feather
He was gentle like a shield
Hard enough to face the weather
soft enough to never yield

[Chorus]
Barracks heart, barracks heart
you kept a lamp behind the guard
I learned that kindness can be armored
and still arrive with open palms

Barracks heart, barracks heart
your boots were heavy on the stair
But when the house became a country
you were the road that almost cared

[Verse 2]
There were medals I could not read
there were stories he would not tell
There were jokes that fed the evening
while the ash went gray and fell

I knew him through small provisions
rides, repairs, a lifted bag
Not speeches from a clean horizon
just his hand around the rag

He had a soldier's way of leaving
even standing in the room
Like tenderness required distance
or it might disobey too soon

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Still, a child can read an ember
under discipline and smoke
I knew the order of his loving
even when it never spoke

[Chorus]
Barracks heart, barracks heart
you kept a lamp behind the guard
I learned that kindness can be armored
and still arrive with open palms

Barracks heart, barracks heart
your boots were heavy on the stair
But when the house became a country
you were the road that almost cared

[Bridge]
I inherited the lighter
not the uniform or rank
I inherited the silence
and the joke before it sank

If my ribs became a fortress
if my jaw learned how to lock
there was mercy in the mortar
there was weather in the rock

[Final Chorus]
Barracks heart, barracks heart
I keep your ember, not your war
A guard post can become a garden
if the gate remembers more

Barracks heart, barracks heart
I carry both the smoke and care
Not every soldier of a country
knows the child he sheltered there

[Outro]
Fold the coat... lower the light...
Old boots resting out of sight
Kind hands still... under guard...
Mercy in the barracks heart
