[TITLE: Glass Frontier]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Ambient Bass / Soft Post-Rock Dub]

[MOOD: Exposed and watched -> inward defiance -> clearer self-possession]

[TEMPO: 88 BPM]

[KEY: A minor with a C major lift in the final refrain]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead with childlike restraint in verses; hook opens but stays held back; doubles arrive slightly out of sync on "frontier"; bridge nearly dry and alone]

[PRODUCTION: Clean electric arpeggios through dub delay, deep rounded sub drone, sparse floor-tom pulse, tiny lens-cloth texture as a rhythmic tick, glassy synth harmonics, sudden dry break before the bridge, final section widens without resolving]
