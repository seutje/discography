[TITLE: Glass Frontier]

[GENRE: Indie Slowcore / Ambient Folk / Soft Post-Rock]

[MOOD: Exposed, humiliated, observant -> inward defiance -> clearer self-possession]

[TEMPO: 88 BPM]

[KEY: A minor with a C major lift in the final refrain]

[VOCALS: Close male vocal, childlike restraint in verses, chorus sung with held-back ache, bridge nearly unaccompanied]

[PRODUCTION: Clean electric guitar arpeggio, felt piano, floor tom pulse, low bass drone, tiny lens-cleaning cloth sound used as rhythmic texture, sudden dry break before bridge]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Glass on face... world made near...
Every edge arrived too clear
Small boy sees... others stare...
Border drawn in open air

[Verse 1]
The first frontier sat on my nose
two little windows, silver sides
The classroom found its ammunition
in the place that helped my eyes

They called me names with easy mouths
made a sport of what I wore
I learned the art of looking down
while seeing every exit door

The body-country kept a border
thin as glass and twice as plain
I could read the board from distance
and still be blind inside the game

[Pre-Chorus]
Nothing breaks
but something learns
how a public laugh can burn

[Chorus]
Glass frontier, glass frontier
I saw too much and disappeared
Every face became a checkpoint
Every hallway, foreign ground

Glass frontier, glass frontier
I kept the world painfully near
If they mocked the way I witnessed
still the witness stayed around

[Verse 2]
Teachers said it made me brighter
children proved it made me marked
I became a walking target
with a country after dark

I climbed the walls behind the playground
ran until my throat went red
When the names came down like gravel
I put distance underhead

The body found its first escape route
up a fence or through a field
If the face became a border
then the legs became a shield

[Rupture - dry vocal, no drums]
They laughed.
I kept seeing.

They pointed.
I kept seeing.

They named me wrong.
I kept seeing.

[Chorus]
Glass frontier, glass frontier
I saw too much and disappeared
Every face became a checkpoint
Every hallway, foreign ground

Glass frontier, glass frontier
I kept the world painfully near
If they mocked the way I witnessed
still the witness stayed around

[Bridge]
There is no shame in needing lenses
no defeat in altered sight
Some countries build their towers
just to catch the morning right

I was not made less human
by the proof upon my face
I was only given windows
in a cruel and public place

[Final Chorus]
Glass frontier, glass frontier
I see the child standing there
Not as joke, not as warning
not as wound they get to own

Glass frontier, glass frontier
I bring the far things almost near
If my country learned through looking
then I bless what made it known

[Outro]
Glass on face... breath held still...
Small boy running up the hill
World made sharp... heart made spare...
Still I saw. I stayed there
