[TITLE: Glass Frontier]

[GENRE: Indietronica / Ambient Bass / Soft Post-Rock Dub]

[MOOD: Exposed and watched -> inward defiance -> clearer self-possession]

[TEMPO: 88 BPM]

[KEY: A minor (C major lift in final refrain)]

[VOCALS: Intimate androgynous lead; whisper-close texture. Verse: restrained/hushed. Hook: airy, wide but controlled. Bridge: dry, raw, intimate. Final: expansive.]

[PRODUCTION: Clean electric arpeggios (dub delay), deep rounded sub-bass, sparse floor-tom pulse, 'lens-cloth' rhythmic tick. High-end is smooth/warm; low-end is thick and stable. Sudden dry drop before bridge. Wide stereo field in final section.]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - lens tick | narrow arpeggio]

Glass on face
world made near

every edge

silver sides

of looking down

every exit door

thin as glass
and twice as plain

burns
