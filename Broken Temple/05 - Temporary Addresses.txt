[TITLE: Temporary Addresses]

[GENRE: Ghost-Folk Indie / Trip-Hop / Chamber Slowcore]

[MOOD: Displaced, watchful, tired -> small refuge -> unresolved warmth]

[TEMPO: 76 BPM]

[KEY: F-sharp minor]

[VOCALS: Near-whispered male vocal, close but distant, soft harmony answers on address phrases, final refrain warmer but not triumphant]

[PRODUCTION: Felt piano, nylon guitar, brushed kick, deep rounded bass, distant suitcase wheel texture, soft room reverb shifting from hallway to friend's living room by the outro]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Write the name... cross it out...
Another bed, another route
Bag by door... hand on strap...
Childhood folded without a map

[Verse 1]
Boarding homes along the body
each one stamped behind the ribs
Different rules for different kitchens
same small fear of being shipped

I learned the sound of borrowed stairways
learned which cupboards not to use
Learned a welcome can be temporary
even when it speaks to you

The country of me had no capital
only files and changing beds
Every pillow held a border
every morning changed the thread

[Pre-Chorus]
Suitcase knows
what people miss
The hand that packs
becomes the fist

[Chorus]
Temporary addresses
written under skin
I kept arriving carefully
and never moving in

Temporary addresses
doors that did not stay
I learned to sleep like someone
who might be asked away

[Verse 2]
Some were kind in careful portions
some were tired, some were cold
Some had rules like narrow bridges
some had silence growing mold

I became a quiet border
easy smile and hidden bag
Good at leaving no impression
good at folding up the flag

When they asked me where I lived
I gave the answer for that week
The truth was always in the hallway
with its shoes on, half-asleep

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Not abandoned
not received
Somewhere else
was always brief

[Chorus]
Temporary addresses
written under skin
I kept arriving carefully
and never moving in

Temporary addresses
doors that did not stay
I learned to sleep like someone
who might be asked away

[Bridge]
Maybe that is why, much later,
I choose the couch, the honest floor
not the hotel with its polished hallway
not the paid-for careful door

A friend's spare blanket feels more lawful
than a room that never asks
Some small countries only settle
where the welcome has no mask

[Final Chorus]
Temporary addresses
still behind the chest
But some names cross an ocean
and still make room for rest

Temporary addresses
I carry what they made
A body trained for leaving
can still learn where it stayed

[Outro]
Bag by door... shoes undone...
Somewhere safe for one more sun
Write the name... leave it there...
Not all shelter is a prayer
