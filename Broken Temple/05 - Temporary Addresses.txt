[TITLE: Temporary Addresses]

[GENRE: Indietronica / Trip-Hop / Ambient Dub Bass]

[MOOD: Displaced and watchful -> small refuge -> unresolved warmth]

[TEMPO: 76 BPM]

[KEY: F# minor]

[VOCALS: Near-whispered intimate androgynous lead; soft harmony answers on 'address' phrases; low doubled voice on 'asked away'; final refrain warms without becoming triumphant]

[PRODUCTION: Felt piano, delayed nylon guitar, deep rounded sub-bass, sparse brushed kick. Spatial shift: hallway reverb (Intro/Verse) $\rightarrow$ intimate living room (Bridge/Outro). High-cut filter on highs for warmth; clean low-end separation.]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - hallway air | suitcase wheel | deep rounded bass]

Write the name
cross it out

another bed
another route

Bag by door
hand on strap

childhood folded
without a map

[Verse 1 - minimal beat | close whisper]

Boarding homes
along the body
each one stamped
behind the ribs

Different rules
for different kitchens
same small fear
of being shipped

I learned the sound
of borrowed stairways
which cupboards
not to use

learned a welcome
can be temporary
even when
it speaks to you

The country of me
had no capital
only files
and changing beds

Every pillow
held a border
every morning
changed the thread

[Pre-Hook - delay tails stretch]

Suitcase knows
what people miss
the hand that packs
becomes the fist

[Hook - sub bloom | harmony on 'address']

Temporary addresses
written under skin
I kept arriving carefully
and never moving in

Temporary addresses
doors that did not stay
I learned to sleep like someone
who might be asked away

[Verse 2 - wider gaps | dub-filtered echoes]

Some were kind
in careful portions
some were tired
some were cold

Some had rules
like narrow bridges
some had silence
growing old

I became
a quiet border
easy smile
and hidden bag

Good at leaving
no impression
good at folding
up the flag

When they asked me
where I lived
I gave the answer
for that week

The truth was always
in the hallway
with its shoes on
half-asleep

[Pre-Hook - low octave shadow]

Not abandoned
not received

somewhere else
was always brief

[Hook - fuller low end | steady pulse]

Temporary addresses
written under skin
I kept arriving carefully
and never moving in

Temporary addresses
doors that did not stay
I learned to sleep like someone
who might be asked away

[Bridge - beat drops | spatial shift: room reverb]

Maybe that is why
much later

I choose the couch
the honest floor

not the paid room
with its polished hallway

not the careful
closing door

A friend's spare blanket
feels more lawful

than a room
that never asks

Some small countries
only settle

where the welcome
has no mask

[Final Hook - warm texture | harmony focus]

Temporary addresses
still behind the chest
but some names cross an ocean
and still make room for rest

Temporary addresses
I carry what they made
a body trained for leaving
can still learn where it stayed

[Outro - intimate living room | percussion fades]

Bag by door
shoes undone
somewhere safe
for one more sun

Write the name
leave it there

not all shelter
is a prayer
