[TITLE: Misfiled Weather]

[GENRE: Art-Indie / Ambient Pop / Dub Folk]

[MOOD: Misread, restless, analytical -> grief of wrong labels -> self-recognition]

[TEMPO: 90 BPM with a missing-beat breath before each chorus]

[KEY: D minor, bridge pivots through B-flat Lydian color]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, slightly faster phrasing in verses, chorus slows into a wider melodic line, bridge spoken-sung and exposed]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked guitar, low dub bass, small hand percussion, muted piano, soft synth pad, clinical click removed after the bridge; final chorus breathes more freely]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Wrong folder... right storm...
Paper says the child won't form
Quick feet... crowded head...
No one reads the road ahead

[Verse 1]
They measured me in moving parts
how I ran, how fast I missed
How my hands kept finding edges
how my thoughts came clenched in lists

ADHD, they wrote it cleanly
like the label closed the case
But the country underneath me
had a different kind of place

I was not only speed and scatter
not a spark that would not sit
I was reading every border
and exhausting all of it

[Pre-Chorus]
Wrong folder
right storm
I kept trying
to perform

[Chorus]
They named the weather badly
and sent me out in rain
No coat for what was coming
no word for why it pained

They saw the running river
missed the pressure in the ground
Called the border disobedient
when the map was upside down

[Verse 2]
I climbed because the world made sense
when every grip was true
Stone did not require small talk
branches never asked for proof

I ran because the route was honest
one lung, one field, one hill
No hidden rule beneath the sentence
no face demanding skill

But classrooms had a hundred weather maps
none of them for me
So I became a faulty forecast
in a country hard to see

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Wrong folder
right storm
Not broken
just unborn
inside their terms

[Chorus]
They named the weather badly
and sent me out in rain
No coat for what was coming
no word for why it pained

They saw the running river
missed the pressure in the ground
Called the border disobedient
when the map was upside down

[Bridge]
ASD came later
like a harbor after years
Not a cure, not a pardon
just a chart for older fears

I looked back at the child
misfiled under blame
and every rough translation
finally had a name

[Final Chorus]
They named the weather badly
but the weather still was real
Every country needs a season
it is allowed to feel

I was the running river
I was the mountain ground
Not the failure of a border
but a border never found

[Outro]
Wrong folder... right storm...
Late note enters, changed and warm
Quick feet... crowded head...
Now I read what went unsaid
