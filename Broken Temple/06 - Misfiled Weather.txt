[TITLE: Misfiled Weather]
[GENRE: Art-Indietronica / Ambient Bass / Dub Pop]

[MOOD: Misread and restless -> grief of wrong labels -> late self-recognition]

[TEMPO: 90 BPM with a missing-beat breath before each hook]

[KEY: D minor with a B-flat Lydian color in the bridge]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead with quicker verse phrasing; hook slows into a wider line; clinical whispers panned wide in early sections; after the bridge the vocal breathes more freely]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked guitar fragments through granular delay, low dub bass, small hand percussion, muted piano, thin diagnostic click removed after the bridge, filter automation opens when ASD is named, final hook less constrained but unresolved]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - diagnostic tick | low weather tone]

Wrong folder
right storm

paper says
the child won't form

Quick feet
crowded head

no one reads
the road ahead

[Verse 1 - tight rhythm | clipped phrasing]

They measured me
in moving parts
how I ran
how fast I missed

How my hands
kept finding edges
how my thoughts
came clenched in lists

ADHD
they wrote it cleanly
like the label
closed the case

But the country
underneath me
had a different
kind of place

I was not only
speed and scatter
not a spark
that would not sit

I was reading
every border
and exhausting
all of it

[Pre-Hook - missing beat | low shadow]

Wrong folder
right storm

I kept trying
to perform

[Hook - bass opens | wider vocal]

They named the weather badly
and sent me out in rain
no coat for what was coming
no word for why it pained

They saw the running river
missed the pressure in the ground
called the border disobedient
when the map was upside down

[Verse 2 - dub echoes | more air]

I climbed because
the world made sense
when every grip
was true

Stone did not require
small talk
branches never
asked for proof

I ran because
the route was honest
one lung
one field
one hill

No hidden rule
beneath the sentence
no face demanding
skill

But classrooms held
a hundred weather maps
none of them
for me

So I became
a faulty forecast
in a country
hard to see

[Pre-Hook - delay repeats]

Wrong folder
right storm

not broken
just unborn

inside their terms

[Hook - fuller, still restrained]

They named the weather badly
and sent me out in rain
no coat for what was coming
no word for why it pained

They saw the running river
missed the pressure in the ground
called the border disobedient
when the map was upside down

[Bridge - diagnostic click stops | exposed vocal]

ASD came later
like a harbor
after years

Not a cure
not a pardon
just a chart
for older fears

I looked back
at the child
misfiled
under blame

and every rough
translation
finally had
a name

[Final Hook - open filter | warmer low end]

They named the weather badly
but the weather still was real
every country needs a season
it is allowed to feel

I was the running river
I was the mountain ground
not the failure of a border
but a border never found

[Outro - fading clickless pulse]

Wrong folder
right storm

late note enters
changed and warm

Quick feet
crowded head

now I read
what went unsaid
