[TITLE: Runner's Republic]

[GENRE: Motorik Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Kinetic defiance -> bodily freedom -> compromised breath]

[TEMPO: 104 BPM $\rightarrow$ 88 BPM (Outro)]

[KEY: E minor, G major suspension]

[VOCALS: Androgynous; Verse: clipped/intimate. Hook: strained lift. Outro: breathy.]

[PRODUCTION: Motorik kick, dub delay, sub-bass. Motif: 4-note climbing synth-guitar (delayed final note). Transition from wide stereo to mono-dry.]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - motorik pulse | motif: 4-note climb]

Fence line
wet palms

heel to earth
and gone

Branch holds
chest burns

body-country
learns

[Verse 1 - driving beat | clipped vocal]

I was good
at leaving ground
good at finding
where to place

one foot
then the other
one hand
above the face

Walls had honest
politics
gravity made
its claim

If I fell
I knew the reason
if I rose
I knew the same

Running gave
the road a verdict
climbing gave
the hands a vote

In the republic
of the body
motion was
the clearest note

[Pre-Hook - delay throws widen | motif: 4-note climb]

No teacher
at the checkpoint

no hallway
taking names

only breath
and timing

only rain
against the frame

[Hook - bass expands | strained lift]

Runner's republic
climber's little state
I could leave the old command
if I could reach the gate

Runner's republic
lungs like borrowed drums
every mile said I was living
every hill said I could come

[Verse 2 - denser pulse | low shadow]

Then cigarettes
arrived early
like a treaty
signed by smoke

Both my parents
held the weather
so I thought
the weather spoke

I learned the drag
before the warning
learned the burn
and called it calm

Little ember
in the borderlands
litter ruin
in the palm

Still I ran
through parks and roofs
still I climbed
where fences bent

Not pure
not clean
not rescued

just a body
spending rent

[Pre-Hook - rhythm thins | motif: 4-note climb]

The road forgave
the damage

until the damage
learned the road

Every freedom
has a cost

when it carries
what it owed

[Hook - fuller low end | wide panning]

Runner's republic
climber's little state
I could leave the old command
if I could reach the gate

Runner's republic
lungs like borrowed drums
every mile said I was living
every hill said I could come

[Bridge - beat drops | sub-bass focus]

I don't regret
the climbing

I don't repent
the run

Those were the first
real borders

I crossed
under my own sun

But smoke
had taken office
in the ministry
of air

and every breath
I won back

found it waiting
there

[Final Hook - tempo easing | strained lift]

Runner's republic
weather in the lung
I was fast because I needed
somewhere else to run

Runner's republic
climber's scarred domain
if the body kept a country
then motion was my name

[Outro - tempo 88 BPM | mono-dry | motif: 4-note climb (slowed)]

Fence line
breath slows

hill behind me
glows

Branch holds
hands sore

still I reach
for more

(the road...)
(the lung...)

[End]
