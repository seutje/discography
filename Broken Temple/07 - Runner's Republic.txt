[TITLE: Runner's Republic]
[GENRE: Motorik Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Kinetic defiance -> bodily freedom -> compromised breath]

[TEMPO: 104 BPM easing to 88 BPM in the outro]

[KEY: E minor with a G major release that remains suspended]

[VOCALS: More rhythmic intimate androgynous lead in verses; hook opens with strained lift; breath sounds used as percussion; final outro returns close and quiet; low doubles on lung and motion images]

[PRODUCTION: Driving muted guitar pulse, motorik kick softened by dub delay, elastic sub bass, handclaps tucked low, climbing four-note synth-guitar figure with the last note delayed, breath percussion, stereo panning follows running motion, outro slows and narrows]
