[TITLE: Runner's Republic]

[GENRE: Indie Rock / Ambient Folk Electronic / Motorik Dub]

[MOOD: Kinetic, young defiance -> bodily freedom -> compromised breath]

[TEMPO: 104 BPM, settles to 88 BPM in the outro]

[KEY: E minor with a G major release]

[VOCALS: More rhythmic male vocal in verses, chorus sung with open but strained melody, final outro quiet and close]

[PRODUCTION: Driving muted guitar, brushed motorik drums, elastic bass, handclaps tucked low, breath percussion, climbing guitar figure ascending four notes with the last note delayed]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Fence line... wet palms...
Heel to earth and gone
Branch holds... chest burns...
Body-country learns

[Verse 1]
I was good at leaving ground
good at finding where to place
one foot, then the other,
one hand above the face

Walls had honest politics
gravity made its claim
If I fell, I knew the reason
If I rose, I knew the same

Running gave the road a verdict
climbing gave the hands a vote
In the republic of the body
motion was the clearest note

[Pre-Chorus]
No teacher at the checkpoint
No hallway taking names
Only breath and timing
Only rain against the frame

[Chorus]
Runner's republic
climber's little state
I could leave the old command
if I could reach the gate

Runner's republic
lungs like borrowed drums
Every mile said I was living
Every hill said I could come

[Verse 2]
Then the cigarettes arrived early
like a treaty signed by smoke
Both my parents held the weather
so I thought the weather spoke

I learned the drag before the warning
learned the burn and called it calm
Little ember in the borderlands
little ruin in the palm

Still I ran through parks and rooftops
still I climbed where fences bent
Not pure, not clean, not rescued
just a body spending rent

[Pre-Chorus 2]
The road forgave the damage
until the damage learned the road
Every freedom has a cost
when it carries what it owed

[Chorus]
Runner's republic
climber's little state
I could leave the old command
if I could reach the gate

Runner's republic
lungs like borrowed drums
Every mile said I was living
Every hill said I could come

[Bridge]
I don't regret the climbing
I don't repent the run
Those were the first real borders
I crossed under my own sun

But smoke had taken office
in the ministry of air
and every breath I won back
found it waiting there

[Final Chorus]
Runner's republic
weather in the lung
I was fast because I needed
somewhere else to run

Runner's republic
climber's scarred domain
If the body kept a country
then motion was my name

[Outro - tempo eases]
Fence line... breath slows...
Hill behind me glows
Branch holds... hands sore...
Still I reach for more
