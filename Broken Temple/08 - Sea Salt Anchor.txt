[TITLE: Sea Salt Anchor]

[GENRE: Dark Indietronica / Deep Space Dub / Slow Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Seduced and ashamed -> recognition -> unsteady refusal]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: F minor]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead; Verse: low/controlled. Hook: strained/distorted. Bridge: breathy whisper. Final Hook: thin/raw human texture. Low octave shadow on 'anchor'.]

[PRODUCTION: Sub-bass undertow, sparse piano, rim clicks (grains on glass), low organ. Tape delay on 'anchor' only. Sudden vacuum drop before bridge. Final section: percussion cut, raw breath against sub-bass.]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - grain clicks | low undertow]

Salt on glass
line drawn thin

old tide knocking
under skin

Hold it down
hold it still

same old rope
a sharper will

[Verse 1 - minimal pulse | dry vocal]

I wrote once
about an anchor
salt water
in the throat

How every cure
can pull you under
while pretending
it can float

Years went by
and I got clever
changed the cup
refined the harm

Called the hunger
by new weather
held the coast
along my arm

White shoreline
on a mirror
little harbor
bitter spark

I said it was
only sea salt
for the country
after dark

[Pre-Hook - delay tightens]

Not a wave
not a drink

just a tide
I chose to think

[Hook - heavy sub | strained harmony]

I snorted sea salt like an anchor
said it kept the wreckage near
every grain became a border
every border disappeared

Salt water anchor
old companion
new coast under older need
I was not chasing heaven
I was teaching ache to feed

[Verse 2 - dub echoes | wide spatial depth]

Cocaine has
a clean white sermon
for the man
who wants control

It says the body
is a nation
you can occupy
by nose

It builds a flag
inside the bloodstream
raises troops
behind the eyes

For one hour
all the ruins
stand at attention
and comply

Then the morning
comes unarmored
then the mouth
is full of sand

Then the country
asks the body
who has governed
by command

[Pre-Hook - low octave shadow]

Not a cure
not a crown

just the old pull
renamed and ground

[Hook - fuller, unstable texture]

I snorted sea salt like an anchor
said it kept the wreckage near
every grain became a border
every border disappeared

Salt water anchor
old companion
new coast under older need
I was not chasing heaven
I was teaching ache to feed

[Bridge - percussion drop | vacuum space]

If I cut it loose
tonight

will the floor
still know my weight

If I leave the salt
untouched

will my hands
begin to shake

I am forty years
of wanting

stacked in jars
along the wall
one says smoke
one says powder

one says nothing
much at all

[Rupture - heavy sub hit | sudden silence]

I called it anchor

It called me back

[Final Hook - percussion removed | raw/thin vocal]

I snorted sea salt like an anchor
until the harbor lost its name
now I stand beside the mirror
with no mercy and no blame

Salt water anchor
(the first song's truth)
some weights pretend to hold you
while they carry you away

[Outro - breath & sub-bass | long delay tail]

Salt on glass
hand withdrawn

old tide speaking
past the dawn

Hold it down
let it go

I am tired
of sinking slow
