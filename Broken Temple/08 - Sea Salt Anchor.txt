[TITLE: Sea Salt Anchor]

[GENRE: Dark Indie / Deep Space Dub / Slowcore Electronica]

[MOOD: Seduced, ashamed, compulsive -> recognition -> unsteady refusal]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: F minor]

[VOCALS: Close male vocal, verses low and controlled, chorus strained, bridge nearly whispered over sub bass; final hook thinner, more human]

[PRODUCTION: Slow dub sub, sparse piano, rim clicks like grains on glass, low organ, tape delay on 'anchor' only, sudden vacuum drop before the bridge, final section removes percussion]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Salt on glass... rail drawn thin...
Old tide knocking under skin
Hold it down... hold it still...
Same old rope, a sharper will

[Verse 1]
I wrote once about an anchor
salt water in the throat
How every cure can pull you under
while pretending it can float

Years went by and I got clever
changed the cup, refined the harm
Called the hunger by new weather
held the coast along my arm

White shoreline on a mirror
little harbor, bitter spark
I said it was only sea salt
for the country after dark

[Pre-Chorus]
Not a wave
not a drink
Just a tide
I chose to think

[Chorus]
I snorted sea salt like an anchor
said it kept the wreckage near
Every grain became a border
every border disappeared

Salt water anchor, old companion
new coast under older need
I was not chasing heaven
I was teaching ache to feed

[Verse 2]
Cocaine has a clean white sermon
for the man who wants control
It says the body is a nation
you can occupy by nose

It builds a flag inside the bloodstream
raises troops behind the eyes
For one hour, all the ruins
stand at attention and comply

Then the morning comes unarmored
then the mouth is full of sand
Then the country asks the body
who has governed by command

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Not a cure
not a crown
Just the old pull
renamed and ground

[Chorus]
I snorted sea salt like an anchor
said it kept the wreckage near
Every grain became a border
every border disappeared

Salt water anchor, old companion
new coast under older need
I was not chasing heaven
I was teaching ache to feed

[Bridge - percussion drops]
If I cut it loose tonight
will the floor still know my weight?
If I leave the salt untouched
will my hands begin to shake?

I am forty years of wanting
stacked in jars along the wall
One says smoke, one says powder,
one says nothing much at all

[Rupture]
I called it anchor.

It called me back.

[Final Chorus]
I snorted sea salt like an anchor
until the harbor lost its name
Now I stand beside the mirror
with no mercy and no blame

Salt water anchor, I remember
what the first song tried to say
Some weights pretend to hold you
while they carry you away

[Outro]
Salt on glass... hand withdrawn...
Old tide speaking past the dawn
Hold it down... let it go...
I am tired of sinking slow
