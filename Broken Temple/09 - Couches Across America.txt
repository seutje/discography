[TITLE: Couches Across America]
[GENRE: Warm Indietronica / Ambient Pop Dub / Soft Bass]

[MOOD: Travel-worn and grateful -> chosen kinship -> quiet belonging]

[TEMPO: 92 BPM]

[KEY: C minor lifting toward E-flat major without full cadence]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate androgynous lead with warmer delivery; small smile in rhythmic phrasing; gentle harmony in the final hook; spoken travel fragments tucked low in the outro]

[PRODUCTION: Soft sub bass, muted acoustic plucks through dub delay, tiny airport ambience, porch-night texture tucked low, couch-spring creak before each hook, low organ pad, percussion loose and human, final section keeps the room tone present]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - gate chime | couch spring | soft sub]

Gate call
red-eye town

bag half packed
and touching down

No gold room
no marble bed

friend's old couch
beneath my head

[Verse 1 - warm pulse | close vocal]

I crossed the ocean
for spare blankets
for kitchens where
the kettle knew

I would rather
sleep near laughter
than in any
polished room

America came
through doorways
not as flags
or hotel floors

but socks beside
a borrowed sofa
and a friend
who said of course

The body-country
found an embassy
in the dip
between two seats

Somewhere west
of my old trouble
someone made
the exile brief

[Pre-Hook - delay opens]

No bellhop
no receipt

just a pillow
and relief

[Hook - warm bass | soft harmony]

Couches across America
soft republics after dark
I have slept in living rooms
and woken with a steadier heart

Couches across America
not glamorous, not grand
but a country can be mended
by the offer of a hand

[Verse 2 - wider room | gentle motion]

I have known
the floor in Boston
known the spare key
in L.A.

Known the Midwest
morning coffee
and the porch
at end of day

Every city
had its weather
every friend
their private scars

We compared
the broken temples
under ordinary stars

I did not need
the perfect mattress
or the towel
folded tight

I needed someone
in the next room
making breakfast
out of light

[Pre-Hook - low room tone]

Old child
near the door

finds a place
not paid for

[Hook - fuller low end | relaxed]

Couches across America
soft republics after dark
I have slept in living rooms
and woken with a steadier heart

Couches across America
not glamorous, not grand
but a country can be mended
by the offer of a hand

[Bridge - beat softens | spoken edges]

Maybe all those
boarding houses

taught me how
to travel lean

Maybe friendship
is the border

where the old route
turns green

I don't need
a room to own me

I don't need
a perfect view

just a couch
that says the body

can stop marching
for a few

[Final Hook - gentle harmony | open room]

Couches across America
small cathedrals made of thread
I have crossed the sea for people
not for monuments or beds

Couches across America
send me home but leave a mark
every borrowed living room
kept a lamp inside the dark

[Outro - room tone | distant airport fragment]

Gate call
morning plane

same old body
changed terrain

No gold room
one good friend

sometimes exile
learns an end
