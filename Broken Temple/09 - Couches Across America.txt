[TITLE: Couches Across America]

[GENRE: Warm Indie Folk / Ambient Pop / Soft Dub]

[MOOD: Travel-worn, grateful, intimate -> chosen kinship -> quiet belonging]

[TEMPO: 92 BPM]

[KEY: C minor lifting toward E-flat major]

[VOCALS: Close male vocal with warmer delivery, small smile in phrasing, gentle harmony in final chorus, spoken fragments tucked in the outro]

[PRODUCTION: Acoustic guitar, soft kick, upright bass, tiny airport ambience, porch insects tucked low, low organ pad, couch-spring creak used once before each chorus]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Gate call... red-eye town...
Bag half packed and touching down
No gold room... no marble bed...
Friend's old couch beneath my head

[Verse 1]
I crossed the ocean for spare blankets
for kitchens where the kettle knew
that I would rather sleep near laughter
than in any polished room

America came through doorways
not as flags or hotel floors
But socks beside a borrowed sofa
and a friend who said, of course

The body-country found an embassy
in the dip between two seats
Somewhere west of my old trouble
someone made the exile brief

[Pre-Chorus]
No bellhop
no receipt
Just a pillow
and relief

[Chorus]
Couches across America
soft republics after dark
I have slept in living rooms
and woken with a steadier heart

Couches across America
not glamorous, not grand
But a country can be mended
by the offer of a hand

[Verse 2]
I have known the floor in Boston
known the spare key in L.A.
Known the Midwest morning coffee
and the porch at end of day

Every city had its weather
every friend their private scars
We compared the broken temples
under ordinary stars

I did not need the perfect mattress
or the towel folded tight
I needed someone in the next room
making breakfast out of light

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Old child
still near the door
finds a place
not paid for

[Chorus]
Couches across America
soft republics after dark
I have slept in living rooms
and woken with a steadier heart

Couches across America
not glamorous, not grand
But a country can be mended
by the offer of a hand

[Bridge]
Maybe all those boarding houses
taught me how to travel lean
Maybe friendship is the border
where the old route turns green

I don't need a room to own me
I don't need a perfect view
Just a couch that says the body
can stop marching for a few

[Final Chorus]
Couches across America
small cathedrals made of thread
I have crossed the sea for people
not for monuments or beds

Couches across America
send me home but leave a mark
Every borrowed living room
kept a lamp inside the dark

[Outro]
Gate call... morning plane...
Same old body, changed terrain
No gold room... one good friend...
Sometimes exile learns an end
