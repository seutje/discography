[TITLE: Broken Temple]

[GENRE: Chamber Indie / Deep Dub Bass / Ambient Folk Electronic]

[MOOD: Adult reckoning, addiction-held -> pressure release -> unresolved dignity]

[TEMPO: 80 BPM, final section breathes in free time]

[KEY: D minor moving through F major and ending suspended on A]

[VOCALS: Close male lead, weary but steady; stacked harmonies in final chorus, low octave support in rupture, outro nearly spoken with group hum]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked guitar, deep dub bass, brushed drums, organ, piano, bowed strings, distant room percussion, controlled distortion in the rupture, final coda strips back to guitar, bass, and human voices]

[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Stone by stone... breath by breath...
Forty years of holding death
Rib and altar... door and lung...
Broken temple, still not done

[Verse 1]
I am forty in the body-country
with a border made of habits
Smoke in the northern rafters
salt under the palace tablets

Old drink weather in the lowlands
white coast where the mirror stood
Screens, bargains, late-night promises
each one saying it meant good

I am not held by one chain
not one villain, not one cause
Just repeated little treaties
signed against my better laws

[Pre-Chorus]
No single wound
No single cure
Just a temple
still unsure

[Chorus]
Broken temple, hold together
not as holy, not as clean
Every crack has kept a record
of the countries I have been

Broken temple, low and weathered
still the candles try to live
I am built from what I carried
and the weight I keep to give

[Verse 2]
Youngest son behind the table
gin rain under kitchen beams
Barracks heart beside the window
glasses catching classroom teeth

Boarding homes along the hallway
wrong diagnosis in the file
Runner's lungs and climber's fingers
smoke arriving all the while

Friends across the ocean held me
on their couches, plain and kind
Some soft republics saved the body
from the borders in the mind

[Pre-Chorus 2]
All of it
still in the stone
All of it
not mine alone

[Bridge - drums thin, bass remains]
I used to think survival
meant never touching ground
Keep the engine in the chest
keep the old tide weighted down

But the temple does not heal
because I curse the falling stone
It needs hands, and time, and weather,
and the truth of being known

[Rupture - full band, then sudden drop]
Mother's rain.
Father's smoke.
Four boys running.
Glasses broke.

Wrong name written.
New name found.
Salt line waiting.
Couch laid down.

Forty years.
Still here.

[Final Chorus]
Broken temple, hold together
not by hiding every scar
Let the late note enter fully
let it say how small we are

Broken temple, low and weathered
not repaired, but not refused
I can be a damaged country
and still choose how I am used

[Coda - free time]
Stone by stone
I ask the body
what it needs
before it breaks

Breath by breath
I leave the anchor
where the old tide
cannot take

No clean ending
No bright pardon
No final bell
across the foam

Just a temple
still receiving
all the people
who came home

[Outro]
Rib and altar... hand and lung...
Fourth note late, but finally sung
Broken temple... weathered through...
Something damaged holding true
