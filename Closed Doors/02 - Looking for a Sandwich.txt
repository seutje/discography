[TITLE: Looking for a Sandwich]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Ironic, wounded, self-aware, dryly funny → warmer, defiant, quietly self-accepting]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: E minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, Spoken-word rap verses with conversational phrasing, sharp pauses, and dry comic timing, Soft indie-folk sung hook, Starts sardonic and defensive, becomes open and human, Close-mic verses, slight rasp on punchlines, occasional whispered doubles, Final section more melodic, less guarded, ]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with percussive busker groove, Lo-fi hip-hop drums: soft kick, brushed snare, hand taps, Warm upright-style bass, Sparse piano after second hook, Subtle room noise for intimacy, Live, imperfect, close mix, Full-stop dropouts before punchlines, Final third adds soft harmonies and warmer bass, staying raw and uncluttered, ]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken | dry, close-mic | sparse guitar]

Someone said
my words were salad.

Which is fair.

There were leaves.
There were layers.
There was dressing involved.

But I couldn’t help thinking—

maybe you were just
looking for a sandwich.

[Verse 1 – spoken rap | light guitar groove]

I spent weeks folding feelings into strange little shapes
Making paper birds out of things I couldn’t say
Then someone came with a fork in their fist like,
“Mate, this is confusing. Where’s the plate?”

I said, “The plate is implied.”
They said, “That’s the problem.”
I said, “The problem is hunger dressed up as logic.”

I wrote, “the moon has a bruise where the morning got in”
They said, “What does that mean?”
I said, “It means I was lonely.”
They said, “Why not say that?”
I said, “Because I was lonely.”

Some people build houses
Some people build tents
Some leave notes in bottles
then complain about rent

Me?
I build mazes with a door at the start
Then get offended when nobody studies the walls
and calls it art.

[Pre-Hook – half-sung, half-spoken]

Maybe I hid too well
Maybe I dressed the wound too fine
Maybe I made a meal
then blamed you for wanting a sign

[Hook – soft sung folk melody]

If you see only salad
maybe you were looking for a sandwich
Maybe I was speaking in storms
when you only packed a blanket

If you couldn’t find the meaning
maybe I buried it too deep
But don’t call it empty
just because it made you reach

[Post-Hook – spoken aside]

Still…
could’ve used less parsley.

[Verse 2 – spoken rap | drums enter, brushed snare]

I used to think being understood
meant being reduced
Like if I gave you the key
then the room would be used

Like someone would walk through my head
with muddy shoes
Point at the curtains and say,
“Bit much, don’t you think?”

And maybe it is.

Maybe I put chandeliers in sheds.
Maybe I describe a headache
like a cathedral arguing with bees.
Maybe I hid a whole confession in a slice of cake
and called it liberation.

But listen—

When you grow up explaining your face
your tone
your hands
your silence
your reasons for standing too close
then too far away
then nowhere anyone can reach—

you learn that plain speech
is not always safer.

Sometimes plain speech is a window.
Sometimes metaphor is curtains.
Sometimes curtains are the only thing
between you
and everyone looking in
like they paid for a tour.

So yes, I served you leaves.
Bitter ones. Strange ones.
A few I should’ve washed.

But there was a pulse underneath.
There was a table.
There was a chair pulled out.
There was me, trying badly,
to invite you somewhere.

[Hook – fuller, warmer]

If you see only salad
maybe you were looking for a sandwich
Maybe I was speaking in storms
when you only packed a blanket

If you couldn’t find the meaning
maybe I buried it too deep
But don’t call it empty
just because it made you reach

[Bridge – beat drops | spoken, vulnerable]

And I get it.

Not every locked door is profound.
Sometimes it’s just a locked door.

Not every strange phrase is a treasure chest.
Sometimes it’s just me
panicking attractively.

Sometimes the truth looks cold all apart,
so I overdress it
and call it art.

Sometimes I leave the door half-open
so the room can change with who walks in
If I name every shadow too clearly
it stops belonging to anyone but me

That’s annoying.

Also useful.

You came looking for lunch
and found a forest.

I came looking for praise
and found a mirror
wearing your disappointed face.

[Verse 3 – warmer spoken rap | piano enters lightly]

Maybe the next one gets a signpost
Maybe the next one gets bread
Maybe the next time I say “grief”
without dressing it up as weather in my head

Maybe I leave room for the listener
without handing them a map
Maybe I stop calling every open hand
a trap

But I won’t flatten the hills
just to make the walk polite
I won’t drain all the color
so the water looks right

There’s a place between nonsense
and over-explained
Between “look how deep I am”
and “please feel my pain”

I’m trying to find it.
Badly.
With herbs.

So if the line feels crooked
maybe sit with the bend
If the meaning comes late
maybe wait for the end

And if you still see salad
well, fair enough, friend—

But I was feeding something
I couldn’t name then.

[Final Hook – warm, gently triumphant]

If you see only salad
maybe you were looking for a sandwich
Maybe I was speaking in storms
when you only packed a blanket

If you couldn’t find the meaning
maybe I buried it too deep
But don’t call it empty
just because it made you reach

If you see only salad
maybe I’ll bring bread next time
But I won’t stop growing strange things
in the garden of my mind

[Outro – spoken, slight smile | instruments fading]

So no hard feelings.

You wanted simple.
I wanted seen.

Somewhere between the lettuce
and the longing…

we were both
a little hungry.
