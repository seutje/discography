[TITLE: Manual Override]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Dry, defensive, over-analytical, quietly ashamed → warmer, embodied, gently defiant, self-trusting]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: E minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, Spoken-word rap verses with conversational phrasing, precise pauses, and dry comic timing, Soft indie-folk sung hook with fragile warmth, Starts controlled and self-mocking, becomes more open and grounded, Close-mic verses, slight rasp on punchlines, occasional whispered doubles on repeated phrases, Final section more melodic and less guarded, ]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with percussive busker groove, Lo-fi hip-hop drums: soft kick, brushed snare, hand taps, Warm upright-style bass, Sparse piano enters after second hook, Subtle room noise and chair creaks for intimacy, Live, imperfect, close mix, Full-stop dropouts before punchlines, Final third adds soft harmonies and warmer bass, staying raw and uncluttered, ]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken | dry, close-mic | sparse guitar]

Please remain calm.

I have reviewed
the situation

and after careful consideration

I have decided

I am probably
the situation.

Which is inconvenient.

[Verse 1 – spoken rap | light guitar groove]

I used to think if I could name every wire
I could stop the whole house from catching fire
Label the switches, diagram the sparks
Put little warning signs over the difficult parts

“This one controls the childhood.”
“This one makes jokes when afraid.”
“This one says ‘I’m fine’ so convincingly
even I file it away.”

Someone said,
“You don’t have to explain yourself.”

Which is sweet.

Also suspicious.

Because I have met myself
and frankly
context helps.

There are doors in me that open sideways.
There are stairs that forget what height is.
There are rooms where the light switch works
but only if no one asks why it’s like this.

And yes,
I know that sounds dramatic.

But so is pretending
a locked basement
is just “storage”
because the listing said
open plan.

[Pre-Hook – half-sung, half-spoken]

Maybe I made the map too large
Maybe I named too many roads
Maybe I turned every scar
into something with a code

[Hook – soft sung folk melody]

I’m looking for the manual override
Something in me still alive
Past the warning lights
Past the reasons I provide

I’m more than the fault I find
More than the wires I hide behind
If you stay beside the noise
you might hear my actual voice

[Post-Hook – spoken aside]

It’s under settings.

Obviously.

Nested.

For no reason.

[Verse 2 – spoken rap | drums enter, brushed snare]

I used to rehearse being human
like there’d be a practical exam

Smile here.
Pause there.
Ask about their weekend.
Do not mention mortality
before the second drink.

I learned eye contact
like a hostage negotiation.

Too little?
Suspicious.

Too much?
Cult leader.

Somewhere in the middle
there is apparently
friendship.

I make plans
with the version of me
who believes in calendars.

Then actual me arrives
wearing yesterday’s weather
and holding one shoe
for reasons
still under investigation.

And people say,
“Just tell me what you need.”

As if need is a clean little package
with a bow and a tracking number.

Sometimes need is fog.
Sometimes need is a chair.
Sometimes need is leaving the room
before my face starts making statements
my mouth cannot legally defend.

Sometimes I don’t know
until the damage
has sent a receipt.

But I am trying.

Badly.

With paperwork.

[Hook – fuller, warmer]

I’m looking for the manual override
Something in me still alive
Past the warning lights
Past the reasons I provide

I’m more than the fault I find
More than the wires I hide behind
If you stay beside the noise
you might hear my actual voice

[Bridge – beat drops | spoken, vulnerable]

And I get tired

of being both
the fire
and the evacuation plan.

Of saying sorry
for the smoke
while still coughing
from the room that made it.

I know I can be sharp.

I have watched my words
leave my mouth
wearing boots.

I have seen kindness
come out crooked
because it had to pass
through old machinery.

But listen—

not every alarm
is manipulation.

Not every shutdown
is refusal.

Not every silence
is punishment.

Sometimes the system
is not ignoring you.

Sometimes it is using
everything it has

not to disappear.

[Verse 3 – warmer spoken rap | piano enters lightly]

So here is the part
where I stop pretending
self-knowledge
is the same thing
as self-trust.

Because I know the patterns.
I know the loops.
I know the exits I choose
then accuse of being proof.

I can name the weather.
That doesn’t mean
I have to become
a forecast forever.

Maybe the override
was never a lever
behind glass.

Maybe it was smaller.

A breath before the joke.
A text before the vanishing.
A hand on the table
instead of a case for the defense.

Maybe I say,
“I’m overwhelmed,”
before I turn into furniture.

Maybe you say,
“Take your time,”
without making time
sound like a loan.

There’s a place
between full disclosure
and closed doors.

Between “here is every wound”
and “nothing to see here.”

I’m trying to stand there.

Not perfectly.

Not politely.

But present.

[Final Hook – warm, gently triumphant]

I’m looking for the manual override
Something in me still alive
Past the warning lights
Past the reasons I provide

I’m more than the fault I find
More than the wires I hide behind
If you stay beside the noise
you might hear my actual voice

I’m looking for the manual override
Not to erase what I survive
Just to choose the door this time
before the old alarms decide

[Outro – spoken, slight smile | instruments fading]

So.

I may require patience.

I may arrive strangely.

I may need to restart
the sentence
several times.

But I am not
a broken machine.

I am a person

learning which buttons
were never meant
to be pressed

and which ones

were mine

all along.
