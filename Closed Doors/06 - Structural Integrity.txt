[TITLE: Structural Integrity]
[GENRE: Acoustic Old-School Hip-Hop / Funk-Folk Rap / Spoken-Word]

[MOOD: Playful panic, brittle humor, overwhelmed → collapse → careful rebuilding, defiant recovery]

[TEMPO: 104 BPM with fast triplet bursts]

[KEY: E minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal with old-school call-and-response energy, fast triplet pockets, sharp internal rhymes, and dry spoken asides, Delivery is witty and rhythmic early, increasingly strained during meltdown, then steadier and warmer after collapse, Layered group-style shouts on key phrases: “Check the beams!” “Load-bearing!” “Clear the room!”]

[PRODUCTION: Percussive acoustic guitar riff, upright bass bounce, handclaps, foot stomps, table taps, brushed snare, and mouth percussion, No glossy electronics, Sudden stop-start breaks before punchlines, During collapse, instruments detune slightly, rhythm stumbles, room sound gets larger, Rebuild section returns with simpler guitar, softer bass, and calmer call-response, ]
