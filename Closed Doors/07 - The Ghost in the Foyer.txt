[TITLE: The Ghost in the Foyer]
[GENRE: Ghost-Folk / Trip-Hop]

[MOOD: Hollow, withdrawn, haunted, self-aware → quietly devastating, unresolved]

[TEMPO: 68 BPM]

[KEY: A minor]

[VOCALS: Whispered male vocal with multi-tracked harmonies, close but distant, like voices from the next room, Fragile spoken-sung verses, soft ghostly refrains, restrained emotion, Occasional breath doubles and murmured backing lines, Delivery feels absent, careful, almost apologetic, ]

[PRODUCTION: Slow ghost-folk/trip-hop, Felt-dampened upright piano, sparse brushed drums, deep muted bass, distant ticking clocks, floorboard creaks, large empty-room reverb, Minimal guitar harmonics and bowed textures, Long silences between phrases, Arrangement feels like walking through an abandoned house, Final section adds faint harmonies, then recedes into room tone, ]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – whispered | distant piano | ticking clock]

Please wipe your feet.

The house is still open.

Technically.

There are lights in the hall,
fresh flowers on the table,
clean cups by the sink.

I keep it presentable.

I just don’t live here anymore.

[Verse 1 – spoken-sung | sparse piano]

I used to meet them at the doorway
with a smile I’d rehearsed
and a careful little welcome
wrapped around the worst

I dusted all the portraits
hung the grief in level frames
put the softer names forward
hid the sharper ones away

There was a room for conversation
with the chairs turned toward the sun
there was a room where I could laugh
if the laughing had to come

And if someone knocked gently
I could almost play the host
pouring tea for their questions
like I wasn’t mostly ghost

[Refrain – soft harmony]

But I moved back
room by room

past the stairs
past the bloom

If you call out
I might hear

but I’m not
standing near

[Verse 2 – trip-hop beat enters softly]

The curator kept working
after the guests went home
straightened every hallway
polished every bone

Changed the labels in the morning
made the silence look arranged
kept the windows mostly open
so the air would feel exchanged

But behind the velvet—
No.

Behind the heavy curtain
behind the locked-up weather
behind the part that answers
“I’m fine, just tired, whatever”

there’s another little passage
and another after that
a narrow set of stairs
where the house forgets the map

I went there without luggage
without leaving any sign
just a habit of retreating
one room deeper every time

[Refrain – fuller, still distant]

And I moved back
room by room

past the clocks
past the blue

If you find me
don’t be sure

that the shape
is mine anymore

[Bridge – beat drops | whispered, next-room harmonies]

This is not privacy.

Privacy has a door
and a person behind it.

This is not peace.

Peace sits down
when the evening comes.

This is a house
kept warm
for visitors

by someone
who sleeps
in the walls.

I know where everything belongs.

The keys.
The coats.
The old apologies.

I know which floorboards complain.
I know which windows lie.

I know how to keep a place
from looking abandoned.

That does not mean
I am still inside.

[Verse 3 – slower, more melodic]

If you made it through the entrance
if you stepped across the dust
if you found the hidden handle
and ignored the smell of rust

If you called my name kindly
down the passage, down the stair
you might hear something answer
but it wouldn’t mean I’m there

Just an echo with my accent
just a draft that learned my tone
just the house remembering
how to sound less alone

I have gone where guests don’t follow
where the mirrors face the wall
where the rooms are only rooms
and no one decorates the fall

[Final Refrain – warm but hollow]

I moved back
room by room

past the names
past the wound

If you came in
looking through

you might find
no one to lose

I moved back
room by room

kept the house
kept the gloom

Still the foyer
waits for you

but the ghost
has left there too

[Outro – whispered | piano fading | clocks remain]

So please—

admire the hallway.

Notice the flowers.

Say the place feels lived in.

That was always
the talent.

Leaving just enough of myself
near the entrance

that nobody noticed

I was gone.
