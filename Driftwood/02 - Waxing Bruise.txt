[TITLE: Waxing Bruise]
[GENRE: Melancholic Indietronica / Brooding Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Lonely, salt-worn, intimate, restless, slow-burning, quietly desperate]
[TEMPO: 84 BPM]
[KEY: D minor]
[VOCALS: Close low-register verses, restrained and nearly spoken, Fragile melodic lift in choruses, Thin doubled phrases on repeated lines, Breath left audible before the break, Distant harmony fragments after the final chorus]
[PRODUCTION: Sparse electronic pulse like a weak signal, Muted kick beneath frayed percussion, Low synth bass with a slow undertow, Single piano notes left to decay, Weathered tape texture on transitions, Dropouts before key lines, Reverb tails shaped like open water]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Hold the shore… count the days…
Tie the dark to another name
Salt in the cup… rope in the hand…
Nothing answers from the sand

[Verse 1]
I woke where the anchor let me go
Face down where the small reeds grow
No chain to bless, no chain to blame
Just my own mouth saying my name

The island kept a narrow room
Of crooked palms and afternoon
A ring of shells, a weathered pail
A prayer scratched thin with a broken nail

I made a fire from fallen bark
Fed it pieces of the dark
Watched the smoke stand up and bend
Like it remembered having friends

[Pre-Chorus]
Out past the reef, a lantern moved
A quiet proof I was not removed
I raised both arms, I split my throat
The light kept walking with the boat

[Chorus]
They pass, they pass, they never turn
Their windows catch the signal burn
I drink the tide and spit it back
Still taste the anchor in the lack

No voice, no hand, no shoreward line
No name returned from the maritime
Only gull marks, ash, and skin
The moon had a bruise where the morning got in

[Verse 2]
By the third week I spoke to stones
Set them near me, called them home
One for hunger, one for sleep
One for promises I could not keep

Some nights a ship would cross the rim
A moving town, all lit within
I learned the shape of almost saved
How close can come and still behave

I waved a shirt until it tore
Beat a pan against the shore
The hulls slid on through silver rain
Too far to hear, too near for sane

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't shout, the empty place
It learns the outline of your face
Til what you miss and what you are
Share one shadow under one star

[Chorus]
They pass, they pass, they never turn
Their windows catch the signal burn
I drink the tide and spit it back
Still taste the anchor in the lack

No voice, no hand, no shoreward line
No name returned from the maritime
Only gull marks, ash, and skin
The moon had a bruise where the morning got in

[Bridge]
I saved the last dry plank for proof
Kept it hidden under the roof
Told myself, not yet, not now
Somebody has to learn this sound

But hope gets small when left unfed
A splintered thing beside the bed
I took the rope, the pail, the door
The shirt that couldn't wave anymore

[Break]
So I built it badly, board by board
From every promise I could afford
A ragged raft, a crooked prayer
The last bit of hope left anywhere

I pushed it out through the biting foam
No sail, no map, no word for home
If the sea had kept my weight before
Let it carry what the island wore

[Final Chorus]
They pass, they pass, but I won't stay
To count their lights across the bay
I drink the tide and give it name
Not cure, not anchor, not the same

No voice, no hand, no shoreward line
No blessed turn, no clean design
Only rope burn, breath, and skin
The moon had a bruise where the morning got in

[Outro]
Hold the shore… let it go…
Something pulling from below
No one saw, and no one came
Still I moved against the frame

Salt in the mouth… wood in the hand…
The island shrinking into sand
I left the fire to learn the rain
And took the thirst out past its chain
