[TITLE: Barometer Teeth]
[GENRE: Melancholic Indietronica / Brooding Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Braced, sea-worn, anxious, watchful, slowly gathering force]
[TEMPO: 86 BPM]
[KEY: C minor]
[VOCALS: Close low-register verses, clipped and intimate, Thin melodic lift in choruses, Breath audible before storm images, Distant doubled phrases on "take the reading" and "not yet", Final chorus strained but controlled]
[PRODUCTION: Muted electronic pulse under slow sub bass, Sparse piano taps like water on tin, Frayed percussion with soft rim clicks, Low weather-bed swelling under choruses, Sudden dropouts before exposed lines, Reverb shaped like a cabin filling with pressure]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Count the glass... count the drop...
Watch the needle learn to nod
Rope on the rail... teeth in the gale...
Something patient in the fog

[Verse 1]
The raft held longer than it should
Wrong nails, old shirt, half-good wood
I left the island breathing black
And felt it watching from the back

The first night kept a sailor's grin
A cold lip tucked beneath my chin
No stars to barter, no clean chart
Just plank and pulse and stubborn heart

I heard the pail roll end to end
Like it had news it would not send
The rope went stiff, the boards complained
The weather took my shape and stayed

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't arrive, it gathers weight
It learns the hinges, tests the gate
The quiet cup, the lowered cloud
The hush before the mast gets bowed

[Chorus]
Take the reading, take it slow
Pressure falling in the bones
Every breath a borrowed berth
Every wave rehearsed by worse

I keep my mouth above the swell
Hold my nerve and ring no bell
If the sky has teeth tonight
Let it find me still upright

[Verse 2]
By dawn the sea had lost its floor
No island left to answer for
The gulls were gone, the foam was hard
My hands had mapped the splintered yard

I thought of cures that pulled me down
Of thirst dressed up in a cleaner gown
I thought of ships that would not turn
Their windows carrying their own concern

Now nothing passed, and nothing waved
No almost-saved, no almost-brave
Just me, the boards, the weather's jaw
And all the knots I never saw

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't beg, the coming squall
It takes attendance from us all
Til what we fear and what we feed
Share one bunk beneath the keel

[Chorus]
Take the reading, take it slow
Pressure falling in the bones
Every breath a borrowed berth
Every wave rehearsed by worse

I keep my mouth above the swell
Hold my nerve and ring no bell
If the sky has teeth tonight
Let it find me still upright

[Bridge]
I used to call the drag a shackle
Then called the leaving borrowed tackle
But out here names go overboard
And wanting wears a sailor's cord

If I survive the lowering glass
It won't be proof the storm has passed
Just proof a crooked little craft
Can keep insisting from the aft

[Break]
(whispered)
Not yet... not under...
Not yet... not water...

[Outro]
Count the glass... count the drop...
Tie the pail before it knocks
Rope on the rail... breath in the gale...
I am not done being small
