[TITLE: Bilge Prayer]
[GENRE: Melancholic Indietronica / Brooding Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Claustrophobic, patient, intimate, exhausted, quietly stubborn]
[TEMPO: 82 BPM]
[KEY: G minor]
[VOCALS: Low close-mic verses, nearly spoken and salt-dry, Fragile chorus with narrow harmonies, Whispered doubles beneath repeated "bail it out", Final refrain thinner and more human]
[PRODUCTION: Slow muted kick like a boot in a flooded hull, Dull wooden percussion, Warm sub bass with undertow, Sparse felt piano, Wet room reverb, Small creaks and rope strain tucked low, Arrangement thins whenever the water rises]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
In the belly... under boards...
Small cold hands beneath the floor
Cup by cup... breath by breath...
Keep the bottom from the rest

[Verse 1]
The storm did not break me clean
It left me working in between
A raft can live and still take in
The sea it swore it had outgrown

I found the leak by candle stub
A silver mouth behind the tub
Not wide enough to call it doom
Just wide enough to fill the room

So I knelt where nobody sees
Past the rope and patched-up knees
And scooped the dark from rib to pail
While rain wrote letters on the sail

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't flood you all at once
It learns your hours, ounce by ounce
It waits until your hands are tired
Then asks again to be carried

[Chorus]
Bail it out, don't bless the leak
Don't make a shrine of what is weak
Cup by cup, the hull replies
I am still here, I have not capsized

Bail it out, no holy phrase
No clean excuse, no better haze
Just two hands and stubborn breath
Keeping water from the bed

[Verse 2]
I named one plank for appetite
One for getting through the watches
One for every almost gesture
Sent from miles of busy beaches

The pail grew heavy with old hours
Island ash and ruined flowers
A shirt that failed to flag a crew
A door I broke to make it through

I almost laughed, and then I did
A cracked thing in the cabin lid
Because the sea is large and mean
But so is mending when unseen

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't heal because you know
The place where all the waters go
But naming where the breach begins
Can keep the whole from giving in

[Chorus]
Bail it out, don't bless the leak
Don't make a shrine of what is weak
Cup by cup, the hull replies
I am still here, I have not capsized

Bail it out, no holy phrase
No clean excuse, no better haze
Just two hands and stubborn breath
Keeping water from the bed

[Bridge]
There is no witness in the hold
No bell, no flag, no bright reward
Only the work the morning finds
When it counts what you declined

And if a ship should pass nearby
Let it see a stubborn eye
Not a rescue, not a wreck
Just someone braced below the deck

[Break]
(whispered)
Cup by cup...
Don't stop...
Not yet...

[Outro]
In the belly... under boards...
Small cold hands beneath the floor
If the raft survives the weather
It will be because I stayed
