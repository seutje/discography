[TITLE: The Cartographer Lied]
[GENRE: Melancholic Indietronica / Brooding Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Disoriented, betrayed, reflective, slow-burning, sharpening into resolve]
[TEMPO: 88 BPM]
[KEY: A minor]
[VOCALS: Intimate low-register verses, Dry and restrained, Chorus opens into wounded melody, Distant harmonies on "paper coast", Spoken bridge with close breath and no drums]
[PRODUCTION: Sparse electronic beat with soft drag, Low synth bass rolling like a far hull, Detuned piano figures, Paper-rustle percussion, Wide reverb on chorus tails, Subtle tape wobble during map imagery, Final section steadier and less murky]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Fold the chart... fold it wrong...
Every crease remembers a song
Ink in the rain... thumb on the mark...
North went missing after dark

[Verse 1]
I kept a map beneath my coat
Salt-stained, thin, and over-read
Every route had careful ink
Every danger neatly said

It promised coves with softer floors
Fresh rope coiled near quiet stores
A harbor drawn with little birds
And welcome written without words

But paper loves a tidy coast
It leaves out hunger, thirst, and ghosts
It marks the rocks, but not the hour
Your own two hands forget their power

[Pre-Chorus]
The compass trembled, then refused
The pencil broke, the margins bruised
And every line I thought was law
Was just a wish somebody saw

[Chorus]
The cartographer lied to me
No safe blue where the reef should be
No sheltered mouth, no gentle bend
No marked return, no waiting friend

The cartographer lied, but still
I kept the oar against my will
If no one drew this stretch of foam
Then I will learn it by the bone

[Verse 2]
I passed the place the ships had crossed
Where almost saved became a trade
I found the smoke I left behind
Still practicing the shape it made

I thought a route could make me clean
A dotted mercy in between
But no drawn arrow tells the truth
Of what you carry from the roof

So I tore the corner from the chart
Wrapped it tight around my heart
Not for faith and not for proof
Just to keep the weather out

[Pre-Chorus 2]
The sea has grammar paper lacks
It changes tense behind your back
Til where you are and where you've been
Both ask to be forgiven

[Chorus]
The cartographer lied to me
No safe blue where the reef should be
No sheltered mouth, no gentle bend
No marked return, no waiting friend

The cartographer lied, but still
I kept the oar against my will
If no one drew this stretch of foam
Then I will learn it by the bone

[Bridge]
Maybe no one meant the harm
Maybe every chart is poor
Maybe those who made the marks
Never had to leave the shore

But I did
And I made the crossing
With wet sleeves
And no clear north

[Break]
So I mark the shallows with my scars
Leave small warnings under stars
Not to rule the water's will
Only to tell the next one: still.

[Outro]
Fold the chart... let it go...
Ink runs honest when it flows
North went missing, so I steer
By what keeps moving under fear
