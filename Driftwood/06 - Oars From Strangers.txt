[TITLE: Oars From Strangers]
[GENRE: Melancholic Indietronica / Brooding Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Hushed, sea-worn, wary, intimate, cautiously held, slow-burning]
[TEMPO: 85 BPM]
[KEY: D minor]
[VOCALS: Close low-register verses, restrained and almost spoken, Fragile melodic chorus with thin doubled phrases, Distant harmony fragments answering from the edges, Breath left exposed before repeated lines, Final chorus warmer but still unsettled]
[PRODUCTION: Subdued electronic pulse like oars dipping out of time, Low synth bass with a slow undertow, Muted wooden percussion, Sparse piano notes left to decay, Weathered tape texture on transitions, Dropouts before key images, Wide tidal reverb that makes the backing voices feel half-seen]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Knock on water... not on wood...
Something moving where nothing should
Fog in the throat... rope in the teeth...
Hands I can't see pulling beneath

[Verse 1]
The morning came without a face
A grey cloth over every place
No gull to mark the lifted air
No mast to prove the world held shape

I had rowed until the oar went thin
Until my shoulder folded in
The raft kept speaking through its seams
In little warnings, little pleas

Then out beyond the broken bow
Another blade disturbed the flow
Not close enough to call a crew
Too close to call the water empty

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't save you like a hymn
It enters at the outer rim
A pull you feel before you see
The hand that makes it possible

[Chorus]
Oars from strangers, dark and slow
Taking turns below the fog
I do not know the names they keep
Only how the timbers ease

Oars from strangers, pull and breathe
Not a rescue, not release
Just another pull in rhythm
Moving with the pull I carry

[Verse 2]
A skiff emerged by half degrees
First the rope, then woolen sleeves
Two bent figures, salt and bone
A child quiet as a stone

No one asked me where I'd failed
No one blessed the ragged sail
One hand reached and set a cup
Where the bilge kept rising up

The old one tied my ruined stern
With knots that did not need my word
The child watched the island ash
Fall from my coat into the wash

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't break the empty spell
It rows beside the private swell
Til solitude, that narrow god
Learns the rhythm of another hull

[Chorus]
Oars from strangers, dark and slow
Taking turns below the fog
I do not know the names they keep
Only how the timbers ease

Oars from strangers, pull and breathe
Not a rescue, not release
Just another pull in rhythm
Moving with the pull I carry

[Bridge]
I had made a room of open sea
Called it truth because it answered me
Every wave returned my shape
Every absence held its place

But someone else can hold an oar
Without becoming what you're for
Someone else can share the strain
And leave your sorrow unclaimed

[Break]
(whispered)
Pull... wait...
Dip... rise...
Do not vanish...

[Final Chorus]
Oars from strangers, dark and slow
Taking turns below the fog
I do not know the names they keep
Only how the timbers ease

Oars from strangers, pull and breathe
Not a rescue, not release
Just another pull in rhythm
Moving with the pull I carry

[Outro]
Knock on water... not on wood...
The raft still broken, but understood
Fog in the throat... rope in the teeth...
More than my own hands underneath
