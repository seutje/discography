[TITLE: The Sounding Line]
[GENRE: Melancholic Indietronica / Brooding Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Focused, intimate, investigative, wary, quietly cathartic]
[TEMPO: 84 BPM]
[KEY: E minor]
[VOCALS: Close low-register verses, Measured and almost whispered, Chorus opens with fragile layered harmonies, Repeated phrases tucked low like crew replies, Bridge nearly a cappella]
[PRODUCTION: Subdued pulse with soft kick, Low synth bass like depth under timber, Plucked muted notes resembling rope against wood, Sparse piano, Long reverb below short dry vocal phrases, Percussion drops out for depth measurements]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Drop the line... let it sink...
Knuckles white around the brink
Lead and twine... mark by mark...
Find the bottom in the dark

[Verse 1]
The fishers taught me depth by touch
Wet knots keeping careful count
No grand device, no silver face
Just touch and patience keeping place

I let it slip through thumb and palm
Felt the sea go cold and calm
Every fathom took a thread
From things I thought were better dead

There was the island, there the pail
There the shirt that failed as sail
There the mouth that called it need
While quietly teaching me to bleed

[Pre-Chorus]
Some depths answer when you ask
Some keep wearing their old mask
Some return a grain of sand
And ask what else your grip is hiding

[Chorus]
Sound it out, don't drag it up
Not every bottom wants a cup
Mark the depth and let it be
There are truths beneath the sea

Sound it out, then give it slack
Some things speak by not coming back
If I must cross what I don't see
Let my hands remember me

[Verse 2]
At twenty marks the water cooled
At thirty I stopped feeling fooled
At forty something in me knew
The old ache had an underside too

It wasn't just the anchor's pull
It wasn't only hunger's hull
It was the fear of open drift
Of having no excuse to lift

The line came back with clay and shell
Small proof from where the quiet settled
I held it like a rough command
Then rinsed the clay from every knuckle

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't vanish when it's named
It doesn't owe you any change
But what you measure without pride
Can stop deciding from inside

[Chorus]
Sound it out, don't drag it up
Not every bottom wants a cup
Mark the depth and let it be
There are truths beneath the sea

Sound it out, then give it slack
Some things speak by not coming back
If I must cross what I don't see
Let my hands remember me

[Bridge]
No cure, no anchor, no final word
I let the thirst become absurd
No blessed turn, no clean design
Just wet rope measured through my grip

And if I shake
Let me shake
If I learn
Let it take

[Break]
(whispered)
Mark by mark...
Not too fast...
Depth is not a verdict...

[Outro]
Drop the line... let it sink...
Leave some room around the brink
Lead and twine... palm and breath...
I can cross without a myth
