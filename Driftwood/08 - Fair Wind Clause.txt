[TITLE: Fair Wind Clause]
[GENRE: Warm Indietronica / Acoustic Folk / Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Brighter, salt-worn, relieved, communal, cautiously hopeful]
[TEMPO: 92 BPM]
[KEY: F major with minor verse color]
[VOCALS: Intimate verses with more air and lift, Warm melodic chorus, Soft group harmonies, Slight smile in delivery, Final refrain open and unguarded]
[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked guitar blended with soft electronic pulse, Brushed drums, Upright bass, Small piano figures, Gentle organ pad, Field texture of rigging and harbor bells kept low, Arrangement opens in the final third without becoming glossy]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Fair wind... if it comes...
Let it find the tired ones
Loose rope... open palm...
No one punished for the calm

[Verse 1]
By morning we had patched the seam
With sailcloth, jokes, and coffee steam
The child had named the battered bow
"Probably Fine" in careful strokes

We laughed because it might be true
The kind of truth that limps with you
Not brave enough to raise a flag
But strong enough to mend a bag

The old scarf dried along the rail
The fishers argued with the sail
The wind came shy, then came again
Like someone learning how to mend

[Pre-Chorus]
It doesn't have to roar to move
It doesn't have to ask for proof
A kinder weather, when it starts
Still has to pass through guarded hearts

[Chorus]
If the fair wind finds us
Let it find us as we are
Salt in every sleeve cuff
Splinters in the spar

If the fair wind carries
Let it carry without debt
Not every good thing coming
Needs a storm to earn it first

[Verse 2]
We shared the bread in uneven parts
One kept crust and one kept heart
Someone hummed a crooked tune
The deck kept time with heel and spoon

I thought of every bitter cup
Every tide I drank enough
And did not curse the younger me
For mistaking thirst for mercy

The sea stayed sea, the ropes stayed rough
The sky made no apology
But there was warmth along the planks
And I let myself receive

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't fix the years behind
It doesn't make the old ache kind
But joy can sit with soaked-through feet
And still be joy, and still be sweet

[Chorus]
If the fair wind finds us
Let it find us as we are
Salt in every sleeve cuff
Splinters in the spar

If the fair wind carries
Let it carry without debt
Not every good thing coming
Needs a storm to earn it first

[Bridge]
I kept waiting for the catch
The hidden reef, the closing hatch
But some days open without teeth
Some hands offer and then leave

No ledger on the cabin wall
No lesson carved beneath it all
Just weather turning from the west
And tired lungs doing their best

[Final Chorus]
If the fair wind finds us
Let it find us as we are
Salt in every sleeve cuff
Splinters in the spar

If the fair wind carries
Let it carry without debt
Not every good thing coming
Needs a storm to earn it first

[Outro]
Fair wind... if it stays...
Let it shorten bitter days
Loose rope... open palm...
No one punished for the calm
