[TITLE: Breakwater Names]
[GENRE: Melancholic Indietronica / Brooding Alternative Electronic]
[MOOD: Nearing land, uneasy, reflective, tender, afraid of arrival]
[TEMPO: 85 BPM]
[KEY: B minor]
[VOCALS: Close restrained verses, Fragile chorus with doubled low harmony, Spoken bridge exposed, Final chorus warmer but unresolved]
[PRODUCTION: Soft electronic pulse with slow bass undertow, Distant harbor bell texture, Sparse piano notes, Muted percussion like steps on wet stone, Reverb narrows in verses and opens at chorus, Final outro leaves only voice and low pad]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Stone by stone... tide by tide...
Something waits on the inside
Harbor mouth... guarded breath...
Land can frighten more than depth

[Verse 1]
We saw the breakwater at noon
A black arm under a silver spoon
The fishers cheered, the child stood tall
I felt my stomach start to fall

It should have looked like being spared
Like proof that someone somewhere cared
But every stone along the pier
Looked like a question at the threshold

What if the land asks who I am
Without the raft, without the jammed
And swollen boards beneath my feet
Without the sea to make me neat

[Pre-Chorus]
I learned the water's rough address
I learned the storm's old tenderness
But harbor has its own demand
It asks what you will do on land

[Chorus]
Breakwater, don't name me yet
I am not finished with the wet
Let me circle once, then twice
With salt still drying in my eyes

Breakwater, hold your stone
Do not promise me a throne
If I step down from this deck
Let me bring the wreckage back

[Verse 2]
The town was small, the gulls were loud
Laundry lifted from a crowd
Somebody rang a dinner bell
Like ordinary was a spell

I saw a window with warm bread
And nearly turned away instead
Because the hungry learn distrust
Of tables set without a catch

No, not catch.
No price.
Just fear
Looking for a rule to steer.

The old woman touched my sleeve
Said, "You can land and still leave."

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't end because you dock
The body still expects the knock
The hand still reaches for the rail
Long after weather leaves the sail

[Chorus]
Breakwater, don't name me yet
I am not finished with the wet
Let me circle once, then twice
With salt still drying in my eyes

Breakwater, hold your stone
Do not promise me a throne
If I step down from this deck
Let me bring the wreckage back

[Bridge]
I thought arrival meant a cure
A clean room and a closing door
But I have lived inside a wave
And learned what even shelter craves

So write no ending on the quay
Leave the rope untied for me
I can touch the harbor wall
Without becoming less at all

[Break]
(whispered)
Once around...
One more turn...
Let the landing learn...

[Outro]
Stone by stone... breath by breath...
Land can frighten more than depth
If I come in from the foam
Let it be as one who chose
