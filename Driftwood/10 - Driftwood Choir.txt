[TITLE: Driftwood Choir]
[GENRE: Warm Melancholic Indietronica / Ambient Folk Electronic]
[MOOD: Tender, weathered, communal, reflective, gently lifted, resolved without certainty]
[TEMPO: 90 BPM]
[KEY: F minor moving toward Ab major]
[VOCALS: Close intimate lead, Fragile melodic verses, Wider layered harmonies in choruses, Group doubles in final refrain, Spoken fragments tucked into the outro, Delivery soft but no longer defeated]
[PRODUCTION: Slow electronic pulse warmed by acoustic guitar, Upright bass, Sparse piano, Soft brushed percussion, Low organ pad, Subtle wood creaks and distant gulls, Reverb opens like a clear inlet, Final chorus adds layered voices while leaving space around the lead]
[LYRICS]
[Intro]
Wood on wood... palm to palm...
Pieces gathered from the strand
Salt in grain... weathered through...
Something broken learning tune

[Verse 1]
We carried every plank ashore
The raft, the pail, the stubborn door
The shirt that would not wave enough
The rope that taught my palms to rough

We laid them out beside the quay
Not as proof, not property
Just things that crossed and did not sink
Laid where other hands could think

The child tapped rhythm on the boards
The fishers found a minor chord
Somebody brought a cracked canteen
And rinsed the sand from what had been

[Pre-Chorus]
Nothing holy, nothing grand
Only wreckage, breath, and hands
But even wood the sea has chewed
Can hold a room when it is used

[Chorus]
Sing, driftwood choir
Not for rescue, not for glory
Sing the crossed-out, carried-through
Every plank that made it too

Sing, salt-worn chorus
Low and rough, but less divided
Not made whole and not made new
Just less alone in what is true

[Verse 2]
I kept the anchor in the song
Not on my ankle, not for long
Its rust had fed the oldest part
That thought a weight could be a heart

I kept the moon with its old bruise
Not as omen, not as news
Just as a mark above the bay
Where morning entered anyway

I kept the map with ruined ink
Pinned above the kitchen sink
So anyone who loses north
Can see a route is not the worth

[Pre-Chorus 2]
It doesn't end in being healed
No final bell across the field
But there are meals and there are names
Spoken gently after weather

[Chorus]
Sing, driftwood choir
Not for rescue, not for glory
Sing the crossed-out, carried-through
Every plank that made it too

Sing, salt-worn chorus
Low and rough, but less divided
Not made whole and not made new
Just less alone in what is true

[Bridge]
I used to drink the sea for ache
Call each thirst a small mistake
Now I leave a cup outside
For the next one off the tide

No clean harbor, no perfect chart
No weather kind enough to start
But hands can gather what remains
And make a shelter out of grain

[Break]
(soft group voices)
Board by board...
Breath by breath...
Room for what survived the depth...

[Final Chorus]
Sing, driftwood choir
Not for rescue, not for glory
Sing the crossed-out, carried-through
Every plank that made it too

Sing, salt-worn chorus
Low and rough, but less divided
Not made whole and not made new
Just less alone in what is true

[Outro]
Wood on wood... palm to palm...
Pieces drying on the strand
If the sea calls back someday
I will know the leaving way

Salt in grain... weathered through...
Something broken learned a tune
