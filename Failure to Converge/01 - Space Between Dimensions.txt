[TITLE: Space Between Dimensions]
[GENRE: Glitchy Indietronica / Experimental Electronic / Abstract Pop]

[MOOD: Curious, slightly ironic, fragmented → expansive, awe-filled, quietly defiant]

[TEMPO: 96 BPM (with micro-variations, occasional stutters and half-time drops)]

[KEY: F# minor (with modal shifts and dissonant textures)]
