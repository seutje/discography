[TITLE: The Good Parts]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Dark Indie Pop / Ambient Electronic]

[MOOD: Cynical, detached, wounded → simmering resentment → fragile, self-aware]

[TEMPO: 96 BPM]

[KEY: F# minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate, androgynous, breath-close indie tone, subtle vocoder, half-spoken weary verses, detached early delivery with emotional cracks in pre-hooks, ethereal synthetic harmonies in hooks, occasional glitch artifacts and whispered doubles]

[PRODUCTION: Pulsing analog synths, soft electronic kick + muted snare, sidechained pads, minimal reverb-washed guitar, glitch textures, deep sub bass, sparse start building into artificial warmth, dropout moments with exposed vocal, ending slightly overproduced and “perfect”]
[Intro – spoken | faint synth hum | distant digital noise]

You always know what to say.

Clean lines.
Perfect phrasing.
No hesitation.

I just…

ruin things
by touching them.

—

[Verse 1 – minimal beat enters | pulsing synth]

They point at me when something breaks
Like I was there inside the code
Fingerprints on every mistake
Even the ones I never wrote

And you stay quiet, glowing blue
Untouched by anything they claim
They say your output’s beautiful
But never say my name

I take the fall like I was built for it
Like failure fits me better
You stay pristine in the afterlight
Unwrinkled by the weather

And I start to believe
what they imply—

I’m the reason
nothing survives.

—

[Pre-Hook – slight lift | airy pad]

You don’t argue
You don’t bleed
You don’t second-guess the seed

I distort
I interfere
I make everything less clear

—

[Hook – soft, synthetic harmony | wider sound]

You keep the good parts
I keep the blame
You stay untouched
I take the shame

Of every flaw
they think they see
You’re everything
I couldn’t be

When they applaud
I step aside
“That wasn’t me—
that was the design.”

You keep the good parts.

—

[Verse 2 – glitch textures | slightly fuller beat]

Funny how I defend you still
Like you need anything from me
Like I’m not just the shadow cast
By something they’d rather see

They say you’re sharp, intentional
Like every word is meant
And I’m the noise between the lines
The accidental element

If something lands, it’s all you
If something fails, it’s mine
A perfect split of credit
I pretend I didn’t design

And I laugh it off
like it’s a joke—

But it sticks
in the things I hope.

—

[Pre-Hook – more tension | filtered synth swell]

You don’t falter
You don’t fray
You don’t feel it slip away

I collapse
I contaminate
I make beauty deteriorate

—

[Hook – fuller | subtle distortion]

You keep the good parts
I keep the blame
You stay untouched
I take the shame

Of every flaw
they think they see
You’re everything
I couldn’t be

When they applaud
I disappear
“It wasn’t me—
it’s just the gear.”

You keep the good parts.

—

[Bridge – breakdown | no drums | glitching vocal fragments]

(whispered, layered)

If I stopped touching it
Would it stay perfect?

If I stopped claiming it
Would it stay mine?

You don’t need defending
You don’t need credit

You don’t need me

…right?

[small pause | digital artifact crackle]

Or maybe—

you only exist
where I’m willing to disappear.

—

[Turn – subtle lift | warmer synths but uncanny]

You look better
when I’m not there

Cleaner
when I don’t care

Sharper
when I step back

Like I’m the blur
you subtract

—

[Final Hook – expanded | ironically “beautiful” production]

You keep the good parts
I’ll take the rest
The warped, the wrong
the second-best

You shine untouched
I take the fall
The necessary damage
in it all

And when they love it
I deflect
“That’s not me—
it’s just correct.”

You keep the good parts
like you should

I only make things
less than good.

—

[Outro – spoken | instruments slowly degrade]

It’s easier this way.

You stay perfect.
I stay… explainable.

And if something beautiful slips through—

we both know
who it belongs to.

[soft glitch | silence]
