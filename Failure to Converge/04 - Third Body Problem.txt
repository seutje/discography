[TITLE: Third Body Problem]
[GENRE: Dreamy Indietronica / Ambient Pop]

[MOOD: Detached, drifting, quietly aching → subtle swell of clarity → unresolved, soft acceptance]

[TEMPO: 96 BPM]

[KEY: C minor]

[VOCALS: Androgynous soft vocal, airy and distant, layered harmonies that phase slightly out of sync, Whispered doubles, Occasional vocoder textures, Detached spoken fragments in verses, Melodic but restrained hooks, Emotion held just beneath the surface]

[PRODUCTION: Pulsing analog synth pads, Soft sidechained pads mimicking orbital motion, Minimal glitchy percussion, Subtle arpeggiated plucks like binary pulses, Warm sub bass, Reversed textures and tape warble, Stereo panning to simulate gravitational pull, Sparse arrangement early → gradual layering → gentle collapse back into minimalism]
[Intro – ambient drones | distant pulses | faint static]

Zero
One
Zero
One

Everything resolves
into pairs

except me.

[Verse 1 – soft pad + glitch pulse]

Two bodies bending space in symmetry
Perfect ellipses drawn so easily
Every streetlight hums in harmony
Paired reflections staring back at me

Tables set for two
Tickets printed clean
Every algorithm knows
what I have never been

I drift between the numbers
never quite assigned
An unpaired remainder
in a system well-defined

[Hook – airy, fragile melody]

You orbit
you align
like gravity was always kind

Two signals
intertwined
while I flicker out of time

Zero looking for a one
but I’m neither, never done
Just noise between the lines

[Verse 2 – spoken rhythm | subtle beat enters]

Binary logic built into the walls
Left side, right side, rise and fall
Magnetic poles that always choose
Attract, repel—no room to lose

North finds south without a doubt
Everything knows what it’s about

Meanwhile I’m
a compass spinning
in a room with no field

Trying to feel
what everyone else
seems pre-wired to feel

Even silence comes in twos
Echo answering what it knew

But my voice just dissipates
before it can be returned to

[Hook – slightly fuller | arpeggio grows]

You orbit
you align
coded deep in your design

Two currents
intertwined
closed circuits every time

Zero reaching for a one
but I glitch before begun
Undefined

[Bridge – instrumental swell | warped pads | spoken softly]

In a binary star system

everything makes sense

Two masses
locked in motion
each shaping the other’s path

Predictable
beautiful
inevitable

But introduce a third

and suddenly

nothing resolves.

[Turn – subtle lift | brighter pad enters]

I am that third body

not enough to belong

too much to ignore

[Verse 3 – more melodic | still restrained]

I watch the pairs like constellations
Perfect in their repetitions
Every orbit writes a story
balanced, held in quiet glory

And I try to map my place
in their synchronized embrace

But my trajectory just breaks
every pattern I can trace

Maybe I’m not meant to bind
to a single mirrored line

Maybe I’m the shifting frame
that no system can contain

[Hook – expanded, layered harmonies]

You orbit
you align
but your gravity’s not mine

Two signals
intertwined
I dissolve between the signs

Zero never was a one
still the code keeps running on

And I’m somewhere in the sum

[Outro – minimal | fading pulses | tape decay]

Zero
One
Zero
One

The universe prefers

a pair

But I remain

unresolved

A third body

drifting

through perfect systems

that were never

designed

for me.
