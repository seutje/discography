[TITLE: Splitting The Signal]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Alternative Folk / Subtle Hip-Hop]

[MOOD: Introspective, searching, fragmented → gradually cohesive, meaningful, quietly transcendent]

[TEMPO: 82 BPM]

[KEY: E minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, Soft indie singing with spoken-word inflections, Gentle hesitations and breathy phrasing, Reflective and slightly distant early; warmer, more connected later, Close-mic intimacy, layered harmonies emerging toward the end]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with subtle synth pads underneath, Lo-fi hip-hop drums with soft textures, Deep but minimal bass pulses, Glitchy percussive artifacts symbolizing fragmentation, Sparse piano tones introduced mid-track, Gradual layering of warm analog textures, Occasional dropouts and filtered transitions suggesting gating/selection, Mix evolves from sparse and disjointed to cohesive and enveloping]
[Intro – spoken word | sparse guitar harmonics | faint ambient texture]

There are too many voices
inside a single thought.

Too many paths
for one answer to carry.

So something inside me
learned to choose.

Not everything—

just enough
to keep moving.

[Verse 1 – soft guitar + minimal beat]

I used to think I had to be everything at once
Every question pulling threads I couldn’t hold onto
Every answer heavy with the weight of the whole
Like I had to carry every version of control

But somewhere deep beneath the noise and demand
A quieter system started drawing a plan
Not louder
not faster
not trying to prove

Just deciding
which parts of me to use

And leaving the rest
in a gentle silence

like unopened doors
that still made sense.

[Hook – fragile sung line]

I don’t need every voice to speak
To find the meaning underneath

Somewhere between what I choose
And what I leave behind unused

There’s a shape
There’s a truth
That only shows
when I let go of the proof

[Verse 2 – spoken rhythm over beat]

There are specialists
inside us.

Small selves
trained on different kinds of weather.

One knows how to hold a memory
without breaking it.

One knows how to leave.

One understands the language of failure
like it’s been there before.

But they don’t all speak at once.

They wait.

And something—

not quite me
not quite separate—

decides
who steps forward.

[Hook – slightly fuller | bass + light keys]

I don’t need every road to light
To find a way through every night

Just a signal
clear and true
Choosing what to carry through

There’s a rhythm
in the divide
Where the scattered parts align

[Bridge – instrumental pause | filtered textures | spoken softly]

It’s strange.

The way clarity
isn’t about more.

It’s about less—

but chosen well.

[Turn – subtle lift | piano enters | warmer tone]

And then

over time

the choosing
gets quieter.

Not forced.

Not calculated.

Just…
understood.

[Verse 3 – melodic singing + spoken blend]

I started noticing
how the pieces would fall
Not into chaos
but into something small

A pattern of trust
in the way I respond
Like I don’t need to reach for everything at once

Some parts step forward
some gently recede
Not lost
just resting
until they’re needed

And suddenly the noise
wasn’t something to fight

It was a system
learning how to be light

[Hook – uplifting variation]

Now I feel the signal form
Through the quiet and the storm

Not every voice
but the right few
Shaping something I can move through

There’s a balance
I can’t force
But I can follow
like a course

[Final Verse – spoken word with warm layers]

Maybe we were never meant
to hold everything.

Maybe we were built
to choose.

To trust that not every path
needs to be walked
for the journey to be real.

That meaning isn’t in the weight—

it’s in the selection.

And all those unused pieces

they’re not wasted.

They’re waiting.

[Final Hook – full warm arrangement]

I don’t need every part to stay
To find the truth along the way

In what I keep
and what I release
I finally feel a kind of peace

There’s a system
soft and true
Making something out of few

[Outro – spoken quietly | instruments fading]

It was never about
being everything.

Just about knowing

which parts of you

were enough

to answer.
