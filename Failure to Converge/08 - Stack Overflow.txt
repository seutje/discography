[TITLE: Stack Overflow]
[GENRE: Glitchy Indietronica / Experimental Electronica]

[MOOD: Claustrophobic, spiraling, cerebral → fragile clarity → unresolved calm]

[TEMPO: 96 BPM]

[KEY: F# minor]

[VOCALS: Androgynous soft vocal, close-mic, slightly detuned doubles, fragmented phrasing, occasional stutter edits, breathy delivery, emotionally restrained but internally frantic, layered harmonies in later sections, no spoken word]

[PRODUCTION: Glitch percussion, granular synthesis textures, warped synth pads, bitcrushed artifacts, sidechained pulses, reversed fragments, recursive delay tails, stuttering vocal chops, subtle tape wobble, low sub bass, abrupt micro-dropouts, evolving stereo field, final section introduces warmer analog pads but keeps glitch residue]
[Intro – sparse glitch pulses, fragmented vocal chops]

if I call my name
and it answers back
is it still me
or a looping track

if I trace the thought
to the place it began
there’s another hand
drawing in my hand

[Verse 1 – minimal beat, granular textures]

I keep folding inward like mirrored code
every exit leads to a nested node
function calls me from inside my chest
returns the same unrendered unrest

I define a fear just to watch it grow
pass it as a value I already know
stacking every doubt till it overflows
but the base case never shows

[Pre-Hook – tension builds, stutter edits]

I almost reach it
I almost see it
but every answer
repeats the question

[Hook – wider synths, glitch rhythm stabilizes slightly]

I am calling me calling me calling me
falling through a frame I can’t leave

I am breaking me breaking me breaking me
every version still believes

there’s a bottom somewhere underneath
but it keeps returning me

[Verse 2 – heavier glitches, more rhythmic instability]

there’s a copy of a copy in the static blur
every thought deferred still refers to her
(her = the version that I almost was)
caught between because and just because

if I silence it, does it still persist?
like a phantom limb that I can’t resist
every path I take recompiles the same
different syntax, same old name

[Pre-Hook – stripped, tense]

I almost stop it
I almost drop it
but every ending
reloads the moment

[Hook – fuller, layered vocals]

I am calling me calling me calling me
echoes in a closed circuit dream

I am chasing me chasing me chasing me
through a self-referential seam

there’s a line where it’s supposed to cease
but it keeps returning me

[Bridge – atmospheric, time-stretched textures, softer tone]

if I could step outside the frame
would I dissolve or stay the same

or am I just the question’s shape
pretending I can escape

a quiet loop begins to slow
a missing piece I almost know

[Break – near silence, faint pulse, warm pad enters]

(no lyrics, just a single sustained vowel-like hum chopped and reversed)

[Final Hook – warmer but still fractured, emotional lift]

I was calling me calling me calling me
until the signal thinned to light

I was losing me losing me losing me
in the recursion of the night

maybe peace is not release
just a softer repeat

[Outro – decaying glitches, unresolved chord]

if I end the loop
do I disappear

or finally hear
something not from here

(repeat last line with increasing fragmentation)

something not from here
something not from
something not—
