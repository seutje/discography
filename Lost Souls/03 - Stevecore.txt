[TITLE: Stevecore]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word / Indie Electronica]

[MOOD: Sardonic, exasperated, dryly amused → reflective, warmer, quietly defiant]

[TEMPO: 88 BPM]

[KEY: D minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, close-mic spoken-word rap verses with conversational phrasing, sarcastic pauses, dry comic timing, soft indie-folk sung hook, starts teasing and irritated, becomes more thoughtful and human, slight rasp on punchlines, whispered doubles on asides, final hook more melodic and open]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with percussive muted strums, lo-fi hip-hop drums with soft kick, brushed snare, hand taps, warm upright-style bass, subtle indie-electronica pads underneath, tiny glitch textures between punchlines, sparse piano entering after second hook, full-stop dropouts before jokes, final third adds soft harmonies and warmer bass while staying raw and uncluttered]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken | dry, close-mic | sparse guitar]

Every day
somebody walks in holding a cup

and says,

“Guys.
I discovered
hot water.”

Then they pour it on the floor
and call it
Thermal Soulwave.

[Verse 1 – spoken rap | light guitar groove]

There’s a rule on the wall
that says tag what it is
So people know what they’re clicking
before they commit

Country ballad
darkwave
trap
folk punk
ambient

Useful little signs
for the tired and impatient

But someone shows up
with four-on-the-floor drums
a violin loop
and a bass drop chewing gum

Says,
“This is not EDM.”

Okay.

“What is it then?”

They say,
“Trekrift Fusion.”

And the room goes still
like a chef just announced
he invented the cucumber

I’m not saying don’t make it
I’m saying if it quacks
maybe tag it bird-adjacent
before you teach it to flap

Because someone came looking
for a country ballad
and got lasers in a tuxedo
fighting a fiddle in traffic

That’s not mystique.
That’s hiding the menu
behind your artist mission.

[Hook – soft sung folk melody]

You didn’t invent boiling water
You just found the kettle loud
You didn’t build a new horizon
You just named your favorite cloud

Call it yours in the margins
Call it strange, call it true
But don’t make me need a passport
just to know what I’m clicking through

[Post-Hook – spoken aside]

Also…
it’s EDM with violins.

That’s fine.

That’s legal.

[Verse 2 – spoken rap | drums enter]

Then there’s the guy
with the Japanese grunge
Like nobody ever put distortion
under another language once

Like Seattle had a customs office
Like sadness needs a visa
Like guitars turn back at the border
if the chorus isn’t in English

And I get it.

The first time you make something
that sounds like your head
you feel like a scientist
raising lightning from bread

You think,
“This is new.”

And maybe to you,
it is.

That matters.

But personal discovery
is not the same as invention

The first pancake
is not a cuisine

The first time you cry to synths
doesn’t make it Stevecore

Though I respect the ambition.

I, too,
have heard one minor chord
and considered founding a movement.

But a genre is not a hat
you put on a song
when you want it to feel taller

It’s a language
other people can use
without calling the author.

[Hook – fuller, warmer]

You didn’t invent boiling water
You just found the kettle loud
You didn’t build a new horizon
You just named your favorite cloud

Call it yours in the margins
Call it strange, call it true
But don’t make me need a passport
just to know what I’m clicking through

[Bridge – spoken | beat drops]

And look.

I’m not immune.

I’ve named things too early.

I’ve heard a weird texture
in the back of a track
and thought,

“Finally.

A genre for people
who overthink comment sections.”

Maybe all artists
want to be unrepeatable

Maybe all of us fear
that if the label fits too easily
then we disappear inside it

Acoustic trap.

Japanese grunge.

(verbal diarrhea)

Whatever.

Sometimes a name feels like a cage.

Sometimes it’s just a door sign.

And the door sign is not there
to shrink the room.

It’s there so strangers don’t walk in
expecting a bakery
and get punched by a fog machine.

[Verse 3 – warmer spoken rap | piano enters]

So put your secret name
in the liner notes

Put your private mythology
where the real ones know

Say,
“I call this Trekrift Fusion”
with your whole chest

But tag it like a neighbor
not a prophet with a quest

Say electronic.
Say violin.
Say dance.
Say weird.

Say grunge in Japanese
if that’s what people will hear

You can bend the genre
without breaking the map

You can be original
and still help people tap

Maybe the magic
isn’t in being first

Maybe it’s making the known thing
hurt in your particular verse

Maybe the point
isn’t to escape every shelf

Maybe it’s to leave a fingerprint
on something bigger than yourself

[Final Hook – warm, gently triumphant]

You didn’t invent boiling water
You just found the kettle loud
You didn’t build a new horizon
You just named your favorite cloud

Call it yours in the margins
Call it strange, call it true
But don’t make me need a passport
just to know what I’m clicking through

You didn’t invent boiling water
but you still made something steam
There’s room inside the old words
for a new and crooked dream

So call it yours in the margins
let the song be strange and true
Just put “EDM with violins”
where the people can see it too

[Outro – spoken | fading]

No hard feelings.

Invent the ocean
in your own bathtub.

Name every wave
after your cat.

But when the sign says “genre,”

maybe don’t write
“me.”

Some of us are just trying
to find a sandwich

and not get ambushed
by a violin drop.
