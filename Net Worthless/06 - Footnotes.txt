[TITLE: Footnotes]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Wry, tender, self-aware, quietly bruised → generous, grounded, hopeful]

[TEMPO: 87 BPM]

[KEY: E minor, lifting toward G major in final hook]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, slight British accent, Conversational spoken-word rap verses, dry comic timing, soft sung folk hook, close-mic phrasing, slight rasp on vulnerable lines, occasional whispered doubles, final chorus warmer with gentle harmonies]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with percussive folk-hop groove, soft kick, brushed snare, hand taps, warm upright bass, sparse piano after second hook, subtle organ pad in final third, roomy and human, beat drops before confessional lines, final section opens gently but stays intimate]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken | sparse guitar]
Funny thing.
Everybody wants
to be original.
Which is brave.
Considering most of us
learned language
from people
who were already talking.
Yeah.
I’ve been stealing fire
with receipts.

[Verse 1 – spoken rap | light guitar groove]
I used to want my name
carved deep in the door
big letters saying,
“I was here before.”
Not huge.
Not greedy.
Just, you know,
tastefully immortal.
A plaque would do.
Maybe brass.
Maybe somewhere
near the snacks.
Because I worked on the thing
till the thing worked back
till my eyes went dry
and my shoulders attacked
till the song had teeth
and the line had feet
till the tiny little engine
could cross the street.
And then someone says,
“Nice.”
Then moves on.
Three weeks of my life
became six seconds
and a thumb.
And I smiled like,
“Thank you.”
While inside
a small accountant
set fire to a chair.

[Pre-Hook – half-sung]
But I didn’t build this
from nothing at all
I borrowed the ladder
I borrowed the wall

[Hook – soft sung folk melody]
I’m standing on shoulders
and lending my bones
Making a step
out of all I own
Maybe they won’t know
what it cost me to climb
But if they see further
then the work did fine

[Post-Hook – spoken aside]
Would still accept applause.
I’m evolving,
not dead.

[Verse 2 – spoken rap | brushed snare enters]
Every song has ancestors
humming in the floor
Every painting has fingerprints
under the door
Every sentence I wrote
has a village behind it
Some teacher with patience
some poet with timing
some stranger online
who explained something twice
while being called an idiot
by a man named IceMantis.
Beautiful species.
Terrible comment sections.
I’ve taken little pieces
from a thousand kind hands
A rhythm from a busker
A wound from a friend
A phrase from my father
A joke from regret
A chord from a song
I pretended to forget
And none of it means
the making was hollow
It means I was fed
before I learned how to swallow
Means I found a path
because others wore ground
Means the map had names
even when I looked down.
So yes, put me in the footnotes.
Small print.
Back page.
Near the typo.
I’ll wave.

[Hook – fuller, warmer]
I’m standing on shoulders
and lending my bones
Making a step
out of all I own
Maybe they won’t know
what it cost me to climb
But if they see further
then the work did fine

[Bridge – beat drops | spoken, vulnerable]
And I won’t pretend
it doesn’t sting.
Of course it stings.
I’m not a monk.
I’m a tired little creature
with a password manager
and approval issues.
I want someone to notice
the hours.
The ugly drafts.
The line I cut
that deserved a small funeral.
The chorus I rebuilt
so many times
it started asking
for union rights.
I want them to know
I cared.
That I didn’t just toss it
into the world
like loose change.
I carried it.
Badly, sometimes.
With snacks.
But I carried it.
And still—
maybe love
is letting the thing go
without stapling
your whole face
to the front of it.
Maybe art is not always
a monument.
Sometimes it’s a bridge
that thanks you
by being crossed.

[Verse 3 – warmer spoken rap | piano enters lightly]
I like the idea
of the open door
the shared shelf
the “take this,
make more.”
A recipe passed on
with sauce on the page
a little note saying,
“Careful here,
this bit breaks.”
That feels honest.
That feels clean.
Not mine forever.
Just mine in between.
Like I found a cracked cup
and filled it with rain
then left it by the path
for whoever came.
And maybe some kid
with a second-hand mic
takes one little line
and makes something bright
Maybe they never know
my name or my doubt
Maybe they build a house
where I only marked out
one nail
one plank
one awkward suggestion
one badly-lit answer
to a private question.
And maybe that’s enough.
God, I hate how mature
that sounds.
Tragic.
Growth has terrible branding
Let the work travel
Let the name travel less
Let the heart leave a handle
for whoever comes next

[Final Hook – warm, layered, quietly triumphant]
I’m standing on shoulders
and lending my bones
Making a step
out of all I own
Maybe they won’t know
what it cost me to climb
But if they see further
then the work did fine
I’m not the first fire
I’m not the last flame
I’m one little match
in a long human chain
If I am forgotten
but the song still gets through
Then I was a doorway
and someone walked through

[Outro – spoken | instruments softening]
So no.
I didn’t make this
from nothing.
Nobody does.
I made it from everything
that made me.
From hands I never thanked.
From names I mispronounced.
From borrowed courage
and returned mistakes.
And maybe someday
someone will step on this
not cruelly
just naturally
on their way
to somewhere better.
And I hope it holds.
I hope it helps.
I hope they don’t look down
too long.
I hope they keep going.
Because maybe the point
was never
“Look at me.”
Maybe the point was
“Here.
Stand here.
You can see
a little further.”
