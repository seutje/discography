[TITLE: Feed Yourself]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Witty, weary, hopeful, culturally skeptical → warm, curious, gently defiant]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: D minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male close-mic vocal, spoken-word rap verses with dry comic timing and clipped pauses, half-sung folk hook, starts sardonic and observant, grows open and grateful, slight rasp on punchlines, whispered doubles on key phrases, final hook warmer and more communal]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with percussive busker groove, lo-fi hip-hop drums, brushed snare, palm taps, warm upright bass, sparse piano after second hook, room hiss, live imperfect feel, sharp dropouts before punchlines, final third adds soft harmonies while staying raw]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken | dry, close-mic]
Good news exists.
I know.
Suspicious, isn’t it?
Like finding a tenner
in last winter’s coat
and wondering
what tax office
you now owe.
But it’s out there.
You just can’t expect it
to arrive
between outrage
and discount trainers.

[Verse 1 – spoken rap]
The feed keeps serving
emotional meal deals:
two disasters,
one hot take,
and a drink
that tastes like dread.
And we keep chewing,
like,
“Well, it was placed before me.”
Mate.
That’s not dinner.
That’s a vending machine
having a breakdown
and charging you monthly.
You want kindness?
Leave the queue.
Look past the headline.
Past the sponsored panic.
Past the man in sunglasses
explaining collapse
from a rented car.
There’s a nurse
singing to someone’s nan.
A kid planting flowers
in a traffic island.
A stranger buying nappies
with a card
that barely survived the week.
No press release.
No profit forecast.
Just small mercy
working overtime
for less than it’s worth.

[Hook – soft sung folk melody]
You can’t just eat
what lands on your feed.
Not every cheap plate
has the thing that you need.
There’s gold in the gutter,
there’s bread in the rain,
but you’ve got to go looking
through the coupons of pain.
Good news is a market
with a hidden back door.
Kindness is expensive,
but there’s always some more.

[Verse 2 – spoken rap | drums enter]
Now the machines
are making songs,
paintings,
poems,
pictures of cats
in legal professions.
And yes,
some of it’s awful.
Some of it looks like
a motivational poster
got trapped in a microwave.
Some of it has
the emotional depth
of a hotel spoon.
But some of it
opens a door
for someone
never handed a key.
Some single dad
after the late shift
makes a lullaby
he couldn’t afford to record.
Some shy kid
builds a whole little world
from a bedroom
with bad curtains.
Some woman
once told
she wasn’t technical
makes the sky
do impossible things
before breakfast.
Is there slop?
God, yes.
Buckets of it.
But that doesn’t mean
the kitchen is worthless.
It means we learn
to taste better.
We learn to dig.
We learn to choose.
We learn the difference
between fast content
and food.

[Hook – fuller]
You can’t just eat
what lands on your feed.
Not every cheap plate
has the thing that you need.
There’s gold in the gutter,
there’s bread in the rain,
but you’ve got to go looking
through the coupons of pain.
Good news is a market
with a hidden back door.
Kindness is expensive,
but there’s always some more.

[Bridge – spoken | vulnerable]
And yes—
this song
would not exist
without Chet Jippedy.
Which is beautiful
and slightly annoying.
Like finding out
your therapist
is also a toaster
with opinions.
But I still had to ask.
Still had to listen.
Still had to cut the line
that sounded clever
but meant nothing.
Still had to hold
each little phrase
to the light
and check
if it had a pulse.

[Verse 3 – warmer | piano enters]
Maybe creativity
was never a mansion.
Maybe it was a stall
in the rain
with a handwritten sign.
Maybe now
more people can afford
a table.
That matters.
Even if some tables wobble.
Even if some soup is thin.
You don’t have to praise
every miracle-shaped product.
You don’t have to swallow
every shiny thing.
But don’t confuse
the landfill
for the whole field.
There are flowers here,
stubborn and underfunded.
There are songs
made by people
who finally found
a way in.
There are strangers
still feeding each other
from nearly empty plates.
So go looking.
Scroll less like a debtor.
Search more like a friend.
The world is not only
what gets promoted.
Sometimes the best thing
is three pages deep,
bad title,
heart intact.

[Final Hook – warm, gently defiant]
You can’t just eat
what lands on your feed.
Not every cheap plate
has the thing that you need.
There’s gold in the gutter,
there’s bread in the rain,
but you’ve got to go looking
through the coupons of pain.
Good news is a market
with a hidden back door.
Kindness is expensive,
but there’s always some more.
So bring your own hunger,
bring a bag,
bring a light.
There’s beauty on discount
if you search through the night.

[Outro – spoken | slight smile]
Good news exists.
Kindness exists.
Beauty exists.
But the algorithm
is not your mum.
It will not cut the crusts off
and check the temperature.
You have to feed yourself.
And sometimes,
after all the digging,
you find one small thing
made by a stranger
and your bankrupt little chest
declares
a surplus.
