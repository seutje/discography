[TITLE: Too Many Windows]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Witty, overloaded, lonely, hyper-verbal, self-mocking -> tender, accepting, quietly relieved]

[TEMPO: 89 BPM]

[KEY: E minor, lifting toward G major in the final hook]

[VOCALS: Intimate male close-mic vocal, spoken-word rap verses with conversational pacing and sudden fast overthinking bursts, dry British comic timing, soft half-sung folk hook, whispered doubles on "too many windows" and "come back to the room", final hook warmer with gentle harmonies]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with percussive folk-hop groove, lo-fi drums with soft kick, brushed snare and hand taps, warm upright bass, faint room hiss, small office and flat sounds tucked low in the mix: keyboard clacks, phone buzz, kettle click, distant traffic, hard dropouts before punchlines, sparse piano after second hook, final third opens with soft harmonies while staying raw and uncluttered]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - spoken | dry, close-mic | sparse guitar]

I opened my laptop
to answer one email.

Reasonable.
Adult.
Very brief.

Forty-seven minutes later
I was reading
public fountain history
in Prague

because someone wrote
"just circling back"

and my brain said,
Lovely.

Let's build a civilisation.

[Verse 1 - spoken rap | light guitar groove]

I've got too many windows
open in my skull

each one playing audio
at a tasteful volume.

There's a tab for rent
and a tab for shame

a tab where I prove
I'm not to blame

a tab where I rehearse
one joke from Tuesday

and another where I search
"is tired a personality?"

Thinking six thoughts
with no permission.

One says:
Text your friend back.

One says:
Invent a better tax system.

One says:
Remember that time
in 2009
you said "you too"
to a waiter
who told you
to enjoy your meal.

Important archive.

National shame.

I can see every angle
except the plain one

solve imaginary courts
before the case comes.

Call it intelligence
if you want the nice word.

Mostly it's a knife drawer
in a bouncy castle.

[Pre-Hook - half-sung]

I know too much
about things I can't change

I name every cloud
but still stand in the rain

[Hook - soft sung folk melody]

Too many windows
too much light

I can see the whole map
and still miss the right

Come back to the room
come back to the floor

The world is not asking
for one thought more

If I go quiet
I'm not leaving you

I'm sorting the noise
from the signal and blue

Too many windows
open at once

and one small heart
trying to answer the front

[Post-Hook - spoken aside]

Also,
if genius was the plan,

someone should have included
a better laundry module.

[Verse 2 - spoken rap | brushed snare enters]

People say,
"You think too much,"

like I do it for the aesthetic.

Like I wake up,
stretch,
and choose

premium anxiety
with unlimited tabs.

I would love
to have one thought.

One clean thought.

Like:
Toast.

Not:
Toast is evidence
of agricultural history,
childhood safety,
late capitalism,
and how I still don't own
a proper butter dish.

I'm at a party
by the coats again

performing normal
for some almost-friends.

They talk about holidays,
houses,
dogs.

I talk for three minutes
about train platform acoustics

then watch my sentence
land in the room

like a chair
thrown by a ghost.

Nobody's cruel.

That's the worst bit.

They're kind.

They nod.

They do that little smile
that says,

This person is a podcast
with shoes.

So I laugh first.

Make it tidy.

Turn the wound
into a party trick.

Say,
"Sorry,
brain's a bit loud today."

As if today
is the outlier.

Bless.

[Hook - fuller, warmer]

Too many windows
too much light

I can see the whole map
and still miss the right

Come back to the room
come back to the floor

The world is not asking
for one thought more

If I go quiet
I'm not leaving you

I'm sorting the noise
from the signal and blue

Too many windows
open at once

and one small heart
trying to answer the front

[Bridge - beat drops | spoken, vulnerable]

Here is the lonely part.

Not dramatic lonely.

No black coat.
No cliff.
No rain behaving symbolically.

Just the quiet little distance
between

what I mean

and what arrives.

I can explain a feeling
so precisely
it dies of paperwork.

I can build a palace
round a wound
then forget
to let anyone in.

I have frightened people
with the speed
of my caring.

I have corrected a detail
in a story

when the correct answer
was clearly

hold their hand,
you absolute spreadsheet.

And I am trying.

Truly.

I am trying
to be less brilliant
at being elsewhere.

Less fluent
in escape.

More present
in the stupid,
holy room

where someone says,
"You alright?"

and the miracle answer
is not
a diagram.

It's:

"Not really.
Can you sit with me?"

[Verse 3 - warmer spoken rap | piano enters lightly]

Maybe the mind
isn't a courtroom.

Maybe it's just
a crowded flat

with bad wiring
and good intentions.

Maybe not every thought
needs entered into evidence.

Maybe I can leave
some tabs unsolved.

Let Prague keep its fountains.

Let Tuesday keep its joke.

Let the old embarrassment
retire
by the coast.

Maybe clever
isn't always climbing.

Maybe clever
is noticing

the guitar in the corner,

the tea going cold,

the friend on the sofa
waiting through my weather.

Maybe love
is not impressed
by processing speed.

Maybe love
is the hand
that closes three windows

and says,

Look.

The room survived
without your supervision.

[Final Hook - warm, gently communal]

Too many windows
too much light

I can see the whole map
and still miss the right

Come back to the room
come back to the floor

The world is not asking
for one thought more

If I go quiet
just stay nearby

I'm learning the shape
of a simpler sky

Too many windows
but one open door

and one small heart
coming back to the floor

[Outro - spoken | instruments fading]

I answered the email,
by the way.

Eventually.

It said,
"Sounds good."

Two words.

Forty-seven minutes.

Honestly,

that's efficiency
if you include
the fountains.
