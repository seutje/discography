[TITLE: Asset Rich]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Witty, class-conscious, tired, quietly proud -> tender, defiant, grateful, resolved]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: D minor, lifting toward F major in the final hook]

[VOCALS: Intimate male close-mic vocal, spoken-word rap verses with dry British comic timing and clipped pauses, half-sung folk hook, slight rasp on vulnerable lines, whispered doubles on "asset rich" and "cash poor", final hook warmer and communal]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with percussive folk-hop groove, lo-fi drums with soft kick, brushed snare and hand taps, warm upright bass, faint room hiss, subtle till beep and coin sounds low in the mix, hard dropouts before punchlines, sparse piano after second hook, final third adds soft harmonies and fuller bass while staying raw and uncluttered]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - spoken | dry, close-mic | sparse guitar]

My bank app asked
if I wanted
financial insights.

Bold.

I said,
Mate,

I've got insights.

What I need
is money.

[Verse 1 - spoken rap | light guitar groove]

I'm asset rich
and cash poor,

which means I can explain
the entire machine

while standing in the kitchen
calculating cheese.

I've got skills
like emergency candles:

bright,
useful,
mostly used
when something has failed.

I know how to fix
three broken systems,

four sad people,

and one router
by threatening it gently.

I can write the email
that makes the room behave.

I can read the silence
in a meeting
and hear
who is about to quit.

I can turn a wound
into a chorus,

a panic attack
into a plan,

a cheap guitar
into a small republic
for the overdrawn.

And still,

the boiler blinks
like a moral question.

The rent arrives
with its little crown on.

The fridge contains
mustard,
light,

and the idea
of dinner.

[Pre-Hook - half-sung]

I count what I know
when the numbers don't land

gold in my head
and dust in my hand

[Hook - soft sung folk melody]

I'm asset rich,
cash poor,
still holding the door

Got a fortune in nerve
and a bill on the floor

Middle-class roof
with the weather coming through

I own what I learned
but the bank owns the view

If wisdom paid rent
I'd be hard to ignore

But I'm asset rich,
cash poor

[Post-Hook - spoken aside]

Diversified portfolio:

one useful brain,
three mugs,

and a cardigan
with leadership potential.

[Verse 2 - spoken rap | brushed snare enters]

They told us knowledge
was a ladder.

Climb it.
Earn it.
Be clever.

So I climbed
till my hands went numb,

got a nice little view
of everyone below me

also climbing,

also renting,

also one bad month
from becoming
a cautionary LinkedIn post.

I did the courses.
Read the books.

Learned the language
of rooms with glass walls.

Stakeholder.
Pipeline.
Resilience.

Tiny spell words
for people
who are tired
but laminated.

And yes,
I'm comfortable.

Comparatively.

I've got heating
most days,

a dentist I avoid
for emotional reasons,

and coffee
that costs enough
to have opinions.

So I won't pretend
I'm at the bottom.

I'm just close enough
to hear it breathing.

Close enough
to know

the floor
is not a floor.

It's a subscription.

[Hook - fuller, warmer]

I'm asset rich,
cash poor,
still holding the door

Got a fortune in nerve
and a bill on the floor

Middle-class roof
with the weather coming through

I own what I learned
but the bank owns the view

If wisdom paid rent
I'd be hard to ignore

But I'm asset rich,
cash poor

[Bridge - beat drops | spoken, vulnerable]

And the cruel joke is:

I do feel rich.

Not always.

Not when the car
makes a sound
like a bag of cutlery
being judged.

Not when the invoice
sits in my inbox
with its shoes on.

But sometimes.

When a friend says,
"How did you know
what I meant?"

When a line arrives
clean and true
after weeks
of scraping fog.

When I fix something
with patience
instead of panic.

When the small room
fills with people
who trust me enough
to be tired.

Then I feel rich.

Disgustingly rich.

Offshore account
in the soul.

Private island
of not giving up.

And no,
that does not pay
the council tax.

I have checked.

Repeatedly.

But it means
the ledger is incomplete.

It means the market
has terrible eyesight.

It means some forms
of wealth
refuse to behave
like wealth.

[Verse 3 - warmer spoken rap | piano enters lightly]

Maybe that's the trick
at the end of the album.

Not pretending
broke is holy.

Not calling pressure
character

because someone rich
needs the story tidy.

But refusing
to let the till
name every treasure.

I have been poor
in cash
and obscene
in questions.

Thin in savings,
heavy with songs.

One foot
in the middle class,

one foot
over the trapdoor,

both hands full
of things
I couldn't sell
without becoming
less alive.

So let the spreadsheet
shake its head.

Let the app
offer insights.

Let the landlord
call it passive income

like my pulse
isn't active expense.

I am still here.

Still learning.
Still useful.

Still making soup
from weather
and wire.

[Final Hook - warm, communal, gently triumphant]

I'm asset rich,
cash poor,
still holding the door

Got a fortune in nerve
and a bill on the floor

Middle-class roof
with the weather coming through

I own what I learned
but the bank owns the view

If wisdom paid rent
I'd be hard to ignore

But I'm asset rich,
cash poor

I'm debt-aware,
hope-sore,
still singing for more

Got a heart in the red
and a light in the store

If love had a wage
we'd all make it through

So I count what is left
and I share it with you

[Outro - spoken | instruments fading]

My bank app says
I'm spending too much
on groceries.

Fair.

But tonight
there are four people
at my table,

laughing
like the rent
can't hear us.

And for once,

the numbers
can wait outside.
