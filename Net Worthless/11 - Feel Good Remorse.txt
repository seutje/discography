[TITLE: Feel Good Remorse]

[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Guilty, wry, defensive, cautiously hopeful → warm, grounded, quietly defiant]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: E minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, Spoken-word rap verses with conversational phrasing, dry comic timing, and careful pauses, Soft indie-folk sung hook with fragile warmth, Starts apologetic and self-mocking, becomes more open and gently defiant, Close-mic verses, slight rasp on punchlines, occasional whispered doubles on repeated phrases, Final section more melodic and relieved]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar with a percussive busker groove, Lo-fi hip-hop drums with soft kick, brushed snare, and hand taps, Warm upright-style bass, Sparse piano enters after second hook, Subtle room tone for intimacy, Live imperfect close mix, Full-stop dropouts before punchlines, Final third adds soft harmonies and warmer bass, staying raw and uncluttered]

[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken | sparse guitar]

I have a confession.

Which is annoying,
because lately
I’ve been trying
not to turn every good day
into a courtroom.

But here it is:

I feel good.

Suspicious, I know.

[Verse 1 – spoken rap]

Ten years back
I was basically a weather warning
with shoes.

Low pressure in the hallway.
Heavy chances of not replying.
Small craft advisory
for anyone trying
to love me before noon.

I wore the end of the world
like a winter coat.
Buttoned it wrong.
Slept in it.
Called it realism.

And now?

Now I wake up
and make little things.

A tiny app for a friend
who forgets their groceries.

A dumb little game
where the frog is sad
but financially responsible.

A song like this,
which is cheaper than therapy
and less likely
to ask about my childhood
with a clipboard.

And somehow
that makes people nervous.

Like joy is only valid
if it arrives by hand,
covered in mud,
with references.

[Pre-Hook – half-sung]

I know the room is worried
I can hear the sirens hum
But I found a little doorway
where I used to find a wall

[Hook – soft sung]

Maybe it’s not all fire
Maybe it’s not all flood
Maybe I’m allowed to feel lighter
without betraying anyone

Maybe I can keep my eyes open
and still let the morning come
Maybe it’s not all ruin
Maybe some things help someone

[Post-Hook – spoken aside]

I said “maybe.”

Please lower the pitchfork
to a conversational angle.

[Verse 2 – spoken rap | drums enter]

Everybody keeps telling me
be careful.

Which is fair.

I am careful.

I check the label.
I check the lock.
I check the thing that checks the thing
then worry
the checker has hobbies.

I don’t trust a ladder
just because it reaches higher.

I test the rung.
I shake the frame.
I keep one hand
on ordinary doubt.

But there’s a difference
between caution
and throwing away the window
because sunlight
has historically been rude.

They say,
“It’ll make you lazy.”

Which is funny,
because I have learned more
in two strange years
than twenty respectable ones
where I sat politely
beside my own confusion
and called it discipline.

Now when I don’t understand something,
I can ask.
Badly.
Repeatedly.
At midnight.
With examples.
With follow-up questions
that would make a teacher
stare out the window
and reconsider cheese farming.

And yes,
sometimes the answer wears
a fake moustache.

So I check.

But sometimes
it opens a door
I didn’t know had hinges.

[Hook – fuller]

Maybe it’s not all fire
Maybe it’s not all flood
Maybe I’m allowed to feel lighter
without betraying anyone

Maybe I can keep my eyes open
and still let the morning come
Maybe it’s not all ruin
Maybe some things help someone

[Bridge – beat drops | vulnerable]

And yes—

I hear the warnings.

I hear the power bills.
The server farms.
The men in expensive jackets
promising the future
with very hydrated faces.

I hear the teachers tired.
The artists scared.
The workers asking
if the ladder is being pulled up
while everyone claps
for the elevator.

I hear it.

I’m not asking you
to stop worrying.

I’m asking
if there is room
inside the worry
for one ordinary person
who was drowning quietly

and found a tool
that helped him breathe
long enough
to make something kind
for somebody else.

Is that allowed?

Or do I need
to be miserable again
for the argument
to stay tidy?

[Verse 3 – warmer spoken rap | piano enters]

Because I remember
when everything felt finished.

Not dramatic finished.

Just dishes in the sink finished.
Unread message finished.
Standing in a shop aisle
holding two kinds of rice
and feeling unqualified
to continue as a person
finished.

And now
I make things.

Small things.

No empire.
No prophecy.
No glowing portal.

Just a friend says,
“Could it remind me?”
and I say,
“Maybe.”

Just a melody catches
on a bad afternoon
and I follow it
until the room stops biting.

Just a question
that used to shame me
turns into a staircase.

And I know
stairs can lead anywhere.

Basements included.

So I bring a torch.
I bring a map.
I bring three backup plans
and a suspicious little face.

But I’m still going.

Not because everything is safe.

Because not everything
that helps you
has to be holy.

Not everything dangerous
is useless.

Not every new door
is a trap
just because the old rooms
were honest about hurting.

[Final Hook – warm, gently defiant]

Maybe it’s not all fire
Maybe it’s not all flood
Maybe I’m allowed to feel lighter
without betraying anyone

Maybe I can keep my eyes open
and still let the morning come
Maybe it’s not all ruin
Maybe some things help someone

Maybe it’s not all endings
Maybe I’m not wrong
For building little bridges
out of questions and songs

Maybe I can stay careful
and still not come undone
Maybe it’s not all ruin
Maybe some things help someone

[Outro – spoken | instruments fading]

So yes.

I feel good.

I’m checking the exits.
I’m reading the small print.
I’m keeping my doubts
well-fed and hydrated.

But for once,
when the world says
“Be afraid,”

I can say,

“I am.

And also—

look what I made.”
