[TITLE: Last Train Missed]
[GENRE: Oldschool Dubstep / Dub-Techno / Night Transit Ambient]
[MOOD: Cold, municipal, watchful, threshold-crossing]
[TEMPO: 140 BPM]
[KEY: F minor]
[VOCALS: Sparse close phrases with light Jamaican Patwa code-switching, mostly half-spoken and processed like platform announcements, short repeated lines tucked into delay, no melodic hook, human breath kept low in the mix]
[PRODUCTION: Opening threshold after the official timetable shuts; transport as class structure. Sparse halfstep, long gaps, severe sine sub under concrete, rimshot dub delays, narrow station pads, gate and shutter hits, ticket errors, dead-phone warning, vending hum, fluorescent buzz. Final train chime stretches into bass. Transition: platform PA dissolves into depot engine tone.]
