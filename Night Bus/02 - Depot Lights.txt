[TITLE: Depot Lights]
[GENRE: Oldschool Dubstep / Dub-Techno / Municipal Bass]
[MOOD: Patient, mechanical, worn, quietly human]
[TEMPO: 140 BPM]
[KEY: G minor]
[VOCALS: Low documentary fragments with light Jamaican Patwa phrasing, driver-radio phrases, filtered shift-board calls, breath and coughs kept as texture, no sung chorus]
[PRODUCTION: Bus infrastructure wakes as hidden labour. Slow halfstep with dub-techno stabs, engine hum tuned into sub, air-brake percussion, indicator-tick hats, tape-worn delays, low depot ceiling. Use diesel rumble, hydraulic doors, depot PA, radio chatter, clipboard taps, thermos lid, route relays, orange-lamp rain. Build idle motors into route pulse. Transition: door hiss thins into kitchen extractor.]

[LYRICS]
