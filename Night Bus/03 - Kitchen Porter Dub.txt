[TITLE: Kitchen Porter Dub]
[GENRE: Oldschool Dubstep / Dubwise Bass / Back-of-House Percussion]
[MOOD: Greasy, physical, disciplined, unsentimental]
[TEMPO: 140 BPM]
[KEY: D minor]
[VOCALS: Medium Jamaican Patwa code-switching in close overheard work phrases, respectful and non-caricatured, mostly breath, counts, and practical lines, occasional call-and-response between dry lead and spring delay]
[PRODUCTION: Back-of-house labour after visible leisure ends. Halfstep dub, clanking trays and cutlery as percussion, spring reverb splashes, deep rounded sub, sparse melodica stabs, break fills from water and bin hits. Use extractor fan, dish rack, spray hose, wet tile shoes, mop bucket wheels, alley bins, staff meal foil, back door latch. Mix cramped and tiled. Transition: alley rain plus phone ping becomes Rider Down.]
