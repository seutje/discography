[TITLE: Night Bus]

[MOOD: Warmest point of the album, restrained, shared, fragile]
[TEMPO: 140 BPM with swung 2-step feel]
[KEY: B minor]
[VOCALS: Dub collage of chopped fragments from the other Night Bus songs, warm medium Jamaican Patwa code-switching, no lead narrative, phrases thrown into tape delay, spring reverb, low filtered doubles, and stop-announcement cuts]
[PRODUCTION: Very dubbed-out title-track collage: halfstep/future-garage swing, huge engine sub, contactless beep skanks, spring reverb, siren delays, tape-drop mutes, fragments from every surrounding song drifting through the bus interior.
