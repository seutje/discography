[TITLE: Hospital Vending Machine]
[GENRE: Future Garage / Ambient Garage / Fluorescent Dub]
[MOOD: Intimate, tired, careful, still]
[TEMPO: 134 BPM, tempo-ambiguous in the intro and outro]
[KEY: E minor]
[VOCALS: Close, soft, nearly spoken, light Jamaican Patwa code-switching in intimate lines, small phrases with long rests, fragile low harmony only in final hook, sample fragments should feel like signage rather than drama]
[PRODUCTION: Quiet care-work and waiting-room endurance. Soft swung percussion like rubber soles, muted sub, fragile minor-ninth chords, long decays, vending hum tuned to pad root, coin drops as sparse hits, airy room tone and lift-door sighs. Use cup fall, fluorescent hum, PA, sanitizer pump, hospital air, chair creak. Spacious and respectful. Transition: vending beep mutates into Warehouse Scanner barcode rhythm.]

[LYRICS]

and stay awake
