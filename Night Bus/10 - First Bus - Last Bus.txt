[TITLE: First Bus - Last Bus]
[GENRE: Oldschool Dubstep / Future Garage / Dawn Transit Bass]
[MOOD: Transitional, reflective, cyclical, pale]
[TEMPO: 140 BPM]
[KEY: D# minor]
[VOCALS: Short paired phrases with light-to-medium Jamaican Patwa code-switching, call-and-response between night workers leaving and morning workers arriving, gentle chopped backing voice, no triumphant lift]
[PRODUCTION: Dawn relay where night shift and morning shift cross. Begin with warehouse loop reduced to pulse, then softer swung garage drums, deep calmer sub, pale pads, birds mixed with traffic, early brake squeals, title-track card beep reprise. Use shutters, bakery trays, newspaper bundles, trolley, boots, driver changeover. Tension loosens but does not resolve. Transition: engine and chords fade into keys and kettle.]
