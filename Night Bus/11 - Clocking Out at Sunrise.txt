[TITLE: Clocking Out at Sunrise]
[GENRE: Ambient Dubstep / Future Garage Closer / Dawn Room Tone]
[MOOD: Gentle, exhausted, dignified, unresolved]
[TEMPO: 136 BPM with beatless passages]
[KEY: F minor]
[VOCALS: Close tired lead with gentle Jamaican Patwa code-switching, very small melodic lift in final hook, low harmonies as breath rather than choir, final fragments nearly spoken]
[PRODUCTION: Closing track: night ends for some while the route continues. Start with softened Last Train Missed echo, gentle sub breaths, sparse swung percussion dissolving, warm unresolved chords, one faint title-track card beep, then domestic room sounds replacing transit ambience. Use distant buses, keys, kettle click, shoes off, radiator tick, laundry, pale traffic, birds. End in room tone, no next-song setup.]
