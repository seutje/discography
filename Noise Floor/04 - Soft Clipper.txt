[TITLE: Soft Clipper]

[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Dub Lounge Funk / Ambient Pop]

[MOOD: Self-conscious, performative ease -> blurred comfort -> tender unease]

[TEMPO: 92 BPM]

[KEY: D minor with warm F major choruses]

[VOCALS: Close-mic androgynous vocal with relaxed slur on hooks; verses precise and dry; stacked doubles loosen slightly with each return; final refrain exposes one unprocessed lead]

[PRODUCTION: Lazy funk bass, brushed rim clicks, spring reverb, tape delay throws; soft saturation rounds transients; chorus widens like a small room getting friendlier; bridge removes saturation to reveal brittle dry edges]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - glassy keys | spring reverb drip]

The room has teeth
until the meter glows

I touch the knob
and all the corners fold

[Verse 1 - relaxed pocket | muted guitar chop]

I keep a stack of borrowed gestures
near the input by the door
A little smile, a slower answer
less precision than before

When the needle warms to amber
I can pass for afternoon
All my angles soften inward
all my clocks agree with you

Nobody asks about the wiring
when the treble has been shaved
I become an easier station
with the difficult parts delayed

[Hook - soft saturation | wider doubles]

Soft clipper, round me over
take the spike out of my name
Make me warm enough for strangers
make me simple in the frame

I can laugh a little later
I can miss a little less
Soft clipper, keep me social
with a fault light in my chest

[Verse 2 - dub bass leans behind beat]

There is mercy in the smoothing
and a cost beneath the tone
Every joke gets through the speaker
but it doesn't sound like home

I can sit inside the chatter
I can sway and not compute
Still the true part waits in standby
with its hands around the truth

By the second warm revision
I am everyone's soft friend
By the third, the tape is shedding
little names I need again

[Break - saturation drops | dry vocal]

Turn it off
and hear the edge

not ugly
just exact

Turn it on
and watch them rest

I hate
how well
it acts

[Final Hook - warmer but thinner]

Soft clipper, round me over
lend me warmth and lend me rain
Let the evening call me easy
while I misplace my own gain

I can laugh a little later
I can drift and not confess
Soft clipper, keep me welcome
with a fault light in my chest

[Outro - tape wobble | single dry line]

When the room goes quiet
I come back sharp

and count
what I spent
