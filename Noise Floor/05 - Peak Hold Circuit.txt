[TITLE: Peak Hold Circuit]

[GENRE: Dark Indie Electronica / Dub Bass / Nervous Funk]

[MOOD: Bright false lift -> measured alarm -> deliberate release]

[TEMPO: 108 BPM; relay-click three-cell recurs as catch-hold-release, bridge drops to half-time]

[KEY: B minor verses against F#/Gb major lift; final section pivots back to B minor]

[VOCALS: Tight close lead with clipped consonants; every hook bends the same catch-hold-release cell; anxious doubles chase then fall behind; bridge uses left-right callouts and a dry unison "release"; outro is nearly spoken]

[PRODUCTION: Fast hi-hat ticks, brittle funk guitar scratches, sub bass pulse, relay clicks and peak meters as counter-rhythm; stereo narrows under pressure; delay and reverb shift by section; layered build, dropout, tape wobble, filtered reprise, bridge break, final texture thins to low hum]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - relay clicks | meter wake]

Red lamp
green lamp
needle snaps awake

hold
wait

Someone fed the console
too much daybreak

[Verse 1 - quick pulse | tight stereo]

Every surface starts reporting
every shadow files a claim
Tiny errors turn fluorescent
with my number and my name

I can sort the room in pieces
I can hear the sockets blink
But the more the circuit flatters
the less room I have to think

There is no clean invitation
only doors that talk too fast
only mirrors checking mirrors
only proof that doesn't last

[Pre-hook - relay cell in bass]

Catch the spike
hold the light

Call it signal
call it right

[Hook 1 - harsh upper mids | compressed bass]

Peak hold circuit, don't let go
catch the spike and make it grow
Every breath becomes a test
every kindness sounds assessed

Bright little engine, useless crown
run me up and grind me down
If this is signal, cut the feed
I don't want what it does to me

[Verse 2 - delay creeps | cell turns inward]

Now my jaw becomes a metronome
now my hands forget their skin
Every friendly constellation
turns to cameras listening

The groove keeps promising precision
but it only sells alarm
I can feel the hidden solder
overheating in my arms

Catch the smile
hold the breath
name the danger
call it depth

[Hook 2 - wider, then narrowed | transformed return]

Peak hold circuit, change the law
show the peak and show the flaw
Every answer overblessed
keeps the question underdressed

Bright little engine, count me down
strip the shine out of the crown
If this is signal, break the lead
I don't want what it makes of me

[Bridge - half-time dropout | whispered panning]

Left channel: check the lock

Left channel: check the sentence
Right channel: leave the place

Catch
hold
catch
hold

[Rupture - full stop | dry unison]

Release.

[Final Section - filtered reprise | low end exposed]

I pull the plug on the altar
let the false sun drain away
Keep your silver little thunder
keep the teeth behind the day

I would rather be uncertain
I would rather move off-grid
than be polished into panic
by a light that calls me fixed

Catch the spike
name the cost
open the hand
let it be lost

[Outro - low hum | no delay]
