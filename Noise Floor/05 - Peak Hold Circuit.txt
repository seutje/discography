[TITLE: Peak Hold Circuit]

[GENRE: Dark Indie Electronica / Dub Bass / Nervous Funk]

[MOOD: Bright false lift -> mounting paranoia -> hard rejection]

[TEMPO: 108 BPM]

[KEY: B minor]

[VOCALS: Tight close vocal, clipped consonants, anxious doubles chasing the lead; hooks are strained rather than anthemic; bridge uses whispered left-right callouts; final line almost spoken and dry]

[PRODUCTION: Fast hi-hat ticks, brittle funk guitar scratches, sub bass pulsing under high hiss; peak meters and relay clicks as motifs; stereo narrows under pressure; delay feedback becomes intrusive; final section cuts the high fizz and leaves exhausted low hum]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - relay clicks | thin hiss]

Red lamp
green lamp
needle snaps awake

Someone fed the console
too much daybreak

[Verse 1 - quick pulse | tight stereo]

Every surface starts reporting
every shadow files a claim
Tiny errors turn fluorescent
with my number and my name

I can sort the room in pieces
I can hear the sockets blink
But the more the circuit flatters
the less space I have to think

There is no clean invitation
only doors that talk too fast
only mirrors checking mirrors
only proof that doesn't last

[Hook - harsh upper mids | bass compressed]

Peak hold circuit, don't let go
catch the spike and make it grow
Every breath becomes a test
every kindness sounds assessed

Bright little engine, useless crown
run me up and grind me down
If this is signal, cut the feed
I don't want what it does to me

[Verse 2 - delay feedback creeps]

Now my jaw becomes a metronome
now my hands forget their skin
Every friendly constellation
turns to cameras listening

The groove keeps promising precision
but it only sells alarm
I can feel the hidden solder
overheating in my arms

No communion in the voltage
no real door behind the glare
Just a room with sharper corners
and a throat full of repair

[Bridge - beat drops | whispered panning]

Left channel: check the lock
Right channel: check the face
Left channel: check the sentence
Right channel: leave the place

Again
again
again

[Rupture - full stop | dry breath]

No.

[Final Section - slower | hiss removed]

I pull the plug on the altar
let the false sun drain away
Keep your silver little thunder
keep the teeth behind the day

I would rather be uncertain
I would rather move off-grid
than be polished into panic
by a light that calls me fixed

[Outro - low hum | no delay]

Red lamp dark
green lamp gone

finally wrong
in a quieter room
