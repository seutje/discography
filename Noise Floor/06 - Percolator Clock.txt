[TITLE: Percolator Clock]
[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Dub Funk / Clockwork Ambient]
[MOOD: Drowsy devotion -> wired affection -> conflicted dependence]
[TEMPO: 100 BPM]
[KEY: G minor with bright B-flat lift]
[VOCALS: Intimate lead with crisp morning diction; playful doubles on short phrases; chorus opens but keeps a nervous edge; final refrain half-whispered as if recorded too early]
[PRODUCTION: Percolating arps, muted funk bass, clock ticks, tape flutter, dub delay on snare rim; arrangement alternates sleepy low-pass verses with bright choruses; bridge desynchronizes tick and bass before settling into a softer pulse]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - kettle hum | clock tick | filtered keys]

Before the screen has found its color
before the room remembers me
you click inside the little tower
and pull a coastline from the sea

[Verse 1 - low-pass groove]

I owe you several first sentences
and the courage of my shoes
You lend the lamps their filaments
you give my hands a use

With you, the hallway has a tempo
with you, the inbox has a floor
I can thread the morning needle
and not vanish at the door

But you keep a second engine
underneath the friendly tone
It starts counting every heartbeat
like it wants to own the phone

[Hook - bright arps | elastic bass]

Percolator clock, I love you
Percolator clock, I don't
You make a bridge across the static
then you charge me for the rope

Tick in amber, tick in chrome
wake the switchboard, shake the bones
I bless the spark that lets me start
and curse the spark that won't go home

[Verse 2 - funk guitar mutes | dub throws]

By the second cup of weather
I can almost pass for clear
By the third, the room is closer
than it ought to be from here

Every task becomes a staircase
every staircase grows a mouth
You say, upward, little circuit
while my center points south

Still I keep you on the counter
like a saint with stained glass eyes
Little bitter morning motor
little lever for the sky

[Bridge - ticks drift out of phase]

If I leave you
the day stays woolen

If I take you
the wires complain

So I measure
not devotion

just the smallest
usable flame

[Final Hook - softer | warm pad underneath]

Percolator clock, I love you
Percolator clock, I don't
You raise a town inside the static
then you rattle every road

Tick in amber, tick in chrome
wake the switchboard, spare the bones
I bless the spark that lets me start
and dim the spark before it owns

[Outro - clock tick dissolves]

Half a cup of weather
one small borrowed dawn

enough to enter
not enough to be gone
