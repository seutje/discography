[TITLE: Bitter Decoder]
[GENRE: Psychedelic Indie Electronica / Dub Funk / Leftfield Ambient]
[MOOD: Curious, open -> connected and luminous -> earthy aftertaste]
[TEMPO: 98 BPM]
[KEY: E minor shifting toward G major]
[VOCALS: Soft androgynous lead, wonder without melodrama; layered chorus harmonies bloom like phase-aligned tape; bridge uses close spoken fragments; final hook keeps a small roughness on the tongue]
[PRODUCTION: Warm analog pads, bubbling filter movement, dub bass with syncopated funk guitar ghosts; granular room tones; stereo image blooms then narrows into tactile earthiness; a faint bitter click motif returns at each transition]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - bubbling filter | faint click]

There is a drawer in the waveform
I had never thought to pull
It opens with a wooden whisper
and the black room fills with wool

[Verse 1 - soft pulse | warm bass]

I read the people as weather
too fast, too bright, too far
Their faces used to scatter
like coins under a car

Then the decoder warms uneven
and the hidden colors lean
Every gesture has a root now
every silence turns green

Not escape, not decoration
not a door out of my head
More like hearing buried speakers
underneath the riverbed

[Hook - wide harmonies | phase bloom]

Bitter decoder, open slow
show me where the soft lines go
Let the human picture gather
from the snow between the frames

Bitter decoder, leave the earth
on my tongue for what it's worth
If the room can understand me
I can carry that strange taste

[Verse 2 - added percussion | dub delays]

Now the old machines are breathing
now the lamps have nervous grain
I can feel the conversation
moving under every name

Someone laughs and I can follow
someone waits and I can stay
All the meanings grow mycelial
underneath the tiled cafe

Still the channel has a flavor
dark and mineral and plain
All this wonder leaves a shadow
like wet pennies after rain

[Bridge - beat thins | close spoken]

I wanted a clean translation
silver, smooth, complete

But the old set hums
through varnish
through soil
through teeth

Maybe understanding
arrives with sediment

maybe light
has an underside

[Final Hook - luminous but grounded]

Bitter decoder, open slow
show me where the root lines go
Let the human picture gather
from the snow between the frames

Bitter decoder, keep the earth
on my tongue for what it's worth
If the room can understand me
I can carry that strange taste

[Outro - click motif | soft low end]

The picture clears
with soil in it

I smile
and rinse the glass
