[TITLE: Carrier Handshake]

[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Dub Funk / Analog Ambient]

[MOOD: Guarded, searching -> warm synchrony -> quietly understood]

[TEMPO: 96 BPM]

[KEY: A minor resolving toward C major]

[VOCALS: Soft androgynous lead, intimate and slightly behind the beat; small whispered doubles pan like return signals; hooks widen into gentle stacked harmonies; phrasing begins clipped and becomes more legato]

[PRODUCTION: Rubber bass with muted funk syncopation; tape-delay pings answer key words; narrow mono verses bloom to wide choruses; short radio static motif repeats at section turns; vintage modem chirps tucked low; final hook lets the carrier tone become a warm pad]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - weak carrier | hand percussion | room hum]

I send a lamp through copper weather
three small pulses, then a pause
No one answers in the meter
only dust behind the walls

[Verse 1 - dry groove | narrow stereo]

Every table has a timing
every laugh arrives on rails
I keep missing where the door is
when the sentence lifts its veil

So I build a little protocol
from breath and tilted eyes
check the checksum of a shoulder
read the voltage in replies

But the channel keeps refusing
my uncommon envelope
I can hear the room assembling
while I stand outside the code

[Signal Turn - first chirp motif]

Then you tap back
not in words
just the rhythm
I had heard

[Hook - wider | bass warms]

Carrier handshake, hold the line
find my weather in your wire
Not a cure and not a mask
just a circuit asking back

You don't smooth the strange away
you make room around its shape
In the echo, something lands
like a language with a hand

[Verse 2 - dub delay answers vocal]

Now the static has a contour
now the pauses have a floor
All the signals I was hiding
open slowly, less unsure

There is color in the timing
there is kindness in the lag
You let meaning bloom sideways
from the margins of the track

I don't have to fake the weather
I don't have to trim the frame
You receive the long transmission
and repeat it without shame

[Bridge - beat thins | modem chirps become melody]

This is not escape
from the room

This is finding
one tuned instrument

inside it

If I overload
you don't vanish

you lower the gain
and stay

[Final Hook - luminous | harmonies braid]

Carrier handshake, hold the line
thread my weather through your wire
Every fragment, every flare
has a landing over there

You don't name the strange as noise
you hear pattern in the voice
In the echo, something stands
like a language with a hand

[Outro - carrier tone becomes pad]

Three small pulses
then you knew

not decoded
just let through
