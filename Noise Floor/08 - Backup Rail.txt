[TITLE: Backup Rail]
[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Warm Dub Bass / Analog Funk]
[MOOD: Fading, practical need -> temporary rescue -> uneasy gratitude]
[TEMPO: 90 BPM]
[KEY: C minor lifting toward E-flat major]
[VOCALS: Close, low, tired lead; chorus harmonies enter like emergency lights; phrasing is plain and physical; final section warm but unsentimental]
[PRODUCTION: Brownout synth bed, soft sub bass, low battery beeps, cassette wow, muted funk bass; kick arrives only when voltage returns; dub delays stretch on survival words; final chorus keeps a small warning tone under the warmth]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - brownout pad | tiny warning beep]

The lamps go thin
the console sighs

I know this shade
behind my eyes

[Verse 1 - almost no drums]

There are mornings with no handle
only carpet, wall, and weight
Every thought a locked cassette
every limb a loading gate

Then I find the hidden rail
in the drawer beside the bed
small bright current, plain provision
for the system in the red

No romance in the rescue
no cathedral, no parade
Just the backup voltage humming
where the human battery frayed

[Hook - kick enters | warm low end]

Backup rail, hold the body
when the main line disappears
Bring the color to the panel
bring the floorboards back from smear

I don't worship the small lightning
I just need the room to stay
When the screen begins to tunnel
you return me to the day

[Verse 2 - bass moves | soft guitar ticks]

People call it little comfort
as if comfort were a flaw
as if every fragile engine
ought to run on iron law

But I know the sound of empty
when the meters barely lift
I know how the whole apartment
tilts around a missing gift

So I keep a measured signal
not for pleasure, not for grace
just to keep the old receiver
from dissolving out of place

[Bridge - dub delay | harmonies vanish]

Dependence is an ugly word
until the rail goes cold

Then every theory
loses voltage

and the body
tells the truth

[Final Hook - fuller | warning beep remains]

Backup rail, hold the body
when the main line disappears
Bring the color to the panel
bring the floorboards back from smear

I don't worship the small lightning
I just need the room to stay
When the screen begins to tunnel
you return me to the day

[Outro - low battery beep fades into pad]

Not a halo
not a chain

just the current
with my name
