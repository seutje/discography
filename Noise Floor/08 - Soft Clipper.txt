[TITLE: Soft Clipper]

[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Dub Lounge Funk / Ambient Pop]

[MOOD: Courteous ease -> warm compliance -> exposed self-recognition]

[TEMPO: 92 BPM with a recurring one-beat breath before each hook]

[KEY: D minor verses, F major hooks, final hook lets D minor sit under F major]

[VOCALS: Close-mic androgynous lead; verses dry and exact; hooks use loosened doubles; each return repeats the same four-note "round me over" cell in a new shape; bridge strips to one unprocessed voice]

[PRODUCTION: Lazy dub-funk bass, brushed rim clicks, glassy keys, spring reverb, tape delay throws; soft saturation rounds transients; the missing-beat breath becomes the section gate; hook one widens, hook two thins, bridge goes dry, final hook keeps the dry edge inside the warm room]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - glassy keys | meter glow]

The room has teeth
until the meter glows

I touch the knob
and all the corners fold

One beat gone
then everybody knows

[Verse 1 - relaxed pocket | muted guitar chop]

I keep a drawer of borrowed gestures
by the input near the door
A gentler laugh, a slower answer
less of what I meant before

When the needle warms to amber
I can pass for afternoon
All my angles tuck their shoulders
all my clocks agree with you

Nobody asks about the wiring
when the treble has been shaved
I become an easier station
with the difficult parts delayed

[Pre-hook - one-beat breath | bass answers vocal]

Round the corner
save the spark

Round the sentence
hide the mark

[Hook 1 - soft saturation | wider doubles]

Soft clipper, round me over
take the spike out of my name
Make me warm enough for strangers
make me simple in the frame

I can laugh a little later
I can miss a little less
Soft clipper, keep me social
with a fault light in my chest

[Verse 2 - dub bass leans behind beat | lyric cell returns]

There is mercy in the smoothing
and a bill beneath the tone
Every joke gets through the speaker
but it doesn't sound like home

I can sit inside the chatter
I can sway and not compute
Still the true part waits in standby
with both hands around the root

By the second warm revision
I am everyone's soft friend
By the third, the backing peels off
little names I need again

[Hook 2 - thinner doubles | missing beat cuts harder]

Soft clipper, round me over
leave the spark but change the shape
Let the room applaud the answer
while the question stays awake

I can laugh a little later
I can miss a little less
Soft clipper, keep me welcome
with a fault light in my chest

[Bridge - saturation drops | dry vocal, no doubles]

Turn it off
hear the corner

not ugly
just exact

Turn it on
watch them rest

I hate
how well
it acts

[Final Hook - F major over D minor bass | dry edge remains]

Soft clipper, round me over
but don't take the spike away
Let it press beneath the chorus
like a name I mean to say

I can laugh a little later
I can miss and still confess
Soft clipper, leave me welcome
with the fault light in my chest

[Outro - tape wobble | single dry line]

When the room goes quiet
I come back sharp

One beat gone

I count
what I kept
