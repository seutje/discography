[TITLE: Pilot Lamp]
[GENRE: Dub Ambient Indie Electronica / Slow Funk]
[MOOD: Inevitable, ritualistic, yellowed -> briefly settled -> accepting the pull]
[TEMPO: 84 BPM]
[KEY: F-sharp minor]
[VOCALS: Soft androgynous vocal, low and resigned; doubled whispers trail like room smoke; hooks are steady and fatalistic; outro almost disappears into tape hiss]
[PRODUCTION: Slow dub bass, dusty organ pad, vinyl crackle, relay hum, felted rimshot; high end gently yellows over time; tape delay trails coil around phrases; a tiny pilot-lamp tone persists through every section and remains alone at the end]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - tiny lamp tone | dusty organ]

The pilot lamp was on already
before I crossed the room
A little amber witness
in the belly of the tube

[Verse 1 - slow groove | narrow]

I tell myself the ritual
is only switch and spark
only hands around a relic
only weather after dark

But the chair has learned my outline
and the window knows the cue
Every evening drops the needle
where the old instruction grew

There is comfort in the filament
there is warning in the stain
There is nothing very sudden
in the way I come again

[Hook - bass opens | tape trails]

Pilot lamp, pilot lamp
small inevitable glow
I can turn from every doorway
still the hallway knows I know

Pilot lamp, pilot lamp
old machine beneath the ribs
not a thunder, not a promise
just the pull that always is

[Verse 2 - organ pad wider | rimshot delayed]

The room gets softer at the edges
and the ceiling lowers down
I can almost call it shelter
if I never name the brown

All the clocks become forgiving
all the mirrors lose their teeth
while the coil writes its circle
under every word I breathe

And tomorrow has a service hatch
with my fingerprints inside
I will say I meant to close it
I will leave it open wide

[Bridge - bass drops | lamp tone alone]

No grand collapse
no bright alarm

just varnish
slowly darkening

on the same
two fingers

[Final Hook - steady | fatal warmth]

Pilot lamp, pilot lamp
small inevitable glow
I can leave the room in daylight
still the socket hums below

Pilot lamp, pilot lamp
old machine beneath the ribs
not a thunder, not a promise
just the pull that always is

[Outro - tape hiss | lamp tone remains]

After the song
the lamp stays lit

not asking
just waiting
