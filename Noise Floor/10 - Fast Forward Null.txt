[TITLE: Fast Forward Null]
[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Dub Funk / Broken Tape Ambient]
[MOOD: Impatient acceleration -> sour futility -> slow rejection]
[TEMPO: 112 BPM collapsing to 86 BPM feel]
[KEY: D minor]
[VOCALS: Tight, breathy lead rushing ahead of the beat at first; doubles smear and fall behind; hook becomes blunt and unimpressed; final verse slows into a dry, centered delivery]
[PRODUCTION: Fast tape transport clicks, rubber bass, skittering percussion, sour resonant synth; wow/flutter grows until the groove drags; metallic mouth-noise textures stay low; final third halves the perceived tempo and lets dub space replace motion]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - fast spool | sour filter]

The wheels spin clean
the counter lies

All that motion
no arrival

[Verse 1 - rushing beat | clipped bass]

I thought the transport had an answer
shove the ribbon through the head
skip the dull part, skip the waiting
make a runway out of dread

But the tape begins to wrinkle
and the picture doesn't change
Same old room at double motion
same old lock in sharper grain

There is metal on the vowel
there is chalk behind the grin
Every shortcut tastes like solder
and the song won't let me in

[Hook - blunt | bass overdriven]

Fast forward null
no treasure in the blur
Fast forward null
just a motor chewing fur

Run the reel until it screams
still the door stays where it stood
Nothing gained by going quicker
when the quicker tastes this bad

[Verse 2 - groove destabilizes]

Now the phrases trip their brackets
now the snare arrives too soon
Now the friendly little engine
turns the hallway into noon

I can rush the conversation
I can counterfeit a spark
but the contact comes up hollow
like a jack without an arc

Every promise says efficient
every outcome says erased
Just a burned electric penny
held too long behind the taste

[Breakdown - tempo feel halves | dub space opens]

Stop the reels

Let the capstan
lose its teeth

Let the hurry
fall out backward

let me hear
the room beneath

[Final Verse - slow, dry, centered]

Worthless little acceleration
cheap parade of moving parts
You don't open human channels
you just overclock the dark

I would rather take the long way
with my odd and careful hands
than arrive as powdered static
in a place that never lands

[Outro - slowed tape | warm sub]

The counter rests
the ribbon slackens

bad taste fading
no prize missing
