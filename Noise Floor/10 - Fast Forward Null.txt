[TITLE: Fast Forward Null]

[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Dub Funk / Broken Tape Ambient]

[MOOD: Impatient acceleration -> warped futility -> chosen slowness]

[TEMPO: 112 BPM rushing feel; bridge drops to halftime; final verse settles near 86 BPM]

[KEY: D minor verses, D major flashes in hooks, final section pivots back to D minor]
