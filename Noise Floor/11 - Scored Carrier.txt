[TITLE: Scored Carrier]

[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Dub Funk / Analog Ambient Pop]

[MOOD: rising warmth -> slight anxious shimmer -> explosive euphoric acceptance]

[TEMPO: 104 BPM]

[KEY: B-flat minor lifting into D-flat major]

[VOCALS: Soft androgynous lead, close and breath-led at first; nervous doubles flicker during the climb; chorus opens into stacked, sensual harmonies; final hook wide, confident, and glowing without losing intimacy]

[PRODUCTION: Warm rubber bass, dub delay throws, vintage synth arps, handclaps tucked behind rim clicks; scored-line click motif appears at transitions; stereo field begins narrow, wobbles in the anxious turn, then bursts wide with saturated low end, bright pads, and tape-warmed vocal layers]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - narrow pulse | scored-line click]

A little mark across the center
like a secret in the shell
One fine groove in old white plastic
where the separate circuits dwell

[Come Up - soft arps | bass warming]

First the room begins to soften
not by hiding any edge
but by teaching every corner
how to lean instead of beg

I can feel the carrier rising
through the floorboards, through my palms
Every guarded little relay
starts releasing into calm

There is heat behind the meters
there is silver in the wire
Not a mask and not erasure
just the body catching fire

[Pre-Hook - pulse lifts | vocal doubles appear]

Half to half
the line remembers

how a split thing
can connect

[Hook - wider | warm harmonies]

Scored carrier, open the channel
bring the far sides into phase
Let the parting line draw closer
what the old room kept away

I am here in all my strangeness
I am shaped and I am fine
Every contact feels more human
when the break becomes a line

[Anxious Turn - arps wobble | bass ducks]

Then the climb gets a little glassy
and the lamps lean in too near
For a breath I check the exits
for a fault that isn't here

Is my face arranged correctly?
Are my hands too much to read?
Does the console know my secret?
Does the room expect a fee?

But the fear is just a flutter
in a transformer under load
One small tremor in the ribbon
not the ending of the code

[Build - handclaps enter | delays answer]

So I stay
with the signal

let it crest
and let it pass

all the locked-up
little switches

turning gold
behind the glass

[Explosive Hook - full width | euphoric bass]

Scored carrier, open the channel
throw the whole room into phase
Let the parting line draw closer
every body, every face

I am here in all my strangeness
I am wanted in my skin
Every contact turns to current
every window lets me in

I feel sexy in the circuitry
I feel honest in the heat
No correction, no apology
just the rhythm finding me

[Bridge - dub breakdown | sensual, close vocal]

Not polished
not improved

just tuned enough
to feel the groove

No borrowed shape
no better face

just my own signal
held with grace

[Final Hook - huge, warm, communal]

Scored carrier, open the channel
bring the far sides into phase
Let the parting line draw closer
what the old room kept away

I am here in all my strangeness
I am wanted in my skin
Every contact turns to current
every locked door lets me in

If the mark was made for parting
then the parting learned to sing
Two small halves become a chorus
and the chorus welcomes everything

[Outro - carrier pad | fading claps]

Line through center
room made whole

warm receiver
open soul
