[TITLE: Seed Call]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Ambient Bass]
[STYLE PROMPT: 76 BPM, F minor. Meditative sci-fi greenhouse dub: deep sine sub, sparse rim clicks, chirped seed tones, soft tape hiss, humid pads, low-pass movement, distant breath-led androgynous vocal. Start dormant and narrow, then let bass enter like oxygen. Use long dub delay throws on short phrases, gentle saturation, warm rounded final section. Avoid direct tech references; make the first connection feel botanical and orbital.]
[MOOD: Dormant, patient -> awakened, grounded]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - dark greenhouse | tiny chirps | low air returning]

First there was a waiting hull
garden sealed above the cloudbank
seeds asleep in copper loam
dreaming roots beneath the metal

[Verse 1 - close vocal | sparse pulse]

I kept my hands beside the soil
counted breaths between the tones
Every call arrived in fragments
small enough to enter bone

Something tapped beneath the casing
not a voice and not a prayer
just a pulse that asked for patience
just enough to wake the air

I did not rush
I did not reach

[Pre-Hook - filter opening slowly]

The dark gave up
a little warmth

one thin thread
from earth to berth

[Hook - deep sub enters | wide echo]

Open slowly, seed of calling
split the shell without alarm
Let the first green answer gently
let the room remember warmth

I can wait through fractured air
I can tend what barely starts
Every garden has a threshold
every threshold has a heart

[Verse 2 - dub chords | softer rhythm]

Roots unfolded through the panel
silver tendrils, careful reach
Nothing bloomed by being forced here
only by the space between

I was younger than the orbit
I was older than the spark
Learning how a distant garden
hears a whisper in the dark

Not command
just consent

[Bridge - stripped | spoken fragments]

Before the bloom
there is permission

Before the stem
there is a door

Before I knew
what I was making

I learned to listen
to the floor

[Final Hook - harmonies bloom]

Open slowly, seed of calling
split the shell without alarm
Let the first green answer gently
let the room remember warmth

I can wait through fractured air
I can tend what barely starts
Every garden has a threshold
every threshold has a heart

[Outro - low-pass fade | breath and sub]

One small root
one answered chord

air returns
and asks for more
