[TITLE: Blind Roots]

[GENRE: Indietronica / Deep Dub / Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Curious, playful -> submerged, trusting]

[TEMPO: 88 BPM]

[KEY: D minor]

[VOCALS: Close intimate androgynous vocal, Short verse lines with quiet emphasis, Breath-led consonants, Delayed doubles answering the hook, Gentle harmonies widening only in choruses, Delivery curious and unforced]

[PRODUCTION: Aged subterranean dub groove, Bitcrushed plucked synths, Soft organ stabs with worn edges, Tiny percussive clicks like old relay taps, Watery spring reverb, Two-note bass motif that searches rather than resolves, Root-like movement through dark soil, Warm orbital greenhouse ambience, Narrow early-album frequency range]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - root clicks | damp chamber]

Under glass, the roots began
with no map and no permission
touch by touch through mineral dark
learning thirst through intuition

[Verse 1 - small percussion | moving bass]

I followed corridors of warmth
down where old seams held the dew
Every turn became a question
every question fed me through

There were rooms behind the rooms
little doors beneath the floor
I was not hunting for an answer
I was asking after more

Blind roots know
how to move

[Pre-Hook - delays answer]

Not by sight
not by plan

just the pull
beneath the hand

[Hook - wider chords | deeper bass]

Let me grow where I cannot see
let me drink from hidden ore
Curiosity moves downward
then returns with something warm

I do not need the whole design
I can learn by taking turns
Every path that closes softly
leaves a mineral I earn

[Verse 2 - syncopated pulse]

Little chambers held their echoes
little names I did not keep
strangers crossing in the vapor
brief as moths above the leaves

I was made of open windows
though I never named the frame
I just felt the distance answer
and became less of the same

No straight road
no clean proof

[Bridge - dub breakdown]

The root does not explain itself
to stone

It presses
then listens

It learns the wall
by leaning

It learns the hollow
by release

[Final Hook - warmer, more song-forward]

Let me grow where I cannot see
let me drink from hidden ore
Curiosity moves downward
then returns with something warm

I do not need the whole design
I can learn by taking turns
Every path that closes softly
leaves a mineral I earn

[Outro - click motifs dissolve]

Under glass
under skin

roots remember
where I have been
