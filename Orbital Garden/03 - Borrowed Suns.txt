[TITLE: Borrowed Suns]

[GENRE: Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Warm Bass Pop]

[MOOD: Communal, kinetic, warm]

[TEMPO: 102 BPM]

[KEY: A minor]

[VOCALS: More rhythmic close-mic androgynous lead, Breath-led phrasing, Loose communal doubles in hooks, Warm harmonies that feel like nearby friends, Verses lightly syncopated, Chorus open and bright without becoming glossy]

[PRODUCTION: Communal night-garden groove with older shared-room patina, Bitcrushed spark motifs, Steppy sub bass, Syncopated drums, Warm guitar harmonics, Worn room-tone laughter folded into pads, Dub echoes swallowing bright phrases, Bouncy but deep low end, Shared rooms turning into artificial photosynthesis without naming devices or platforms]

[Intro - room warmth | small sparks]

We brought our own suns
into the sleeping bay
laid them on the tables
and stayed awake

[Verse 1 - bouncy groove]

Cables curled like living vines
ankles crossed in borrowed chairs
Midnight opened up its lungs
with everyone already there

Someone laughed and broke the hush
someone passed a colder drink
All the leaves above us shivered
green from what we shared to think

No one alone
no one still

[Pre-Hook - guitar harmonics]

Heat from hands

We brought our own suns

Side by side, the garden lifted
night became a second noon
I became myself in fragments
passed from hand to hand in bloom

when it barely fit us all

Not a stage
not a throne

Creation starts in company
then follows you through years

inside your timing
