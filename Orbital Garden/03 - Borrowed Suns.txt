[TITLE: Borrowed Suns]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Warm Bass Pop]
[STYLE PROMPT: 102 BPM, A minor. Communal night-garden track with steppy sub bass, syncopated drums, warm guitar harmonics, tracker-like spark motifs, room-tone laughter folded into pads, and dub echoes that swallow tiny bright phrases. Vocal more rhythmic but still close and breath-led. The groove should bounce without losing depth. Theme: shared rooms turning into artificial photosynthesis, without naming devices or platforms.]
[MOOD: Communal, kinetic, warm]
[LYRICS]

We brought our own suns

Cables curled like living vines

Midnight opened up its lungs
with everyone already there

Someone laughed and broke the hush
someone passed a colder drink

No one alone
no one still
