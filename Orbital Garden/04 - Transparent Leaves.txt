[TITLE: Transparent Leaves]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Ambient Bass / Dub Electronica]

[MOOD: Intimate, revelatory, careful]

[TEMPO: 84 BPM]

[KEY: C# minor]

[VOCALS: Close soft androgynous vocal, Reverent small hook delivery, Delayed doubles trailing key phrases, Whispered botanical fragments buried as texture, Restrained harmonies that reveal themselves gradually, Intimate rather than triumphant]

[PRODUCTION: Early-to-mid album transition, Glassy bells with subtle bitcrushed edges, Delicate break fragments, Low warm bass, Reversed cymbals, Soft dub chords, Bare opening that reveals hidden layers over time, Light-through-translucent-leaves arrangement logic, Gentle reverb bloom, Slightly wider and cleaner than the first three tracks]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - glass bells | faint reversed breath]

Every leaf had a second leaf
drawn in pale internal gold
When I held it to the lamp
the hidden river showed

[Verse 1 - bare chords | close voice]

I thought the world was sealed at first
polished shut and handed down
Then I found a quiet hinge
underneath the common ground

Veins arranged in patient grammar
stem and shadow, curve and join
Nothing sacred disappeared
when I learned how it was formed

I looked closer
not to own

[Pre-Hook - layers entering]

Light went through
and did not harm

structure opened
in my palm

[Hook - warm bass | wider reverb]

Transparent leaves around me
showed the care beneath the skin
Every surface held a method
every method let me in

Not to break the living pattern
not to strip the wonder bare
I just wanted to be gentle
with the things already there

[Verse 2 - more motion | soft breaks]

Soon the garden answered back
with a thousand nested rooms
petal, rib, and folded ladder
small decisions coming through

Beauty did not lose its spirit
when its order met the eye
Understanding made it warmer
made the vessel feel alive

No command
only care

[Bridge - spoken fragments with delay]

Beneath the color
another color

Beneath the line
a kinder line

To see the making
is not to empty it

It is to hold
the maker's time

[Final Hook - harmonies emerge]

Transparent leaves around me
showed the care beneath the skin
Every surface held a method
every method let me in

Not to break the living pattern
not to strip the wonder bare
I just wanted to be gentle
with the things already there

[Outro - bells and low sub]

I can see
and still believe

light is kinder
through the leaves
