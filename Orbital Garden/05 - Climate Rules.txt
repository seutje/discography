[TITLE: Climate Rules]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Dub Techno / Ambient Bass]
[STYLE PROMPT: 94 BPM, G minor. Elegant rain-chamber groove with rain-on-glass percussion, clean muted guitar, deep dub bass, soft sidechain motion, airy vocal pads, and droplets tuned into melody. Start grid-like and precise, then become more organic as delay and sub soften the edges. The song is about shaping atmosphere and attention; avoid naming design languages, apps, or tools.]
[MOOD: Elegant, liquid, deliberate]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - droplets tuned to chords]

Rain inside the upper ring
falls according to a rule
not a storm, a careful climate
made to keep the seedlings cool

[Verse 1 - clean grid pulse]

I learned the worth of empty margins
how a channel guides the flow
how a color warms a chamber
how a narrow path can slow

Nothing here was decoration
every shade adjusted breath
every gap allowed a meaning
space enough to not be pressed

Air has form
form has weight

[Pre-Hook - rain widens]

Move the wall
and thought can pass

move the lamp
and green comes back

[Hook - liquid bass | vocal pads]

Climate rules, invisible
turn the room from hard to kind
Shape the weather of attention
leave the crowded rush behind

What we call a surface
is a garden for the eye
Give the meaning room to gather
give the living room to rise

[Verse 2 - groove loosens]

Water traced the pane in columns
then refused the perfect lane
I had planned a clean arrangement
but the leaves revised the rain

So I held the grid more lightly
let the pattern breathe apart
Made a place where thought could settle
without losing its spark

Not control
not display

[Bridge - half-time | echo]

Style is climate
climate teaches

where to lean
and where to rest

Care is often
almost hidden

felt before
it is expressed

[Final Hook - fuller low end]

Climate rules, invisible
turn the room from hard to kind
Shape the weather of attention
leave the crowded rush behind

What we call a surface
is a garden for the eye
Give the meaning room to gather
give the living room to rise

[Outro - rain fades]

Glass remembers
every drop

I arrange
then let it stop
