[TITLE: Night Pumps]

[GENRE: Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Focused, pressured, responsible]

[TEMPO: 106 BPM]

[KEY: E minor]

[VOCALS: Close and tired but steady androgynous lead, Controlled breath, Minimal vibrato, Low octave doubles during pressure lines, Hook opens into mature restrained confidence, No melodrama, Delivery feels like staying awake to maintain something alive]

[PRODUCTION: Focused working-garden track at the hinge of the album, Disciplined sub bass, Restrained kick, Precise hats, Alert-like bell motifs, Fluorescent pads growing heavier, Filtered chord stabs, Cavernous snare delays, Older grit still present on percussion, Electrodub pressure beginning to dominate, Midnight maintenance atmosphere]
