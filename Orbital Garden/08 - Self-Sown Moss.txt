[TITLE: Self-Sown Moss]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Ambient Bass / Experimental Dub]

[MOOD: Hypnotic, curious, self-growing]

[TEMPO: 82 BPM]

[KEY: F# minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate curious androgynous vocal, Soft instructional phrasing, Delayed doubles that drift out of sync, Sparse harmonies appearing late, Phrases feel like instructions given to a living wall, Calm wonder rather than certainty]

[PRODUCTION: Hypnotic self-growing electrodub arrangement, Loops of unequal length, Modular droplets, Evolving heavy pads, Random-seeming arps becoming melodic, Delayed percussion fragments, Sub bass appearing late like a shadow then thickening, Begins nearly beatless and slowly gathers a dub pulse, Clean modern weight replacing the early bitcrushed patina]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - evolving pads | scattered droplets]

I made a place
then stepped aside
and moss appeared
where rules could hide

[Verse 1 - nearly beatless]

Not planted in a tidy row
not named before it took
It crossed the wall in patient islands
writing green without a book

I gave it limits, gave it rainfall
gave it corners, gave it shade
Then it answered with a pattern
I could not have made

I chose the vessel
it chose the route

[Pre-Hook - pulse begins]

Some kinds of care
are indirect

you set the vessel
then accept

[Hook - low bass arrives]

Self-sown moss on the station wall
softly multiplying
I designed the chance for wonder
not the wonder arriving

Let it creep beyond my measure
let it teach my careful hand
Making is a space for other
forms of life to understand

[Verse 2 - phasing arps]

Loops returned at different hours
never meeting quite the same
one would lean into another
one would leave before it came

Soon the garden had a pulse
I could follow, not command
like a thought that keeps unfolding
after leaving my hand

Not control
not neglect

[Bridge - spoken fragments, delay]

I wrote the rain
and watched it choose

I wrote the shade
and watched it move

I wrote the edge
then lost the edge

to something
quietly alive

[Final Hook - wider, richer]

Self-sown moss on the station wall
softly multiplying
I designed the chance for wonder
not the wonder arriving

Let it creep beyond my measure
let it teach my careful hand
Making is a space for other
forms of life to understand

[Outro - beat dissolves]

Green beyond
what I began

living work
from open hands
