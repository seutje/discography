[TITLE: Outside Pollination]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Half-Time Bass / Aerial Dub]

[MOOD: Kinetic, ecstatic, risky, focused]

[TEMPO: 142 BPM, felt half-time]

[KEY: C minor]

[VOCALS: Tight breath-led verse phrases, Wide hook delivery, Close androgynous lead with urgent restraint, Brief stacked harmonies on the refrain, Sudden dry vocal moments before delay returns, Focused rather than frantic]

[PRODUCTION: Fast aerial chapter, Heavy half-time sub, Clipped propulsive hats, Sweeping flyby effects, Pitch dives, Airy pads, Sudden dub delay dropouts, Kinetic percussion, Small handmade fliers pollinating exterior bloom arrays in orbit, Avoid direct vehicle or control terminology]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - fast hats | rising air]

Outside the glass
the flowers turn
small wings crossing
where they burn

[Verse 1 - tight rhythmic vocal]

I built a body out of focus
made it light enough to trust
sent it through the outer trellis
where the petals held their dust

No room left for inner chatter
no spare thought to pull apart
Only breath and angle changing
only weather through the heart

Fast and low
wide then near

[Pre-Hook - pitch dive]

Speed can make
a sudden hush

when every nerve
becomes the bloom

[Hook - half-time sub drop]

No thoughts at full throttle
only garden, glass, and air
I become the moving question
I become the line I dare

Out beyond the safer walkway
past the rail and through the spray
I can feel the flowers answer
when I carry gold away

[Verse 2 - hats return | wide pads]

Sometimes impact taught me patience
sometimes pieces taught me craft
I would gather up the lesson
from the floor and build it back

Flight was never pure escape
it was attention under strain
how to lose myself completely
and return with pollen grains

Break, repair
rise, revise

[Bridge - dub delay dropout]

The body learns
what fear cannot explain

The hand goes quiet

The path gets bright

For one clean second
I am only

what the garden
needs in flight

[Final Hook - ecstatic, wide]

No thoughts at full throttle
only garden, glass, and air
I become the moving question
I become the line I dare

Out beyond the safer walkway
past the rail and through the spray
I can feel the flowers answer
when I carry gold away

[Outro - air sweep fades]

Small wings pass
then disappear

the bloom remains
the room draws near
