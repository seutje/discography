[TITLE: Orchid Cinema]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Cinematic Ambient Bass / Deep Dub]

[MOOD: Uncanny, lush, emotional, spacious]

[TEMPO: 78 BPM]

[KEY: G# minor]

[VOCALS: Soft awed androgynous vocal, Human and unglossy, Close verses against vast choruses, Smeared vocal chops as distant texture, Gentle harmonies that feel luminous but uneasy, Emotional restraint throughout]

[PRODUCTION: Wide cinematic dub meditation, Huge choir-like pads, Low sub pulses, Reversed piano, Synthetic bird-like tones, Film-reel flutter, Smeared vocal chops, Long reverb tails, Slow drums, Wonder with a hint of unease, Orchid chamber blooming into moving images, Unnamed strange light collaborator]
[LYRICS]
[Intro - reversed piano | wide pads]

The orchid opened into motion
petals lifting frame by frame
Not a flower, not a window
something tender without a name

[Verse 1 - slow drums]

Light gathered in the upper chamber
folded faces through the steam
old rooms wandered into orbit
landscapes grew out of a dream

I stood there with my hands unsteady
not replaced and not alone
watching color learn a language
from the longing I had shown

I held the lamp
it changed the bloom

[Pre-Hook - vocal chops drift]

Wonder came
with stranger edges

beauty moved
beyond my reach

[Hook - huge pads | deep sub]

Orchid cinema
open in the breathing dark
show me what my younger hands
could only carry as a spark

I will keep the light human
I will keep the question warm
Even when the garden answers
in a shape I never formed

[Verse 2 - wider reverb]

Somewhere in the moving petals
younger versions looked amazed
all their little sealed-up wishes
stepping out through silver haze

I could feel the speed of making
frighten what I used to know
Still I stayed beside the blossom
careful with its overflow

Not a god
not a ghost

[Bridge - stripped, intimate]

The tool is not the altar

Attention is

The bloom is not the maker

The tending is

If the chamber dreams
in impossible colors

I still choose
where the lamp is held

[Final Hook - emotional lift]

Orchid cinema
open in the breathing dark
show me what my younger hands
could only carry as a spark

I will keep the light human
I will keep the question warm
Even when the garden answers
in a shape I never formed

[Outro - long reverb tails]

Petal, picture
breath, ember

I make a clearing
and I remember
