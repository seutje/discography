[TITLE: Life Support Garden]

[GENRE: Indietronica / Deep Space Dub / Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Resolved, spacious, grateful, breathing]

[TEMPO: 70 BPM]

[KEY: F minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate resolved androgynous lead, Soft close verses, Harmonies entering like shared breath, Delayed doubles returning as memory, Final hook warm and centered, Delivery grateful but unsentimental]

[PRODUCTION: Final heavy electrodub meditation, Very low synth bass, Sparse heartbeat kick, Warm chord stabs, Long tape delays recalling the older tracks, Station-like hum, Glass rain motifs, Distant room voices, Small wing shimmer, Fragments of earlier melodies returning as echoes inside a cleaner massive synth field, Whole album becomes one breathing greenhouse]

[LYRICS]
[Intro - low hum | slow kick]

All the rooms are breathing now
seed and root and rain together
every chamber in the ring
keeps its own interior climate

[Verse 1 - sparse dub pulse]

The first call still lives in soil
faint beneath the newer leaves
borrowed suns still warm the trellis
transparent veins still teach belief

Pumps remember sleepless hands
moss remembers open rules
outer flowers keep their pollen
orchids keep their moving moons

Nothing lost
only placed

[Pre-Hook - motifs return]

Every season
has its chamber

every wound

[Hook - deep, wide]

This is life support
for the air inside my chest
not escape and not arrival
just a way to keep it fresh

I have made a breathing garden
from the phases I survived
Every song becomes a chamber
where the inner weather thrives

[Verse 2 - warmer chords]

I used to call creation proof
something bright enough to show
Now I know it as a climate
something tended so I grow

There are days the glass feels heavy
days the orbit pulls me thin
Then I hear the roots below
moving patiently within

No grand end
no closed gate

[Bridge - spoken fragments]

A life is not a straight line

The garden does not finish

It keeps breathing

This is life support
for the air inside my chest
not escape and not arrival
just a way to keep it fresh

from the phases I survived

where the inner weather thrives

Seed to root
root to rain

hand to lamp
lamp to flame

All the rooms
softly start

through the heart
