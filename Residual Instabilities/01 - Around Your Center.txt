[TITLE: Around Your Center]
[GENRE: Indietronica / Ambient / Deep Space Dub]

[MOOD: Gravitational, dependent → destabilized, aware → quietly surrendered]

[TEMPO: 74 BPM]

[KEY: F# minor]

[VOCALS: Close, intimate and slightly breathless, Soft androgynous lead with restrained vibrato, Layered harmonies that orbit unevenly around the lead, Occasional distant, filtered doubles drifting out of sync (orbital lag), Whispered astrophysical terms buried low in the mix, Hooks expand but remain fragile and unresolved]

[PRODUCTION: Sub-heavy low end representing gravitational pull, Slow LFO-modulated pads that “orbit” in stereo field, Sparse kick pulses like distant signals, Irregular percussive ticks mimicking orbital perturbations, Analog synths with slight pitch drift (Doppler-like), Long reverb tails that collapse inward rather than expand, Delays that tighten toward the center over time, Final section increasingly mono and centered to suggest collapse into barycenter]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – distant low hum | slow oscillation | faint high-frequency pulses]

Two bodies in a quiet bind
one defines the frame of time

I trace a curve I never chose
held in paths your mass composed

[Verse 1 – minimal pulse | soft sub movement | wide empty space]

I was given less to hold
less inertia in my role
Every step I try to take
turns around your heavier wake

You don’t move the way I do
you just are—and I accrue
every shift and every bend
as if you were origin

I revolve
you remain

[Pre-Hook – delays tighten | subtle pitch dip]

Center drifts
but not for me

I translate
you just be

[Hook – deeper sub | widened harmonic field | slow modulation]

I circle where the balance leans
closer in, not just between
Every arc I try to claim
folds back into your domain

Not by force I can resist
just by scale, by what persists
I am pulled into the proof
of how much I depend on you

[Verse 2 – more movement | orbital synth panning | gaps widen]

There’s a center we both trace
set too deep within your space
I can map it, I can see
it’s not placed for symmetry

You define the common ground
I just trace what falls around
Every cycle that I keep
writes your name in where I sweep

You don’t chase
you don’t strain

[Pre-Hook – harmonic tension rises | slight detune]

Mass decides
what aligns

I adjust
every time

[Hook – fuller low end | harmonies offset like phase shift]

I circle where the balance leans
never equal, never clean
Every path I try to hold
tilts toward your manifold

Not devotion, not a choice
just the shape of lesser force
I’m the echo of a frame
that was never set the same

[Bridge – stripped back | spoken fragments | drifting tones]

You don’t need to move

to keep me moving

You don’t need to call

I’m already falling

What we share…
is not divided

It’s weighted

What if center
isn’t mutual

just…

inherited

[Drop – sub swell | slow inward collapse | narrowing stereo field]

(ahh—)

[Verse 3 – slower | more grounded | less spatial width]

I begin to feel the strain
of revolving in your name
Not a prison, not a flaw
just a fundamental law

If I slipped out from your spin
you’d forget I’d ever been
But if you were pulled away
I would lose my frame to stay

I’m defined
by your mass

[Final Hook – warmer but resigned | centered mix | stabilized delays]

I remain where balance lies
even skewed, it still survives
Not as equal, not as free
but consistent in its need

I don’t fight the way it bends
I just follow where it ends
In the orbit I can see
you are more than I can ever be

[Outro – low-pass fade | collapsing reverb | single sustained tone]

No escape
no demand

just a curve
I understand

Not abandoned
not undone

just the lesser
of the suns
