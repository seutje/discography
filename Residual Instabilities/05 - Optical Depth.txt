[TITLE: Optical Depth]
[GENRE: Dark Indietronica / Dub Electronica / Cosmic Ambient Bass]

[MOOD: Disconnected, observational → swelling intensity → unresolved, suspended]

[TEMPO: 86 BPM]

[KEY: D minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate androgynous lead, near-whispered in verses, fragile with slight tremble, Loose doubles drifting out of sync, Distant reverbed harmonies like deep-space signals, Chorus opens but stays restrained, Subtle pitch drift on sustained notes, Final layers stack without resolving]

[PRODUCTION: Deep sub bass with gravitational weight, Dub delay throws on key words, Sparse offbeat percussion, Metallic highs early, Gradual spectral thinning, Redshift-style filter automation (highs stretching away), Expanding reverbs for scale, Wider pads in chorus without full release, Ending dense yet hollow, like a star that fades before detonation]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – distant hum | faint pulses | airy hiss like cosmic background]

I stand where light arrives too late
measured echoes, second-hand

something passes through my frame
never settling in my hands

[Verse 1 – minimal beat | tight low-end pulse]

They speak in wave-lengths I can trace
spectral lines I recognize
Hydrogen praise, shifting red
as it slips beyond my size

Every compliment decays
like a photon stretched too thin
By the time it reaches me
it forgets where it has been

I calculate the drift, the scale
still I fail to feel it land

[Chorus – wider pads | sub bass blooms | echoes stretch]

I am not the place it stays
I am transit, I am phase
Every brightness bends away
before it knows my name

Like expansion pulling wide
nothing held, just carried by
All the warmth that should remain
passes through, unchanged

[Verse 2 – dub delays | more space | percussive echoes]

They say the universe retains
every trace of what occurs
Background whispers, fossil light
quiet proof of what once stirred

So where is mine, that lingering field
that afterglow of being seen
Did it thin across the span
or collapse somewhere between

I rehearse acceptance lines
but they scatter when I speak

[Verse 3 – slightly denser rhythm | tension building]

Maybe I’m a dying star
core too light to hold the burn
Fusion falters at the edge
nothing left to overturn

Iron silence at my center
no more elements to give
All reactions slow to stillness
yet I’m somehow asked to live

Not explosion—just a dimming
drawn-out, unobserved retreat

[Chorus – fuller but unstable | harmonies drift]

I am not the place it stays
I am transit, I am phase
Every brightness bends away
before it knows my name

Like expansion pulling wide
nothing held, just carried by
All the warmth that should remain
passes through, unchanged

[Chorus – layered, more intense | unresolved swell]

If there’s gravity in me
why does nothing choose to keep
Orbit fails, escape is easy
everything exceeds

I can map the missing mass
trace effects I cannot grasp
Something should be holding fast—
but it never is

[Outro – dense echoes | low-pass closing slowly | no resolution]

Redshift stretching every word
longer… thinner… less defined

Background glow without a source
still detected, never mine

I remain where signals cross
not receiver, not the cause

just a distance
just a frame

still expanding
all the same
