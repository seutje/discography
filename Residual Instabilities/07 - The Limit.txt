[TITLE: The Limit]
[GENRE: Indie Electronica / Ambient Pop / Experimental]

[MOOD: Brooding, ominous, introspective → tense, pressurized → fragile release, quietly transcendent]

[TEMPO: 92 BPM]

[KEY: D minor]

[VOCALS: Androgynous, intimate and breath-forward, slightly fragile tone; subtle distortion on peaks to imply “collapse, ” Verses inward and restrained, hooks more open yet haunted, Occasional whispered doubles and light vocoder layers for inner dialogue; harmonies bloom gradually like distant signals, ]

[PRODUCTION: Deep sub-bass pulses like gravitational pressure; sparse detuned pads drift with slow modulation, Glitchy ticks and unstable arpeggiators suggest strain; sidechain swells mimic pressure cycles, Granular fragments, noise washes, and tape warble add decay, Arrangement builds from minimal tension to dense near-collapse, then resolves into airy, weightless space, ]
