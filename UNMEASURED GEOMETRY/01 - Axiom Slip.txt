[TITLE: Axiom Slip]
[INTRO]
Whispered patterns coil in the hallway,
Another lesson meant for “everyone.”
But the shape they draw around me
Never finds a surface.

[VERSE 1]
I hear them stacking truths like stones,
Symmetry they swear is universal.
But my corners bend the other way,
A geometry the mirror won’t hold.
I am the shadow that refuses to lengthen
When the sun commands obedience.
I am the riddle that answers nothing,
Unfolding under no one’s doctrine.

[PRE-CHORUS]
They chant: “We're all the same.”
But their mantra falls through me—
A net with holes too wide
To catch my breathing.

[CHORUS]
I don’t fit the circle,
I don’t fill the mold.
Their axioms slide off my skin
Like rain on a closed eye.
If truth is a cage of averages,
Then let me stay unmeasured—
Undefined, unaligned,
Unbroken by their clarity.

[VERSE 2]
Blueprint prophets speak in absolutes,
Painting halos on their common ground.
But I walk the negative space—
The place their language won’t name.
My pulse refuses their metronome;
I move in tides they cannot chart.
What they call “everyone”
Echoes hollow in my ribcage.

[BRIDGE]
Maybe I was carved from a crooked silence,
Born where the pattern breaks—
A fractal that forgot to repeat
And learned to breathe instead.
Their currents push, but I drift sideways,
Following coordinates etched in dreamlight.
Where meaning sheds its skin
And the self grows new eyes.

[CHORUS 2]
I don’t fit the circle,
I don’t fill the mold.
Their truths dissolve in my hands
Like sand remembering the ocean.
If belonging demands surrender,
Then let me stay divergent—
Unheld, unruled,
A hymn beyond their harmony.

[OUTRO]
In the hush between their certainties,
I finally hear myself—
A quiet, shifting resonance
That answers to no shape.
