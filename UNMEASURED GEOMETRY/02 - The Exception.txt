[TITLE: The Exception]
[VERSE 1]
You draw your circles in the sand
and tell me everyone fits inside.
But my edges keep dissolving,
drifting past your patient definitions.
I wear the shape you cannot hold,
a moving breach in your quiet geometry.

[PRE-CHORUS]
You speak in maxims,
as if the sky obeys the fences you raise.
But I hear the cracks beneath your certainties—
echoes leaking through the seams.

[CHORUS]
I fall outside the lines you cherish,
in the negative space you never name.
Your universal truths pass through me
like light through unfinished stone.
And still you tell me all things apply.
They don’t.
Not here.

[VERSE 2]
You chart the paths of common souls,
confident in their obedient spirals.
But I fracture the compass—
let it spin out its trembling confession.
I am the anomaly you overlook,
the one the theorem forgot to bind.

[PRE-CHORUS 2]
Your claims dissolve
whenever they reach my skin.
Your “everyone” was never meant
to include the ones who listen inward.

[CHORUS 2]
I fall outside the lines you cherish,
a quiet refusal in human form.
Your inherited wisdom slips from my hands,
never fitting the fingers you swear we share.

[BRIDGE]
If I don’t reflect the world you scripted,
am I the error
or the unwritten proof?
If your truth must break to reach me,
was it truth at all?

[OUTRO]
So speak your absolutes again—
I’ll let them pass like distant weather.
I remain the shifting glyph
you still can’t translate.
