[TITLE: If Then Els]
[Verse 1]
In the glow of the kitchen light, your shadow cracked like code,
Syntax of a slurred command, love commented out in overload.
Arms that never opened, only clenched into the night,
I learned recursion of your rage, looping fear instead of light.

[Pre-Chorus]
And every variable in me still carries your design,
A blueprint written drunkenly across a child’s mind.

[Chorus]
If then Els—
Your name a failing function in my chest.
If then Els—
The cause, the code, the reason I can’t rest.
I trace the logic of the hurt I drag like chains,
Finding every broken piece still maps back to your name.

[Verse 2]
You spoke in crashing arguments, exceptions thrown like rain,
I indexed every silence, every unpredictable refrain.
No hug, no warmth, just brittle truths you never meant to send,
But I compiled them anyway, a child trying to comprehend.

[Pre-Chorus]
Now I’m the program glitching with the ghosts of what was real,
A system built on warnings that I never learned to heal.

[Chorus]
If then Els—
Your code runs deeper than the years between us now.
If then Els—
The trigger in my thoughts I still can’t debug somehow.
Every jagged thought I fight, every storm inside my head,
Is just the echo of the world you wrote for me instead.

[Bridge]
Still I reach for something tender in the logic of your face,
But memory is a labyrinth with no exit, just your trace.
I wander through each hollow room, the echoes intertwine—
A paradox of wanting love that never once was mine.

[Breakdown]
I can’t rewrite the origin,
Can’t unbind the primal cause—
Your absence is the algorithm
That feeds my every flaw.

[Final Chorus]
If then Els—
I am the sum of all the pain you couldn’t hold.
If then Els—
Your cold became the framework in my soul.
Yet through the static, I keep searching for a way to transcend,
To break the chain of code you left—
To close the loop you never meant.
