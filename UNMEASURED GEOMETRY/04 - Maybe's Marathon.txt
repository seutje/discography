[TITLE: Maybe's Marathon]
[verse 1]
I was thrown through weathered hallways,
Paper names pinned to my skin.
Dorm-room echoes called me “broken,”
But I was only tuning in.
Every threshold felt like exile,
Every silence—locked and sealed.
How do you learn to run a lifetime
When you’re born without the wheels?

[pre-chorus]
But the world kept shouting “faster,”
As I dragged my phantom feet;
They mistook the sparks for failure
While I smuggled in the heat.

[chorus]
Was I meant to cross horizons
On a marathon of air?
Legless on the starting line,
Still convinced I’d get somewhere.
If the winds had bent more gently,
If a hand had steadied mine—
Would I be another story
Or the ghost of what aligned?

[verse 2]
I was coded in mosaics,
Shards of wavelengths misperceived.
They kept patching me with diagnoses
That my inner pulse refused to breathe.
Shuffled like a quiet cipher
Through the gates of borrowed time—
But even fractured engines whisper
Dreams that never learned to rhyme.

[pre-chorus]
Still the chorus of the “normal”
Tried to script my every movement;
How do you trust the compass
When the needle was never proven?

[chorus]
Was I meant to cross horizons
On a marathon of air?
Legless on the starting line,
Still convinced I’d get somewhere.
If the tides had leaned toward mercy,
If a spark had met my flame—
Would I stand on newer continents
Or dissolve into the same?

[bridge]
I crawled the track in circles,
A ceremony of unseen bones;
Dragging futures made of
Unanswered dial-up tones.
But somewhere in the static
I still hear the version “me”—
The one who learned to levitate
Where legs were never meant to be.

[final chorus]
I was running through the ages
On a path that never stayed;
Carrying invisible victories
No stopwatch ever weighed.
If the world had offered shelter,
If the sky had cracked a sign—
Would I rise as evolution
Or remain this shadowed spine?

[outro]
Still I move—
Without the legs they swore I’d need.
Still I breathe—
A marathon of unseen speed.
Still I ask—
What shape I’d bear in gentler light.
Still I run—
Across the dark that made me bright.
