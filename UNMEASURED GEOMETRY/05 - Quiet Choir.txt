[TITLE: Quiet Choir]
[Intro]
Whispered shapes inside the hum
Pulling at the threads I’ve spun
Every voice a distant drum
Calling me to come undone

[Verse 1]
I reach for the warmth of the human fire
But the smoke claws into my lungs
Their shadows lean in, wired with need
While my pulse starts coming undone
I feel the gravity of wanting
Press its hands against my face
Yet the noise beneath their breathing
Turns my shelter into waste

[Pre-Chorus]
Pull me closer, push me through
I’m the many, split in two

[Chorus]
I want the light that circles crowds
But not the bodies making sound
Craving union, fearing touch
Needing everything too much
I’m the echo chasing form
I’m the quiet in the storm

[Verse 2]
I trace the shape of conversation
Like a ghost rehearsing truth
But the weight of every heartbeat
Makes me crawl back to my youth
Where silence grew its sharpened teeth
And carved its home in me
Still I beg for one connection
Just enough to let me breathe

[Pre-Chorus]
Pull me nearer, tear me free
Let the choir swallow me

[Chorus]
I want the light that circles crowds
But not the bodies making sound
Craving union, fearing touch
Needing everything too much
I’m the echo chasing form
I’m the quiet in the storm

[Bridge]
Tangled in the wanting
Fractured in the fear
Reaching for the voices
But folding when they’re near
Let me be the longing
Let me be the void
Let me find the center
In a world so over-noised

[Outro]
If I step inside the circle
Will I vanish into need?
Or will solitude release me
When I finally let it bleed?
