[TITLE: Worn Wristbands]
[Verse 1]
Crowds rising like vapor off the sunburnt ground,
We were wildfire—running, stumbling, laughing too loud.
Neon nights stitched our names into the air,
Every rooftop anthem said we’d always be there.

[Pre-Chorus]
But the fever runs thin when the years lean in;
I hear the pulse fade out where the echoes begin.

[Chorus]
Now I’m drifting through the silence of a past life—
Ghost in the crowd, shadow on the strobe light.
All of my friends were thunder, now they’re thunderheads gone—
Leaving me mouthing old lyrics to a half-finished dawn.

[Verse 2]
Plastic cups on the pavement, broken halos we wore,
We carved our nights like scripture on the festival floor.
But the laughter turned spectral, scattered under new skies,
And I’m older than the music that once opened my eyes.

[Pre-Chorus]
Still the chorus calls back from a memory glare;
I reach for the noise, but there’s nobody there.

[Chorus]
Now I’m drifting through the silence of a past life—
Ghost in the crowd, shadow on the strobe light.
All of my friends were thunder, now they’re thunderheads gone—
Leaving me mouthing old lyrics to a half-finished dawn.

[Bridge]
Is this growing or eroding—
This slow collapse of sound?
I keep searching for the spark
But the spark won’t be found.
All the bodies in the breaker line,
All the stars behind their smoke—
Were they real or just the flicker
Of a younger mind’s hope?

[Outro]
Still I walk through the shimmer of forgotten nights,
Hearing phantom cheers rise under phantom lights.
If the world grows quiet when the memories fall,
Maybe that silence was the loudest truth of all.
