[TITLE: Ultraviolet Hunger]
[Intro]
A silver thread pulls tight around the hollow in my chest,
Whispers drip like mercury—
“One more taste, one more shape of light…”

[Verse 1]
I chase the echo of a feeling I don’t recognize,
Trading pieces of myself for shadows dressed as highs.
Glass serpents coil around my thoughts,
Feeding me their borrowed fire
’Til I forget what warmth should be.

[Pre-Chorus]
Reflections fracture, bending into new commandments.
Every craving is a teacher,
Every hunger writes my name.

[Chorus]
I am the open cage, singing to the hand that shuts it.
I am the trembling nerve, begging for another spark.
Somewhere in the storm of wanting,
A quiet voice keeps asking—
“Who is left when the wanting ends?”

[Verse 2]
Attention blooms like neon flowers on my skin;
I harvest praise like oxygen,
Yet suffocate beneath the weight of it.
I scroll through borrowed heavens,
Hoping one will claim me as its god.

[Bridge – Polyrhythmic / Spoken]
Patterns fold, unfold,
Meaning spirals inward.
Dopamine hymns in ultraviolet tones—
A choir of invisible hands
Tuning my bones to their frequency.

[Breakdown]
Let the cycle crack—
Let the hunger speak in riddles—
Let the pulse confess its crime.
I’ve worn this crown of static long enough
To know it never fit.

[Chorus – Reprise]
I am the open cage, singing to the hand that shuts it.
I am the trembling nerve, begging for another spark.
Somewhere in the storm of wanting,
A quiet voice keeps asking—
“Who are you when the wanting ends?”

[Outro]
Maybe the self is a room with no doors—
Or maybe the doorway was always inside.
I walk toward the silence,
Hoping it remembers me.
