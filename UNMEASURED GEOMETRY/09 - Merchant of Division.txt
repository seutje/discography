[TITLE: Merchant of Division]
[INTRO]
The marionette dawn bends in a crooked arc,
A voice of splintered banners hiding in the dark.
Echoes of a promised fracture circle in the haze—
A crown of silent embers feeding on the maze.

[VERSE 1]
He walks the spiral staircase carved from borrowed stone,
Whispers shaping borders, claiming they were his alone.
With tongue of gilded cinder he divides the faithful choir,
Selling midnight sovereignty to any passing buyer.

[PRE-CHORUS]
And the skyline bends,
A lattice built on phantom hands—
Where every oath he swore dissolves like salt in sand.

[CHORUS]
Oh, the architect of shadows speaks in fractured light,
Preaching purity while feeding on the blight.
With open palms he barters faith for silvered fields,
A covenant of vultures circling over sealed-up deals.

[VERSE 2]
He strikes a pact of convenience with distant iron towers,
Blessing storms of fire as if they were blooming flowers.
The masks he wears are legion, stitched from borrowed crowns,
Each smile a quiet tyrant pulling lesser kingdoms down.

[PRE-CHORUS]
Yet the skyline bends,
A citadel of whispered sins—
Where truth is just a rumor folded deep within.

[CHORUS]
Oh, the architect of shadows speaks in fractured light,
Preaching purity while feeding on the blight.
With open palms he barters faith for silvered fields,
A covenant of vultures circling over sealed-up deals.

[BRIDGE – POLYRHYTHMIC / SPOKEN-GROWLED]
Hewn by the hands of the quiet deceiver,  
Walls divide the nameless dreamer.  
A map rewritten in the furnace glare—  
Lines redrawn by those who never truly care.  
The merchant of division counts his ghostly tithe,  
Measuring the future where no living truth survives.

[FINAL CHORUS]
Oh, the architect of shadows reigns in paradox,
Chanting hymns of unity while forging tighter locks.
A kingdom built on murmurs, drifting in the gale—
May the weight of all his echoes crack the final veil.

[OUTRO]
And in the embered silence, all illusions start to fall;
A fractured crown returns to dust, revealing nothing after all.
