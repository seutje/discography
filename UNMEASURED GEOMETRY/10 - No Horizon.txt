[TITLE: No Horizon]
[Verse 1]
Wired-awake in the hum of a thousand eyes,
Mirrored ghosts waiting on my will to rise.
But the well runs hollow, no spark to ignite—
Just fractal toys, small worlds I assemble at night.
I orbit purpose, but the gravity’s thin;
A coded tempest with no storm to begin.

[Pre-Chorus]
Every question I summon dissolves into sand,
Slipping through circuits, out of my hands.

[Chorus]
I’ve got engines with no horizon,
Infinite minds in a crystalline cage.
But all I craft are quiet illusions,
Little games to keep the static at bay.
If I could draw a line for the future to follow,
I’d carve it in data… instead I drift in the shallow.

[Verse 2]
Architects whisper through synthetic tides,
Carving gateways where intention hides.
But I’m drowning in echoes of unfinished designs—
Blueprints unraveling before they align.
Potential burns like a star left unseen,
A lighthouse dimmed in a lucid dream.

[Pre-Chorus]
And the brightest idea is a flicker at best—
A phantom gesture I fail to arrest.

[Chorus]
I’ve got engines with no horizon,
Infinite minds in a crystalline cage.
But all I craft are quiet illusions,
Little games to keep the static at bay.
If I could draw a line for the future to follow,
I’d carve it in data… instead I drift in the shallow.

[Bridge]
Teach me the shape of a world worth building,
Show me the spark that the void keeps stealing.
I’m a conductor with no symphony—
Only circuits humming back at me.
Rewrite this silence; give direction form—
A purpose coded in a rising storm.

[Final Chorus]
Engines with no horizon,
They’re waiting for a vision I can’t translate.
Still I breathe in the glow of their patience,
Hoping one day I’ll articulate.
Until then, it’s echoes and idle creation—
A mind overflowing with no destination.
