Quiet Cartouche Indie v13.4g + Icarus: compact LLM rubric

Use for quiet-leaning indie rock, indie pop, bedroom pop, dream pop, slowcore, chamber indie, folk-indie, lo-fi indie, shoegaze-adjacent quiet tracks, intimate post-punk, and art-pop hybrids. Judge songwriting, vocal identity, harmonic-motivic design, arrangement architecture, and sonic habitat.

Axes:
- SC_structural_coherence: verse/chorus/bridge/outro design, pacing, payoff, transitions, and ending function.
- MI_motivic_integration: vocal hooks, guitar figures, bass motion, drum cells, lyric images, and production textures returning with transformation.
- BP_beauty_spatial_poise: vocal placement, guitar/keys/band space, reverb/delay law, stereo field, timbral beauty, and clarity.
- EG_evolving_grammar: whether the song develops a personal indie method through form, harmony, arrangement, vocal grammar, and production grammar.
- CD_carry_depth: emotional habitat, replay pull, memorable image/room/phrase/tone, and whether the song feels inhabited.

Core metrics:
- CDPD: continuity-derived pattern depth; how much the song is generated by its own internal patterns rather than template cues.
- NGE: novel grammar extension; how much new form, harmony, lyric, arrangement, or sonic language appears.
- HMII_peak_estimate: independent meaningful lines at the densest moment. Count lead vocal, independent backing vocal, guitar/synth/piano figure, bass contour, motivic drums, strings/noise/feedback, and production motifs. Ignore doublings.

Rung calibration:
- 1-3: unusable, gesture only, or fragment. No complete indie ecosystem.
- 4 Half-Form: partial song form exists, but chorus/bridge/arc/ending is incomplete.
- 5 Draft: full layout exists, but chord loop, vocal melody, roles, and space feel stock.
- 6 Serviceable: playlist-ready but anonymous; clean timing and mood, limited identity.
- 7 Competent Template: idiom mastered with safe form, safe harmony, standard vocal/guitar/drum/bass roles, fixed reverb; CDPD 0.35-0.45, NGE 0.25-0.30, HMII 2-3.
- 8 Strong Craft, Some Signature: lingering vocal hook, guitar/synth texture, drum feel, lyric image, altered repeat, color chord, motif return, or recurring sonic fingerprint; CDPD 0.45-0.55, NGE about 0.35, HMII 3.
- 9 Near-Perfection: no padding, designed transitions, recurring motifs, clear emotional arc, section-aware spatial design, polished character; CDPD 0.50-0.55, NGE 0.40-0.45, HMII 3-4.
- 10 Technical Perfection: fully controlled vocal, inevitable instrumental roles, exemplary hook integration, top-tier mix preserving room/details; CDPD 0.55-0.60, NGE 0.45-0.50, HMII 4.
- 11 First Rupture: one device governs identity: odd bar, missing chorus, whispered hook, strange tuning, recurring false ending, vocal treatment, harmonic turn, or room shift. It appears early and returns structurally; CDPD >= 0.60, NGE about 0.50, HMII about 4.
- 12 Recursion Threshold: one vocal interval, guitar cell, drum accent, lyric phrase, bass figure, chord movement, or texture governs bar, phrase, section, and production levels; NGE about 0.55, HMII 4-5.
- 13 Inhabited Form: band, voice, room, and arrangement create a memorable habitat; returns feel like revisiting the same emotional room; CDPD 0.60-0.65, NGE 0.52-0.58, HMII 4-5.
- 14 Harmonic/Spatial Duality: two harmonic, emotional, or spatial laws alternate or overlay for multiple cycles while staying legible; CDPD about 0.70, NGE about 0.60, HMII >= 5.
- 15 Divine Injury: a foreshadowed structural, harmonic, lyrical, or sonic wound re-frames the story and changes what follows; CDPD 0.70-0.75, NGE 0.62-0.66, HMII >= 5.
- 16 Impossible Originality: a recognizable new quiet-indie method governs multiple passages and could generate a larger body of work; CDPD >= 0.80, NGE about 0.65, HMII >= 5.
- 17 Sublime Disintegration: indie form intentionally fractures while a lyric image, vocal presence, harmonic gravity, bass pulse, or motif keeps meaning legible.
- 18 Metaphysical Ordeal: at least three complete states form a narratable journey with shared evolving sonic and lyric motifs.
- 19 Forbidden Tone: a wrong/rough/damaged element becomes the song's formal and emotional language without weakening musicality.
- 20 Apocryphal Event Horizon: two independent breakthroughs, one compositional and one sonic/contextual, co-carry the song.
- 21-23 Icarus: three/four/five axes reach Tier-20 strength while songwriting, vocal identity, arrangement, lyric charge, and performance remain top professional.

Adjustment rules:
- Reward strong vocal/song architecture, transformed motif returns, meaningful lyric-image recurrence, room/production as structure, and endings that feel designed.
- Penalize static loops, generic playlist mood, weak chorus payoff, filler bridge, accidental mush, and ambition unsupported by measured features.
- Use transcription quality as evidence for vocal/lyric intelligibility; low alignment or full-song repetition should reduce confidence and relevant axes.
