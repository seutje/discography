[TITLE: Dear Mr. Weaver]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Tense, confrontational, cynical, politically charged → simmering defiance]

[TEMPO: 90 BPM]

[KEY: D minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate but sharper male vocal, Spoken-word verses with rhythmic, almost rap-like phrasing, Occasional half-sung folk hook, Tone controlled but cutting, like reading a letter through clenched teeth, Slight vocal grit in louder sections, Close-mic delivery with occasional layered whispers repeating key phrases, ]

[PRODUCTION: Dark fingerpicked acoustic guitar with minor chords, Dusty boom-bap hip-hop drums (tight kick, dry snare), Subtle upright or electric bass, Sparse piano stabs and bowed guitar textures for tension, Occasional beat dropouts under spoken lines, Vinyl crackle and room ambience, Arrangement gradually thickens with percussion and low drones, maintaining a raw protest-song atmosphere, ]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken | sparse guitar]

Dear Mr. Weaver,

I hope this letter finds you well—
or at least comfortable enough
to hear what the city has to tell.

[Verse 1 – guitar + light drums]

Dear Mr. Weaver, you speak of a fracture
Say the whole country's held back by its structure
You point at the ceiling, the pipes and the floor
Then quietly sell off the house to the store

You talk about freedom, efficiency, speed
But markets grow fat while the sick start to bleed
The clinics grow thinner, the waiting rooms long
Yet towers grow higher where developers throng

You split up the tenants, divide up the blame
And somehow the landlord walks out winning the game

[Hook – sung]

Dear Mr. Weaver
who holds your thread

Who profits quietly
from the words you’ve said

Dear Mr. Weaver
the pattern is clear

The cloth gets thinner
every single year

[Verse 2 – stronger rhythm]

You preach about order, discipline, law
About moral decay that you swear that you saw
You chase after smoke in a teenager’s hand
While powder arrives by the ton to the land

Your harbor stays busy, your speeches stay neat
The gangs grow louder in every street
The sirens keep singing a midnight refrain
But press rooms insist everything’s under control again

The rules are strict for the weak who survive
But strangely flexible for money that thrives

[Spoken Break – beat drops]

And of course—
the sausages.

Every delay, every bill, every fall
There's always a sausage responsible for it all.

Remarkable creatures.
They seem to control half the parliament hall.

[Hook – fuller]

Dear Mr. Weaver
your loom spins fast

But thread pulled tight
will snap at last

Dear Mr. Weaver
your cloth may tear

When too many hands
find nothing there

[Verse 3 – darker tone]

Strange little shadows stitched into the past
Old flags that certain families cast
You say that history’s buried and gone
Yet echoes still hum in the songs you lean on

Your party denies every whisper and claim
But sometimes the smoke looks a lot like a flame
The slogans feel older than most will admit
Just polished and tailored to better fit

A suit and a tie can soften a creed
But underneath fabric the stitches still bleed

[Bridge – quieter]

Sell the rails.
Sell the care.
Sell the future piece by piece if it’s there.

Balance the books, tighten the rope,
Privatize daylight and invoice the hope.

[Final Hook – stronger melody]

Dear Mr. Weaver
answer me true

Who keeps the country
we’re losing to you

Dear Mr. Weaver
the pattern shows

The richer the towers
the poorer it grows

[Outro – spoken]

Dear Mr. Weaver,

You keep weaving the story
that everything’s fine.

But the people you’re threading
are starting

to read
between
the lines.
