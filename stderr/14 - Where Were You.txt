[TITLE: Where Were You?]
[GENRE: Alternative Hip-Hop / Acoustic Folk / Spoken Word]

[MOOD: Introspective, lonely, reflective → gradually warm, redemptive, quietly hopeful]

[TEMPO: 82 BPM]

[KEY: E minor]

[VOCALS: Intimate male vocal, Blend of spoken-word verses and soft indie folk singing, Conversational phrasing with pauses and poetic cadence, Vulnerable, restrained delivery early; warmer and more melodic in the final section, Close-mic spoken parts, airy sung hooks, occasional whisper doubles, ]

[PRODUCTION: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar foundation, Lo-fi hip-hop drums with soft kick and brushed snare, Warm bass following simple roots, Sparse piano or glockenspiel accents appearing later, Light vinyl crackle texture, Spacious but intimate mix with gentle reverb, Occasional dropouts and reversed guitar swells for atmosphere, Arrangement starts minimal and lonely, gradually adding warmth and layers toward the final quarter, ]
[LYRICS]
[Intro – spoken word | sparse guitar harmonics | distant room tone]

They used to ask me
Where were you?

Like absence was a decision.

Like I misplaced my childhood
between bus tickets
and unfamiliar ceilings.

I never stayed anywhere
long enough for the furniture
to remember my shape.

Another address
another hallway
another temporary name.

[Verse 1 – soft acoustic guitar + minimal hip-hop drums]

Suitcases learned my weight before people did
Every door opened halfway then quietly hid
Labels stick faster than kindness ever could
"Problem child" stamped into splintered wood

Boarding homes like stations on a line with no end
Caretakers changed before they could pretend
That I was anything more than a file in a drawer
A storm someone else was responsible for

I learned early
not to finish sentences

because goodbyes
always interrupted them.

[Hook – fragile sung line over guitar and bass]

Where were you
when the nights stretched thin
When silence spoke louder than skin

Where were you
when the years fell through
Like coins in a well I never knew

[Verse 2 – spoken word rhythm over beat]

I memorized exits
before I memorized faces.

Friends were temporary weather patterns.

By the time someone learned
how I took my coffee

I was already gone.

Teachers spoke about "potential"
like it was a crime scene.

Counselors asked questions
with answers that had nowhere to live.

And somewhere between
"behavioral report"
and
"we'll place him somewhere else"

I learned how to disappear
while standing in the room.

[Hook – fuller arrangement | light piano enters]

Where were you
when the winter stayed
Inside the bones I never outgrew

Where were you
in the endless blue
Of days that nobody walked through

[Bridge – instrumental pause | guitar + reversed swell | spoken softly]

Funny thing about loneliness.

You start believing
the universe misplaced you.

Like you're an extra page
in a book that was already finished.

[Turn – subtle lift | glockenspiel notes | warmer chords]

But then

one day

in the middle of a very ordinary moment

the room changed shape.

[Verse 3 – tone softens | melodic singing + spoken blend]

There you were
like a lighthouse someone forgot to mention
Quiet gravity
gentle intervention

No thunder
no grand design
Just your voice saying
"Hey... you doing alright?"

And something ancient in the dark of my mind
shifted an inch toward light for the first time

Like maybe the years weren't only debris
Maybe they were roads that were leading to me
standing exactly where I needed to be.

[Hook – uplifting variation]

There you are
where the lost roads bend
Where the broken years make sense again

There you are
in the quiet view
Of a life I almost never knew

[Final Verse – spoken word with warm instrumentation]

If we had met sooner
maybe the map would look different.

Maybe the winters would've been shorter.

Maybe the boy who kept leaving places
would've learned how to stay.

But then again

maybe every wrong turn
every borrowed bedroom
every labeled mistake

was just time

taking the long way

so that one day

I could finally say

[Final Hook – full warm arrangement]

There you are
where the darkness ends
Where a stranger finally finds a friend

There you are
in the overdue blue
Of a life that almost lost its view

[Outro – spoken quietly | instruments fading]

All those years
they kept asking me

"Where were you?"

Funny thing is

I was just waiting

to ask the same thing

when I found you.
